Plarium released RAID: Shadow Legends version 11.95.5 today, November 23, 2026. Most of it is about smoothing out grinding.

- **Relic crafting Mercy System**: a pity mechanic for crafting Relics.
- **Arena alongside Background Multi-Battles**: you can now fight in Classic and Tag Team Arena while Multi-Battles run in the background.
- **Boss Coalition Event and Tainted Demon Lord**: mechanics adjusted.
- **Faction Wars Hard Mode**: some challenges rebalanced.
- **Wishlist Boost Event**: a new event.
- **Rhazin Scarhide**: Fusion requirements adjusted.
- **Champion rebalance**, plus champion AI fixes and other fixes.

The Arena change saves the most time. Until now, running Multi-Battles in the background meant you couldn't play Arena at the same time, and many players farm and fight Arena in the same session.
