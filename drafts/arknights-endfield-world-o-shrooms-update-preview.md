Arknights: Endfield is preparing its next free update, Meeting You in the World o'Shrooms, for November 25. GRYPHLINE's announcement includes a new adventure with Kay, a free Operator named Mimino, a permanent factory-building mode and multiplayer card activities.

## Maintenance timing

The publisher lists Americas and Europe maintenance from November 24 at 17:00 to 23:00 UTC−5, equivalent to November 24 at 22:00 through November 25 at 04:00 UTC. The mobile pre-download window closes November 25 at 05:30 UTC+8.

The update is not live at the time of today's editorial research. Players should finish time-limited activities before maintenance and follow the official instructions for their platform.
