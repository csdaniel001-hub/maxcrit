Com2uS released an update for MLB 9 Innings Rivals 26 on November 18, 2026.

New content:

- **Club Battle Pick 'em**: a new prediction feature tied to Club Battles.
- **Position Training**: the level cap is expanded.
- **Special Workshop**: a new crafting option.
- **WBC Tier Skills**: new skills in the WBC tier.

Improvements:

- Daily Missions and Premium Check-in are revamped.
- Special Training gets an EXP filter, and the Skill Index gets a filter display.
- There's now a pause screen in games.

The Position Training expansion is the change that affects existing rosters. Raising the cap means your best players can grow past where they stopped, so players you had parked at the old maximum are worth revisiting.

The release notes are a list of names without details, so the rewards for Pick 'em and what the Special Workshop makes aren't spelled out. The in-game notices will have those specifics.
