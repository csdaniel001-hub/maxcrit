Subway Surfers City's version 2.7.0 landed on the App Store today, a week after Google Play named it the Best Pick Up & Play game of 2026. SYBO's release notes cover the rest of the year in three steps.

- **Fall Holiday season (now)**: Thanksgiving events, plus special bundle deals over Black Friday and Cyber Monday.
- **Christmas (coming)**: a month-long celebration with a new snowy District to explore. You earn Christmas Tokens in Weekly Wonders and spend them in the Token Shop on exclusive Outfits and Avatars.
- **A new Season**: starting December 2.

The Christmas Tokens are the part to plan for. They come from Weekly Wonders, which suggests a weekly cadence across the month, so playing each week will matter more than one long session if you want the exclusive Outfits.

SYBO hasn't said in the notes when the snowy District opens, only that Christmas is coming and that the new Season starts on December 2. The Black Friday and Cyber Monday bundles are the only part of this update with money attached, and they are optional.
