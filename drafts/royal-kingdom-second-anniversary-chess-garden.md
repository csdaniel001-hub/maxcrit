Dream Games is marking Royal Kingdom's second anniversary with a fresh batch of match-three stages. Version 35329, dated November 16 in the official iOS changelog, adds 100 levels and a new Chess Garden district.

## New puzzle content

The Crystal item introduces another obstacle to work around, while the Slime enemy strengthens its armor when the blobs move. The anniversary celebration also advertises a gift for players. The precise gift contents have not been established by the cited listing.

## Anniversary and challenge windows

An earlier US App Store snapshot reports the anniversary promotion and Chess Garden feature through November 30 at 08:00 UTC. Medal Missions, Richard's Journey, Kingdom Pass and Spooky Collection were listed through December 1 at 08:00 UTC. These times are secondary-reported store observations; verify the in-game countdown. The Chess Garden promotional window does not necessarily mean its levels will disappear afterward.

The November 2 update introduced Medal Missions, with an avatar as a reward, and Weekly Goals, which uses quills to unlock rewards. The earlier Weekly Goals rotation ended November 23; any replacement rotation should be checked in-game rather than inferred from the previous schedule.
