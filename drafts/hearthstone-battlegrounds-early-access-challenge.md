Hearthstone players can watch the next Battlegrounds season in action before Patch 37.2 arrives. Blizzard's two-day Early Access Challenge brings 16 players together, hosted by Shadybunny.

## Stream dates and rewards

Broadcasts begin November 24 and 25 at 09:00 PST (17:00 UTC). Eligible viewers can earn two Battlegrounds Tokens per hour watched, up to two qualifying hours each day. Across both days, that is a maximum of eight Tokens.

The event runs on Hearthstone's Twitch channel and participating creators' channels. Verify account linking and Drops eligibility before watching.

## What comes next

Patch 37.2 is scheduled for December 1. The limited 2v2 Tavern Brawl runs December 9–23, with preset decks during the first week and custom decks during the second. These are upcoming features, not content that goes live today.
