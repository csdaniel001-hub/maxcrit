Monopoly GO is returning to the Marvel universe for Doomsday Go!, a seasonal crossover that runs from November 25, 2026 to January 27, 2027.

## What's new

The collaboration introduces 23 sticker sets, rare Super Stickers with corresponding Super Token cosmetics, animated collectibles, and seasonal boards inspired by Doomstadt and the X-Mansion. Doctor Doom also features in a timed Coin Flip encounter. A new Treasures minigame ability can destroy between 25% and 100% of remaining tiles depending on its charge.

Players who have passed board four can claim a free Avengers Shield during the season window. The publisher has not specified a launch hour in its announcement.

The crossover starts tomorrow, so do not spend resources expecting these features to be available before the new season begins.
