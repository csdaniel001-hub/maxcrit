Zenless Zone Zero's Version 3.3 Phase II Signal Searches run until December 1 at 14:59 server time, the day before Version 3.4 launches. If you're deciding between pulling now and saving, 3.4 brings a new Agent, a free crossover S-Rank and, from January, a second crossover Agent on a limited channel.

## Current Signal Searches

November 11, 12:00 to December 1, 14:59 (server time).

| Channel | Featured S-Rank |
|---|---|
| Answers in the Wind | Severian (Wind, Attack) |
| Outlier of Prodigies | Norma (Fire, Stun) |
| Incarcerating Grip | Incarcerating Grip (Attack W-Engine) |
| Chief Sidekick | Chief Sidekick (Stun W-Engine) |

In the Exclusive and W-Engine channels, A-Rank Rate Up lets you pick 2 A-Rank Agents or W-Engines as the boosted ones; if you don't choose, the channel's defaults apply.

The Final Callback trial stages let you try Severian and Norma until the banners close, with rewards for the first clear. You need Inter-Knot Lv. 8 and the Event feature unlocked in the Main Story Prologue Intermission.

## Version 3.4, from December 2

| When | Agent | How you get her |
|---|---|---|
| From December 2 | Sunbringer | New Void Hunter and the first Enchainer Agent |
| 3.4 login event | Robin | Free limited-time crossover S-Rank, with upgrade materials and a set of custom Drive Discs |
| After January 1 | Kafka | Limited-time Signal Search (Zenless Zone Zero × Honkai: Star Rail crossover) |

Robin comes free just for logging in during the event, so she costs no Polychrome. Kafka is a Signal Search Agent, so she's the one to budget for if you want her.

The 3.4 check-in events also give Polychrome: 480 from "The Final Battle Before Dawn" and 1,840 from the version check-in, which also adds Encrypted Master Tape x10 and Boopon x10.
