This calendar covers limited activities introduced or updated by the November 26 patch. Regional dates follow the server clock: Europe UTC+0, Americas UTC-4 and APAC UTC+8. The same displayed hour can represent three different deadlines.

## Event calendar

| Event | Start | End | Eligibility or reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fantasy Resonance | After Nov 26 update | Dec 17, 03:59 server time | Student I; Jaguaraun tasks, Offering Recipe and Fantasy Token |
| Annual Carnival | Nov 27, 14:00 | Dec 1, 12:00 server time | Student I; daily gifts and a free Gratitude Gift Box |
| Starlight Echoes | Nov 27, 14:00 | Dec 1, 12:00 server time | Student I; Lumin Crystal spending milestones |
| Limited Home Shop stock | Nov 27, 04:00 | Dec 2, 03:59 server time | Resource Exchange items |
| Egg Heist bonus | After Nov 26 update | Dec 9, 23:59 server time | Team Mode; common and prized collectible drop rates +100% from chests and Umbral Aniimo |
| Gracewing reset | After Nov 26 update | Dec 10, 20:00 UTC | Eligible pre-update Gracewing forms; EXP and invested materials refunded with corresponding progress removed |

## Free rewards and deadlines

The Carnival gift is available under Shop > Pack after login: one Departure with the Wind: Collectible Blind Box, 100 Wonder Sugar Crystals and five Vein Essences. Starlight Echoes requires spending Lumin Crystals; it is a separate reward track.

Fantasy Resonance spawns Jaguaraun daily at 04:00. Companion Mode catches by friends count toward relevant tasks. Completing the investigations earns the featured Offering Recipe; a Fantasy Token, earned from the designated task in each round, buys one Offering Recipe for an Aniimo from a past round at the Event Vendor.

The reset ends December 10 at 16:00 Americas time and December 11 at 04:00 APAC time. It is an investment reversal, so decide whether to retain your existing Gracewing before using it.
