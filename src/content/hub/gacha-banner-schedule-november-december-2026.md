This page lists the banners that the publishers have announced for three of the biggest gacha games on mobile. Everything here comes from the official version announcements. Where a publisher hasn't given a date, the table says so, and we don't fill the gap with guesses.

## Genshin Impact Version 7.2

Version 7.2, "Hour of Ruin", went live on November 4. HoYoverse announced the Event Wishes in two phases.

| Phase | Characters |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Mitya (new, 5-star Electro Catalyst), Valeriy (new, 4-star Electro Sword), Furina (rerun) |
| Phase 2 | Zibai and Linnea (reruns), plus a Chronicled Wish with certain Natlan characters |

The announcement doesn't give the date on which Phase 2 replaces Phase 1, so check the Wish screen in the game for the timer.

## Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.4

Version 3.4, "The Finality of the Day", launches on December 2. It starts the crossover with Honkai: Star Rail, and every player who logs in after the update gets Robin for free.

| When | Channel | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| From day one, all of Version 3.4 | Sunbringer, with the W-Engine Dragonfire Mirage | S-Rank Agent |
| From day one, all of Version 3.4 | Robin (crossover) | S-Rank Agent |
| Second half of Version 3.4 | Kafka (crossover) | S-Rank Agent |
| Second half of Version 3.4 (rerun) | Alice, with the W-Engine Practiced Perfection | S-Rank Agent |

HoYoverse hasn't given an exact date for the second half yet.

## Wuthering Waves Version 3.8

Version 3.8, "Lilies in a Dying Dream", adds the Resonator Lily and her weapon, Lullaby. Both lead the Phase I Convenes.

| Phase | Featured Resonator Convenes | Featured Weapon Convenes |
|---|---|---|
| Phase I | Bloom of Yesterday's Dreamsoil, When the Runes Glitter, Wanderer Knows No Far and Near | Lullaby, Solsworn Ciphers, Emerald Sentence |
| Phase II | Voices on Azure Feathers, Blessings From Dewy Winds, Thousand Futures Mirrored in Snow | Azure Oath, Firstlight's Herald, Frostburn |

Version 3.8 also runs two Reverb Convenes, Starpath Reverbs II for Resonators and Tideforge Reverbs II for weapons. In both, you pick a 5-Star Resonator or 5-Star Weapon as your target and can change the pick at any time. Kuro Games hasn't said in the announcement which Resonators and weapons are in those pools.
