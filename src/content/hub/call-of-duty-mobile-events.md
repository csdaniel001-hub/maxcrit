## Event details

### Operation: Verdansk Convergence

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 to 2026-12-09 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Work through the Verdansk story objectives. This one carries the most concrete rewards on the list.

- **Rewards**: Ghost Operator skin and Legendary assault rifle blueprint.

### Mail Call Anniversary

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 to 2026-12-09 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Open the anniversary event and complete its listed objectives.

- **Rewards**: Anniversary rewards.

### 7th Anniversary Login

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 to 2026-12-09 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Log in and claim anniversary rewards.

- **Rewards**: Login rewards.

### DMZ: Recon Season Rewards

- **Event window**: 2026-11-13 to 2026-12-09 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Play DMZ: Recon and complete reward objectives.

- **Rewards**: Season rewards.

### Ready Set Ford

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 to 2026-12-09 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Complete the Ford collaboration event objectives.

- **Rewards**: Collaboration rewards.

### Super Attack of the Undead 3.0

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 to 2026-12-09 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Join the multiplayer playlist.

- **Rewards**: Match progression.

### Firing Range 10v10

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Play 10v10 Firing Range.

- **Rewards**: Match progression.

### Chase

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (UTC calendar dates; hours unpublished).

Select Chase from the multiplayer playlist.

- **Rewards**: Match progression.

## Before the event ends

Play Firing Range 10v10 and Chase first if you want them, because both end on 2026-11-25. Everything else gives you until December 9, and the Verdansk story is where to spend that time if you're after the Ghost Operator skin and the Legendary assault rifle blueprint. Claim rewards as they unlock. Dates can change after publication, and a campaign window doesn't mean every activity is open every day.
