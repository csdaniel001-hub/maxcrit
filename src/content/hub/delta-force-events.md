Delta Force's METAL GEAR SOLID Δ: SNAKE EATER collaboration arrives on November 27, after an update on November 26 and 27 with no downtime. If your client is out of date, you'll need to update to log in. The collab brings a free Legendary weapon appearance, a paid Naked Snake draw and mode events with their own deadlines.

## Event schedule

|---|---|---|
| Operations and Warfare Weekly Arsenal | Nov 24–30 | Weekly login rewards |
| Expert Choice | Nov 24–30 | Weapon missions |
| Tekniq Alloy Store, Score Calculation, Dark Tide Supplies, Damage Results | Nov 24–30 | Mode events and shop |

| Nox - Naked Snake draw | Nov 27–Dec 31 | Delta Tickets or Delta Coins; appearance prize pool |
| Weekend Merit Boost | Nov 28–29 | Double Merit on your first three Warfare Grade Matches |
| DFI Celebration | Nov 30–Dec 11 | Login rewards, including a Delta Force Invitational AKM appearance |

## The free SR-3M and the Naked Snake draw

The Collab Legendary Gift is the free part. Play Operations, Warfare or Detonation, complete activity missions such as surviving matches, and reaching the milestones unlocks Tactical Armament Vouchers and finally the Legendary "METAL GEAR SOLID Δ - SR-3M" appearance. It runs to December 27, ten days after the Cardboard Box events close, so you have time to finish both.

The Nox - Naked Snake draw costs Delta Tickets or Delta Coins. Every attempt gives either an appearance from the prize pool or Naked Snake Coins, and each appearance can only drop once. The pool holds the Naked Snake - Nox appearance, the METAL GEAR SOLID Δ - S12K Shotgun, a Name Card, Avatar, Calling Card, Spray Paint and Voice. Naked Snake Coins buy pool appearances or this season's TurBricks in the Fortune Store. When the event ends on December 31 the pool is removed and any unspent Naked Snake Coins are cleared, so spend them before then.

The DFI Celebration marks the end of the Delta Force Invitational tournament in Sweden: log in for the required days for a supply pack, avatar, calling card and the AKM appearance.
