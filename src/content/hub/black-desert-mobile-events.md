### Black Desert Friday: Help Wanted

- **Event window**: 2026-11-17 after maintenance to 2026-11-30 22:30 (Publisher schedule; check server clock).

Black Desert Friday campaign; consult the event panel for current objectives.

### Black Desert Friday chat event

- **Event window**: November 27, 00:00 to 23:59.

Type "Black Desert Friday" in Server chat once during the day. Every participant receives 10,000 Restoration Scrolls by mail. The Lucky 7 draw runs on the same messages: on each server, the 7th, 17th, 27th poster and every tenth after that get 30,000 Restoration Scrolls, and ten adventurers whose posting number contains at least two 7s (77th, 177th, 727th) are drawn for 200,000. Only your first message counts, so a second post does not improve your number.

- **Rewards**: Restoration Scroll x10,000 for everyone; x30,000 or x200,000 for Lucky 7 winners. Event items cannot be moved or restored.

### Friday Hot Time and Thank-You Reward

- **Event window**: 2026-11-17 after maintenance to 2026-11-27 23:59 (Server time for Hot Time).

Campaign window remains open; the next actual Hot Time session is November 27, 00:00–23:59. It is not active on November 24.

- **Rewards**: During Hot Time: +100% gathering EXP and +100% field/Great Desert/Everfrost/Sherekhan EXP and item drop rate.

### Weekend Surprise Mission and GM Quiz

- **Event window**: 2026-11-17 after maintenance to 2026-11-29 23:59 (Publisher schedule; check server clock).

Campaign covers weekend activities; mission and quiz times depend on each weekend's sessions.

### Aim to be the Best of the Class

- **Event window**: 2026-11-21 12:00 to 2026-12-06 17:55 (Published tournament schedule; confirm server clock).

Class-specific competitions take place on Saturdays and Sundays. November 28: Striker 12:00, Maehwa 13:15, Lahn 14:30, Mystic 15:45, Wizard 17:00. Each listed slot lasts one hour, and the other classes have their own slots on the official schedule.

- **Rewards**: Black Pearls, Restoration Scrolls and a title.

- **Eligibility**: Eligible class for the scheduled bracket.

### November Check-in

- **Event window**: 2026-11-01 00:00 to 2026-11-30 23:59 (Publisher schedule).

Log in daily and collect the check-in rewards.

- **Rewards**: Daily login rewards.

### December Check-in

- **Event window**: 2026-12-01 00:00 to 2026-12-31 23:59 (Publisher schedule).

The December Check-in needs a daily mission before the check-in counts: defeat 500 enemies, then open the Event Menu from the Gift Box icon and tap [Check-in]. The mission alone doesn't register the day. Daily missions reset between 00:00 and 01:00, and extra bonus rewards unlock at 5, 15 and 25 check-in days. No Event Tab is needed to take part.

- **Rewards**: 25 days of check-in rewards plus 5-day, 15-day and 25-day bonuses.

## Planning the last week of November

The Daily To-Do List, Restoration Scroll Rush and Help Wanted all close on November 30 at 22:30, and November Check-in ends at 23:59 that night, so the final days of the month are when to clear the event panel and claim what you've unlocked. November 27 is the busiest date: the chat event and the day-long Hot Time both fall on it. Dates may change after publication, and a campaign window does not guarantee every activity is available every day.
