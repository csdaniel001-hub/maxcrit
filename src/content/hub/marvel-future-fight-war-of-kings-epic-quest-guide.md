Lilandra is the free starting point for MARVEL Future Fight's War of Kings Epic Quest, introduced in the November 18 update. Her progression has its own rules, and the other 2 newcomers have separate acquisition requirements. Knowing how the quest treats all 3 characters will help you decide how far to go before spending Crystals.

## How to get Lilandra, Deathbird and Vulcan

| Hero | Acquisition and progression |
| --- | --- |
| Lilandra | Free Native Tier-2 hero, upgraded through the Epic Quest. Tier-2 passive activates on acquisition; progression rewards provide the Tier-3 advancement without Potential Search or Enhancement. |
| Deathbird | The quest's Deluxe benefits include an immediate 6-star Deathbird unlock. |
| Vulcan | Native Tier-3 unlock through the False Emperor menu after meeting its hero ownership and advancement requirements. |

Follow Lilandra's story before treating her like an ordinary upgrade project. Her special path is tied to the Epic Quest, and the Tier-3 advancement bypasses two progression steps that players might expect to perform themselves. This makes the quest a useful starting objective even when your account has no budget for a pack.

Vulcan is a different commitment. Open False Emperor and read the roster checklist before deciding which existing heroes to upgrade. Each requirement must reach its indicated specification. The presence of a new Tier-4 in the update does not mean you can begin with that advancement; first obtain the character.

## What the Deluxe packs include

| Pack | Purchase method | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deluxe Pack | Crystals | Extra story completion rewards, 6-star Deathbird, access to Royal Pains |
| Special Deluxe Pack | In-app purchase | Same benefits, plus 1,000 Crystals and 2,500 VIP Points |

These packs are mutually exclusive and can only be purchased once per account. Royal Pains remains unlocked permanently after purchase. The benefits activate immediately for the Crystal purchase and when the Special Deluxe Pack is opened from the Inbox, so refunds may be restricted once applied.

If your immediate goal is simply Lilandra, finish the free path first. A purchase decision should depend on whether you need Deathbird, the permanent stage and the additional rewards. If you are pursuing Vulcan, inspect his full requirement list before assuming that buying a pack completes it. Both choices grant the same basic quest benefits; the Special Deluxe Pack adds currency and VIP Points.

## Collection rewards and repeated missions

Imperial Guard Training Academy is the associated Epic Quest Collection. Stage clears can drop collection materials according to their assigned probabilities. The November update also allows you to choose the number of stage Sweeps, which previously offered one run or the daily maximum. Use the new setting to reserve runs and resources for other progression projects when a full sweep is unnecessary.

Lilandra, Deathbird and Vulcan all have Tier-3 Ultimate Skills and Tier-4 Striker Skills available. That makes their unlocks only the beginning of their progression; budget for the specific character you plan to use before committing to the other two.
