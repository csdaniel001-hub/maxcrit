After reaching Town Hall 19, decide first which upgrades keep your attacks working while your builders are busy. The Clash of Titans update adds Squad upgrades, two new defenses and higher Hero levels, and trying to push all of it at once leaves you with several half-built armies and a long queue of expensive upgrades. The full unlock list is in our [Town Hall 19 patch notes](/hub/patch-trackers/clash-of-clans-town-hall-19-patch-notes.html); this guide is about the order.

## First upgrades at Town Hall 19

| Priority | Upgrade | Why |
|---|---|---|
| High | Clan Castle level 15 | +5 troop capacity, now 60 |
| High | Laboratory level 17 | New research levels for your main army |
| High, if needed | Hero Hall level 13 | Opens Town Hall 19 Hero levels |
| High for Squad users | Squad upgrades for the Squads you deploy | Squad Level 2 adds the Squad's ability; upgrades are Town Hall 19 only |
| Conditional | Gold and Elixir Storage 20, Dark Elixir Storage 14 | Move them up when storage blocks a planned upgrade |
| Conditional | Spiky Tower, Firecracker Tower | Cover weak points in your layout and meet merge prerequisites |

This order is our recommendation. If you arrive with lower-level buildings, finish any missing prerequisite before starting a new level.

Spend your first builders on whatever opens progression for your main attack. A Laboratory upgrade only pays off once you research something with it, so pick the research as soon as the level finishes. If you attack with a ground army, don't put your first research into an air troop just because its new level is there.

The Clan Castle upgrade helps every army: 5 more housing spaces in reinforcements lets you improve your usual request without changing your whole army. Storage deserves an early builder only when it's what's stopping your next important upgrade.

## Commit to one army first

Split your usual army into core damage, support troops, key spells and Heroes, and upgrade the pieces that make the attack work first, whatever order the Laboratory lists them in.

Squads change this a little. Squad members are the same level as the troops you've already upgraded, so levelling a troop also improves any Squad that uses it. Squad upgrades themselves are only available at Town Hall 19, and a Squad gains its ability at Squad Level 2. The Golem Witch Squad, for example, holds 1 Golem and 4 Witches: the Golem goes down first and the Witches deploy behind it less than a second later. If your Witches are behind, upgrading them helps both your normal army and that Squad. There are 12 Squads at launch, and our [Squads guide](/hub/guides/clash-of-clans-squads-every-squad.html) lists them all; level the ones you actually use.

The Heroic Spell unlocks at Town Hall 17, and Town Hall 19 adds its fourth level. Ground troops in its area can walk through Walls, which opens new routes, so test it with your current army before ranking it above another spell. Its damage reduction doesn't stack with the Grand Warden's Eternal Tome or Heroic Torch; the strongest effect applies.

## Plan Hero upgrades around war

Hero max levels rise to 115 for the Barbarian King and Archer Queen, 100 for the Minion Prince, 90 for the Grand Warden, 60 for the Royal Champion and 30 for the Dragon Duke. Time Hero upgrades around your clan's war schedule, and keep an alternate army ready for when your usual Hero lineup isn't available.

Upgrade the Heroes your main strategy depends on first. An Archer Queen entry needs different Heroes from a push built around the Grand Warden, so there's no point levelling all six at once.

Hero levels and Hero Equipment are separate decisions. Ore now comes from the Star Bonus at an amount set by your Town Hall level, and Ore gem prices were cut, with Starry Ore down from 35 to 20 gems. Future Epic Equipment costs less to upgrade, while older Epics will get cheaper gradually. Our [Hero Equipment and Ore guide](/hub/guides/clash-of-clans-hero-equipment-ore-priority.html) covers costs per level, loadouts by Hero and saving for the Blizzard Axe.

## Build defenses around your layout

Town Hall 19 adds two defenses, each with up to 2 buildings and 2 levels:

- **Spiky Tower**: a Dark Elixir defense that never attacks. When hit, it returns 6% or 9% of the attacker's max hitpoints.
- **Firecracker Tower**: a merge of a max-level Archer Tower (level 21) and a max-level Scattershot (level 7).

Plan those prerequisites before you reserve a builder for the merge, and let your base layout decide where the first defensive upgrades go. In war, check your defense replays for the routes attackers keep using; a defense that covers a repeated weakness does more than another level on a building attackers rarely reach.

The Town Hall 19 Town Hall no longer houses the Guardian, which moves to a separate Guardian Shrine. Walls are a useful outlet for spare resources, as long as you don't spend what you've set aside for your next big upgrade.
