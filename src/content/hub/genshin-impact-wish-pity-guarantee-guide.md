A Genshin Impact account with 70 pity can be twenty wishes away from a 5-star character and still need up to 110 wishes to guarantee the featured character. The difference is whether its previous 5-star was off-banner. For planning, you need both the wish count and the guarantee state, plus the exact wish system you intend to use.

## Record your wish state before spending

Open the Wish history for the relevant banner type and count wishes since its most recent 5-star. Check whether the last character from Character Event Wish was featured. For a weapon or Chronicled Wish target, check the currently selected path and any Fate Point shown in the interface. Wish history can take time to update, so avoid counting recent pulls twice.

| Wish system | Maximum wishes to next 5-star | Carryover to watch |
|---|---:|---|
| Character Event Wish / Wish-2 | 90 | Shared pity and featured-character guarantee |
| Epitome Invocation | 80 | Weapon pity persists; Epitomized Path Fate Point expires |
| Chronicled Wish | 90 | Pity persists; Chronicled Path Fate Point expires |

The official Version 7.2 Phase II notice also specifies shared pity between Chronicled Wish and Lightrace Wish. This counter remains separate from the ordinary character and weapon counters.

## Calculate the worst-case cost

Let p be your current pity on the intended wish system. For a featured character with an active guarantee, subtract p from 90. Without that guarantee, reserve another 90 wishes in case the first 5-star is off-banner. The official Capturing Radiance Q&A gives a consolidated 55% probability of obtaining the featured character when an otherwise unguaranteed 5-star is drawn. That published rate is not the probability of getting a 5-star on an individual wish, nor a personalized forecast based on your account history. The conservative spending ceiling remains unchanged.

For a correctly selected Epitomized Path, a current Fate Point reduces the maximum to 80 minus your weapon pity. Without a point, reserve another 80. Without a selected Epitomized Path, a featured-weapon guarantee can still award the other featured weapon. On Chronicled Wish, use 90 instead of 80 for the equivalent calculation with an active Chronicled Path.

| Scenario | Current pity | Maximum remaining wishes | Primogem equivalent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Featured character guaranteed | 70 | 20 | 3,200 |
| Featured character not guaranteed | 70 | 110 | 17,600 |
| Chosen weapon, one Fate Point | 30 | 50 | 8,000 |
| Chosen weapon, zero Fate Points | 30 | 130 | 20,800 |
| Chronicled target, one Fate Point | 40 | 50 | 8,000 |
| Chronicled target, zero Fate Points | 40 | 140 | 22,400 |

A 70-pity account without a featured guarantee needs at most 20 + 90 = 110 wishes. Earlier 5-star drops or Capturing Radiance may lower the actual cost. The table is deliberately conservative and makes no claim about the odds of obtaining a character before the ceiling.

## Convert your resources without double counting

One Intertwined Fate corresponds to 160 Primogems. Take the whole number of wishes you can buy from your existing Primogems, then add the Intertwined Fates you already own. Acquaint Fates cannot pay for the limited wish types covered here.

For example, 8,450 Primogems buys 52 wishes and leaves 130 Primogems. Add 18 saved Intertwined Fates and you have 70 wishes. If your chosen target requires a maximum of 110 more, you are 40 wishes short. After applying the remaining 130 Primogems, the currency gap is 6,270 Primogems.

Count future event rewards only after checking eligibility and deadlines. Keep uncertain exploration rewards separate from currency already available. The [current Genshin events page](/hub/events/genshin-impact-events.html) can help identify available sources; it is not a promise that every reward is accessible on your account.

## Choose a stopping rule for expiring paths

A regular character guarantee can be saved for a future Character Event Wish. An Epitomized Path or Chronicled Path Fate Point cannot be banked in the same way. If you miss the selected item and the event ends, your remaining pity can carry within the relevant wish family, but the path-specific guarantee resets. Changing the selected path can also discard the accumulated point.

Before beginning a path, reserve the maximum cost or consciously accept the risk of leaving without your target. If your available wishes do not cover the worst case, waiting preserves your ability to make a fully funded choice later. Do not spend additional money merely to avoid losing a point.

Use the [banner schedule](/hub/banners/genshin-impact-banners.html) to confirm dates and target pools, then write down the amount you are willing to commit. Treat a successful early pull as saved resources for a future character rather than an obligation to spend the remainder.
