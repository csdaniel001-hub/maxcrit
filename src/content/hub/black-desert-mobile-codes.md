Pearl Abyss has published a November 30 deadline for the CRIMSONXBDM coupon. Its rewards include a Dream Horse Selection Chest, Restoration Scrolls and a crystal selection chest, making it a particularly substantial offer for Black Desert Mobile players who have not claimed it yet. Some codes may already be deactivated, used up or region-locked.

## Current Black Desert Mobile codes

| Code | Reward | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CRIMSONXBDM` | 1 Dream Horse Selection Chest, 200,000 Restoration Scrolls, 1 Blessed Light/Fog Crystal Selection Chest | Nov 30, 23:59; timezone not stated |

The publisher lists the closing time as 23:59 without a timezone in the announcement. Redeem early to avoid a timezone mismatch. The two chests are selection items. Read their individual selection prompts before opening them.

## How to redeem on mobile

Android players can open the in-game menu, go to Settings and select Redeem Coupon. Enter the code and confirm the result. For the web method, visit the [official Pearl Abyss coupon page](https://game.world.blackdesertm.com/coupon). Use your actual region and Family Name. You can find the Family Name in your account information in Settings. Then enter CRIMSONXBDM and submit it.

The web route is useful for players who cannot find the in-game coupon option. Do not substitute the name of an individual character for the Family Name when the site requests the latter. Check the reward mail in the correct region after redemption.

## Claim the selection rewards carefully

Read the options displayed by each chest before committing to a selection. The Restoration Scrolls are a separate part of the coupon reward, and the chests have their own selection interfaces. The code itself does not dictate which option will be most useful to your Family.

If you receive an already-used message, check whether you claimed the coupon earlier on the same Family. A deadline error or a region mismatch requires a different remedy from a typo. Older coupon strings that lack a current publisher deadline or reliable corroboration are excluded from this page.
