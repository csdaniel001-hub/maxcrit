Some codes may already be deactivated, used up or region-locked.

## How to redeem Monster Hunter Now offer codes

1. Open the [official offer redemption page](https://store.monsterhunternow.com/offer-redemption).
2. Sign in using the account credentials associated with your hunter.
3. Enter a code exactly as listed, including its capitalization.
4. Check the redemption result and open the game to collect or inspect the items.

The official help page requires Google sign-in for the redemption route. If you currently sign in through Apple only, follow the account-linking instructions in the official help article to add a Google login method before trying to claim a code. Avoid creating a separate hunter account simply to complete the login form.

## Inventory limits and missing rewards

The support instructions warn that inventory capacity can affect redemption. Check your Item Box if the website refuses to add items. If the site accepts the offer but the supplies do not appear in the game, restart the application and inspect the inventory again.

A code that worked for another hunter may not work for you if it has expired or you already redeemed it. Enter each code individually. Preserve the mixed capitalization in Brachydios and LetsGoOsaka. If an offer fails, move on to the next eligible one and consult the official support page before changing your login method.
