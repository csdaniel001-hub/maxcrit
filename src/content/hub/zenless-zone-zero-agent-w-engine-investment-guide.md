Should your next Encrypted Master Tapes go to a new Agent or a signature W-Engine? In Zenless Zone Zero, the answer starts with which role your squad is missing and how many searches you can afford if the featured item does not arrive early. A signature W-Engine can improve a well-built Agent, but it cannot supply an extra teammate for an unfinished squad.

## Budget for the featured item

Ordinary Exclusive Channels have 90-search hard pity and a 50% featured chance on an unguaranteed S-Rank. Ordinary W-Engine Channels use 80 searches and a 75% featured chance. A non-featured S-Rank makes the next S-Rank in that channel family guaranteed to be the featured item. Both families use Encrypted Master Tapes, but their counters are separate. Each tape costs 160 Polychrome when exchanged from currency.

| Target | Maximum from zero pity, unguaranteed | Polychrome equivalent |
|---|---:|---:|
| Featured Agent | 180 | 28,800 |
| Featured W-Engine | 160 | 25,600 |
| Both, starting from zero on both channels | 340 | 54,400 |

For a guaranteed Agent at 40 pity, the ceiling drops to 50 searches. For an unguaranteed W-Engine at 30 pity, it is 130. A player with 120 searches available can therefore reserve 50 for the Agent but cannot guarantee the W-Engine with the remaining 70. Spending those 70 anyway risks leaving the equipment unfinished.

V3.3 Phase II also has S-Rank Signal Customization: you can choose six eligible non-featured S-Rank Agents or W-Engines for the relevant pool. This improves the usefulness of a potential loss but does not guarantee a particular one of those six outcomes. Choose options that your roster could actually use.

## Special rerun channels use a different first guarantee

Exclusive Rescreening and W-Engine Reverberation are limited-time selectable rerun channels. When their Special Guarantee is available, your first S-Rank is the selected item: at most 90 searches for an Agent or 80 for a W-Engine from zero pity (14,400 or 12,800 Polychrome). Once that benefit is spent, standard guarantee rules apply within the relevant rerun channel. Do not count ordinary channel progress toward these separate channels. The Special Guarantee covers only the first S-Rank from that channel, so an S-Rank you have already pulled there means the standard rules apply to your next one. This section explains how the channels work whenever they return.

## Decide which investment solves the problem

| Account situation | Priority |
|---|---|
| Cannot assemble the required teams for your chosen content | Fill a missing role or save for the Agent that enables the team |
| Main damage dealer lacks a compatible buffer or Stun teammate | Check team conditions before spending on personal equipment |
| Established team with a functional alternative W-Engine | Keep the alternative and reserve for future banners |
| Strong team where the signature fixes a documented mechanical restriction | Compare the signature with your existing option and check the remaining budget |

For Anby: Soldier 0, Severed Innocence is her limited signature, but Starlight Engine offers an accessible A-Rank Attack alternative. Its temporary ATK bonus activates after a Dodge Counter or Quick Assist. If your planned sequence rarely includes those actions, the bonus may not cover the damage window you care about. Check your actual rotation before paying for a signature. A W-Engine's Specialty-specific passive also needs a matching Agent Specialty.

Do not assume every duplicate or signature offers the same gain. The value depends on the Agent's kit, current W-Engine, team, and whether the new passive changes something your rotation actually uses. Compare those conditions before committing to a banner.

## Protect the next banner's reserve

List the desired Agent and W-Engine separately, with their existing pity and guarantee status. Deduct the worst-case cost of your first priority from the total available Tapes and converted Polychrome. Leave rewards you have not yet earned in a separate column. The official Signal Search Statistical Tool announced November 20 helps with checking your history, and each channel's Details screen holds its current guarantee rules.

The [current events calendar](/hub/events/zenless-zone-zero-events.html) helps identify obtainable rewards and deadlines. Check the exact end time before counting an event's currency toward a pull. A signature can wait for a rerun if getting it now would prevent a more important team investment.
