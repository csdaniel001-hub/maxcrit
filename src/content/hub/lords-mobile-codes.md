## How to redeem Lords Mobile codes

1. Open the [IGG Exchange Center](https://lordsmobile.igg.com/gifts/).
2. Identify your account with your IGG ID or player name, as the page asks.
3. Enter a gift code and claim it.
4. Collect the reward from your in-game mail.

In the game, you can also tap the settings gear and scroll to Redemption Code.

## Why might a code fail?

Check capitalization, especially on the lowercase codes, and the deadline. Some offers have a fixed claim limit or need an updated client. Never share your password with a third-party code generator.
