Fewer Safaris are needed to level from Lv. 41 to 54, with compensation by mail for affected players. Completing the Chapter 6 challenges in Chapter of Breezy brings you to Grade I Trailblazer, which resumes Title Promotion in Chapter of Whisperwake. The update also lowers the largest RV unlock to level 16, improves Android downloads and map zoom, and targets S25 Ultra stuttering.

## Earlier Egg Heist changes

The November 17 update adds a Departure with the Wind: Collectible Gift Box for reaching Egg Emperor with 200 stars, and Egg Emperor players can buy a limited number of Collectible Blind Boxes each week in the Egg Heist shop. Giga-Egg jumping is restricted and attacks cause slight knockback.

Our [November 26 news](/news/aniimo-november-26-update-whisperwake-isles-events.html) covers the release in context, and the [Aniimo events calendar](/hub/events/aniimo-events.html) tracks the deadlines. The publisher notices linked above carry the complete fix lists.
