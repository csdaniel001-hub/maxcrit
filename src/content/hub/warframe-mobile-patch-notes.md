## Hotfix #5, November 2

Hotfix #5 is a small certified client update for mobile only, so depending on your auto-update settings you may need to update the app by hand.

| Area | Fixes |
|---|---|
| Touch and controller | Touch and controller menu navigation no longer interfere with each other; controller Aim Assist works when aiming down sights |
| Touch controls | Lock-on works against Dax in The Duviri Paradox; Autofire recognizes Follie in Follie's Hunt; Contextual Action Buttons appear in your Base of Operations |
| Camera | No longer detaches after dismounting a Dargyn, using Loki's Switch Teleport, exiting a wall latch or being hit by an Infested Charger |
| Crashes | Vena's Gemini Emote and Atlas's Rumblers with the Rumbled Augment |
| Movement | Harrow can bullet jump while channeling Thurible; Nyx can bullet jump during a Helminth ability on touch controls; Mesa keeps her movement when an ability is infused over Peacemaker |
| Progression | Dying in the Ravenous Maw sequence of The War Within no longer breaks touch controls |
| UI and settings | Video settings no longer reset on relaunch; elemental ammo indicator shows under Elemental Ammo Buffs; RPG ammo UI appears in a Warframe: 1999 sequence; Move Assist flicker in hubs fixed; Blueprints list where to farm their parts |

## Hotfix #4 + 4.1, October 28

On Android, Hotfix #4 fixed a progression stop in The Lotus Eaters quest caused by repeated touch inputs before the Operator and Drifter dialogue options appear. It also removed the Dismantle Demolishers modifier from Cavia Bounties, where it was making Rogue Necramechs permanently invulnerable, and dropped the Nami Skyla Prime Blade from 100 to 65 Ducats because of its varied rarity in Void Relics.
