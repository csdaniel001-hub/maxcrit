Silver Wolf LV.999 is an Imaginary Elation damage dealer and a separate character from Silver Wolf (Nihility: Quantum). Her equipment choices make much more sense once you understand why Speed and the team's Punchline production matter more than conventional attack stats. This guide assumes E0 unless noted.

## Relics and planar ornaments

| Slot | Starting build |
|---|---|
| Relics | 4-piece Ever-Glorious Magical Girl |
| Planar ornaments | 2-piece Punklorde Stage Zero |
| Body | CRIT Rate |
| Feet | SPD |
| Planar sphere | HP% or DEF% |
| Link rope | HP% or DEF% |

The in-game Build Guide and HSRZone recommend this combination. Diviner of Distant Reach is a reasonable alternative when its Speed makes a meaningful difference to turn order. Choose defensive planar pieces with better SPD and CRIT rolls instead of chasing ATK or Imaginary DMG mains for her Elation attacks.

False Ending Speedrun activates at 160 SPD, adding 50% Elation and another 2% per point above that threshold, counting at most 100 extra SPD. At 180 SPD, that contribution is 90%; at 200 it is 130%. These figures measure the Trace's Elation contribution. Above 160 SPD, additional Speed continues to help until the Trace cap; her signature makes higher totals easier to reach.

Hidden MMR supplies CRIT Rate during combat. Once the rate reaches 100%, further MMR contributes CRIT DMG instead. Build adequate CRIT for the early part of her damage window rather than assuming every excess point is wasted. When comparing relics, prioritize meeting the Speed breakpoint and then evaluate CRIT across the full cycle.

## How Aeon ★ Aha changes the build

Version 4.7 adds Faces of Elation ★ Holographic Mirage when the Path of Elation ascends. With Aeon ★ Aha at E0, Silver Wolf can overflow Hidden MMR by up to 360, gains 40% CRIT DMG and receives Hidden MMR equal to 20% of each Party Trick gained. The 999 overflow limit and 40% conversion require Aha E2. The higher resource ceiling also makes the conversion of excess CRIT Rate into CRIT DMG more relevant; do not sacrifice Speed merely to stack more CRIT.

## Light Cones

| Light Cone | Why use it |
|---|---|
| Welcome to the Cosmic City | Signature: SPD, Elation DEF ignore and Punchline on Ultimate |
| See You at the World's End! | Free S5 reward for completing the 4.7 Trailblaze Mission; CRIT DMG increases with Elation teammates and Aha on field |
| Mushy Shroomy's Adventures | Free alternative with Elation vulnerability that helps teammates |
| Today's Good Luck | Paid Battle Pass option; useful offensive stats but no SPD |

The signature at S1 adds 18% SPD. It makes demanding Speed targets easier, whereas either free cone lets you spend Stellar Jade on assembling the team first. Do not buy the Battle Pass solely to replace a usable free cone.

## Teams

These compositions illustrate documented synergies without performance rankings.

| Team | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Silver Wolf LV.999 / Yao Guang / Aeon ★ Aha / Gallagher | Elation core with a sustain slot |
| Silver Wolf LV.999 / Sparxie / Yao Guang / Gallagher | Double-damage setup with Skill Point interactions |
| Silver Wolf LV.999 / Trailblazer (Elation: Lightning) / Asta / Gallagher | Lower-cost setup using Punchline support and SPD buffs |

Gallagher is an example sustain; another built sustain can fill that slot. The in-game guide also lists Silver Wolf LV.999 / Sparxie / Yao Guang / Pearl as an Elation-focused lineup. Yao Guang helps feed her resource generation; Sparxie can spend Skill Points to trigger loot effects while also contributing damage. The Trailblazer (Elation: Lightning) is the accessible starting point when the limited supports are missing. Asta's Speed buff can help the third team, but it is not a substitute for an Elation teammate.

## Gameplay and Trace investment

Outside Godmode Player, use Skill to build Punchline. Her Ultimate becomes available at 60 Hidden MMR. Enter Godmode Player when your team can feed the damage window. At E0 without effects that grant extra uses, the state ends after her third Enhanced Basic ATK. Top Loot Box randomly selects one of three effects, so Skill Point recovery is conditional. Plan enough resources for the rest of the party.

Prioritize Basic ATK, then Ultimate, Talent and Elation Skill. The Speed conversion and Hidden MMR major Traces are especially important. Spend on those before repeatedly farming marginal upgrades to defensive pieces.
