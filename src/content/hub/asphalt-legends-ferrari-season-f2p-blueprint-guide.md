Ferrari Season gives Asphalt Legends players a choice between pursuing a featured car and building a broader garage. A free-to-play budget works best when you identify an achievable unlock first, then decide which events deserve your racing time and Credits.

## Ferrari Season events to plan around

| Event | Dates | Featured opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Mazda Furai Car Hunt | Nov 25–Dec 8 | Mazda Furai |
| VLF Force 1 V10 Car Hunt | Dec 4–17 | VLF Force 1 V10 |
| Club TLE | Dec 4–7 | Season-car Blueprints and Credits |
| Cyber Monday | Nov 30–Dec 5 | Trion Nemesis and Icona Vulcano Titanium feature in the event |

All dates are from Gameloft's November 10 patch notes.

The two Hunts overlap from December 4 through 8. If you already have progress toward the Furai, prioritize finishing that target before its deadline. The VLF hunt keeps running after the Furai window closes, which leaves a later stretch to concentrate on the second car. Our suggested approach is to choose one main target when event windows overlap, especially if time is limited.

## Make the Club event count

The season's Club TLE falls inside both Car Hunts. It is the clearest scheduled chance to pursue season-car Blueprints with your clubmates. When Gameloft introduced the Club Series in September, full rewards required 15 participating members, and Gameloft said the event returns on the last weekend of each Season. Coordinate participation with your clubmates before the event ends.

Club Milestones increased from 24 to 28, with the full Reputation target remaining 6,000,000. The first half contains Blueprints for season cars, while the second half contains their EIPs. A useful team target is the next Blueprint milestone. If the club can reach all 28 milestones, the later parts are an additional benefit.

For illustration, dividing the full 6,000,000 Reputation by 15 equally contributing players (the September full-reward threshold) yields 400,000 each. Actual contributions will vary.

## Decide when to commit upgrade resources

Gameloft's unlock FAQ distinguishes cars built with enough Blueprints from special event cars that require a Key. A stockpile of Blueprints may therefore fail to deliver an immediately usable car. Establish the unlock path before directing Credits or Tokens toward a featured model.

Keep a simple event budget. If you need another 20 Blueprints and can attempt 40 runs, you need to average 0.5 Blueprint per run. Treat this as a planning pace for your own runs. If progress falls short, reassess the target early enough to redirect your time. Give priority to rewards you can earn without paid purchases and avoid spending merely because a limited-time event is close to ending.

## A practical schedule for December 4–8

Reserve time first for the Club TLE through December 7. Use the remaining time toward the earlier-ending Furai hunt, then move your main focus to the VLF hunt after December 8 if you still need its Blueprints. Players who need no more Furai Blueprints can start the VLF hunt earlier. This schedule is our planning recommendation based on the published event windows; it makes no promise about event eligibility or drops.
