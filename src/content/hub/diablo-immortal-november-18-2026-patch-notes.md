Bout of Realms is coming, and Diablo Immortal's mid-November update gets the servers ready for it, with new reward activities and server changes alongside. Maintenance windows fall on November 18–19, depending on your region.

## Class balance highlights
| Class | Changes highlighted in Blizzard's balance notice |
|---|---|
| Necromancer | Whisperwill radius 6 → 12 yards, duration 4 → 3s, cooldown 12 → 9s; Rotted Harvest golem takes 75% less player damage; Black Wall summons two mobile Umbra Eaters |
| Druid | Budding Union changes to healing and ally damage support (+30% for 2s); Bristlebough gains two charges; Teeth of the Fen summons grow immediately |

The official forum post carries more values and equipment changes for all five classes. Preview values can change before release, so treat these numbers as provisional and go by the latest forum revision.

## Events and systems
- Harvest Treasures runs November 18–December 8, with a 3×3 scratch-card grid unlocked through ordinary gameplay tasks.
- Phantom Gallery runs November 18–December 1 and offers a finite pool of rewards through progressively priced draws.
- Maiden's Chains Battle Pass, Cross-Server Bout of Realms adjustments and quality-of-life changes are included.
- Selected EU and Korean servers are subject to population merges.

## Player priorities
Look up your regional server status first. Read the equipment changes for your class before you spend resources on gear, and clear the free gameplay tasks for Harvest Treasures while the grid is open.
