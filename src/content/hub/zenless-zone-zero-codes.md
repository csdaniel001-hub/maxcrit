## How to redeem Zenless Zone Zero codes

1. Finish enough of the introductory story to access the main menu and Mail.
2. Open the menu and select More.
3. Choose Redemption Code, paste the code and confirm.
4. Collect the items through Mail.

### Redeem online

1. Go to [the official ZZZ redemption page](https://zenless.hoyoverse.com/redemption).
2. Log in, select the correct server and character, and paste the code.
3. Redeem and check your in-game Mail.

## Why is my code not working?

Capitals, spelling and spaces cause most failures, so retype first. After that, suspect expiry, a full redemption cap, an earlier claim by you or a lock to another server. The More menu and the official ZZZ page are the only places to redeem.
