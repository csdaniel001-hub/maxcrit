Season 26 of Cosmic Crucible starts its first Trial on December 4 at 21:00 UTC. Defense setup opens 2 days earlier, and Ultimate 32 has already begun. The two competitions share some room names, but their summon and global rules differ. Copying an Ultimate 32 defense into the December season without checking the stage may waste its strongest advantage.

## Schedule and global modifiers

| Competition | Defense setup (UTC) | First Trial (UTC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ultimate 32 | Nov 25, 21:00 | Nov 26, 21:00 |
| Season 26 | Dec 2, 21:00 | Dec 4, 21:00 |

Both grant defenders 20% extra Max Health and 10% extra Damage. Completed attacks add 1 Exhausted to the defense; canceled attempts do not. Ultimate 32 applies a 15% Max Health self-attack on each character's turn. Season 26 instead heals Acolytes of Doom, on attack and defense, for 5% Max Health whenever one of their allies dies.

That changes how you should read the matchup before committing a squad. A character's recovery and the timing of its turns matter more under Ultimate 32's global modifier. In Season 26, plan for the opposing Acolytes of Doom to recover when allies fall. Recalculate the next kill after each recovery.

## Ultimate 32 room rules

| Stage | Rule and conditions (both sides) |
| --- | --- |
| 1: Doom Bot Parade | Any character death damages all enemies for 5% of the dying character's Max Health. Repeated consecutive summons of the same character anywhere on the field increase future instances of that summon's Max Health by 20% each. |
| 2: When It Rains | Applying a positive or negative effect adds 1 duration to all corresponding effects on allies of the recipient, capped at 3. |
| 3: Repair Protocol | TECH characters flip Defense Down and receive Repair on their turn. |

Do not treat every death in Stage 1 as ordinary attrition. A crowded field can create successive bursts of damage as characters fall. The repeated-summon effect specifically increases health for matching future summons; switching summon types is different from repeatedly producing the same unit. In Stage 2, check what the enemy can extend before you rely on a short debuff window ending.

## Season 26 room rules

| Stage | Rule and conditions (both sides) |
| --- | --- |
| 1: Standard Room | Global modifier only. |
| 2: Invulnerable | Raider or Skirmisher Iso-8: +200% Damage and Armor below 50% Health. |
| 3: When It Rains | Effect application extends matching positive or negative effects on the recipient's allies by 1, capped at 3. |
| 4: Doom Bot Parades | Summoned characters deal 5% Max Health damage to all enemies when they die. Repeated matching consecutive summons increase future matching summons' Max Health by 5% each, on either side. |
| 5: Repair Protocol | TECH characters flip Defense Down and gain Repair on their turns. |
| 6: Mark of the Serpent | Every turn includes a 15% Max Health self-attack. Successful hits inflict Heal Block, Bleed and Disrupted; Heal Block on turn grants Taunt, which lowers Resistance by 50%. |

Stage 2 deserves particular attention during roster setup. A squad relying on gradual damage can leave its enemy in the most dangerous health bracket. The bonus belongs to the qualifying Iso-8 classes; changing every character's class is not a universal upgrade. Consider the character's normal role and whether it can survive long enough below half health to use the modifier.

For Stage 4, the smaller health increment and summon-only death effect are important differences from Ultimate 32. Stage 6 is the other trap: healing plans must account for repeated self-damage as well as Heal Block from landed hits. A character with Heal Block can also acquire Taunt, exposing it with reduced Resistance. Plan for those interactions when assigning your remaining attackers.

## Prepare your roster before December 2

Start by reviewing the Iso-8 classes of candidates for Stage 2 and the TECH characters available for Stage 5. Keep a separate list of teams for Stage 6 that can deal with Heal Block. The standard room can house a team whose value depends on its own kit instead of a stage bonus. Use these room-selection principles alongside the actual opponent's lineup before choosing your attack. Scopely notes these rules can still change before they go live.
