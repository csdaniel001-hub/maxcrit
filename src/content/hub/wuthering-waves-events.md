Bountiful Crescendo, a limited-time material double drop, is live in Wuthering Waves until December 3 at 03:59 server time. It is one of 8 events in Version 3.8, which runs until the Version 4.0 update on December 22 (UTC+8).

Times below are server time unless a row says otherwise, so they follow your account's server wherever you play from. The Battle Rush contest uses Pacific time and gives a date only.

The second Endstate Matrix cycle, Adversity Vanguard, also ends with Version 3.8, on December 22 at 03:59 (UTC+8).

## Event details

### Bountiful Crescendo

- **Eligibility**: Union Level 14.
- **How it works**: spend Waveplates on Simulation Challenge or Forgery Challenge to receive double rewards.
- **Limit**: 3 double claims per day, shared between the two challenge types. The remaining count shows on the event page, and it refreshes daily at 04:00 server time.

Because the 2 challenge types draw on one allowance, spend the 3 daily claims on whichever material is holding back your next Resonator or weapon upgrade.

### Lahai-Roi Blocks: Turnabout

A Leisure Event update to Lahai-Roi Blocks that adds a rotate feature, along with new mechanics. It shares Bountiful Crescendo's end time, December 3 at 03:59.

### Second Coming of Solaris: Unwritten Dreams

A school-life simulation in the Second Coming of Solaris series. You plan a year of schedules and skills, prepare for a final evaluation and reach one of several Story Endings.

- **Rewards**: the Sigil Graduation Season and the Avatar Between Classes. animonlife reports up to 1,200 Astrite across the event.

### Dreamfield Odyssey

A Combat Event set in the Desolate Dreamfield, a new region of the Somnoire. Taking part awards the Title Beyond the Dreamfield.

### Lollo Campaign: Groundwork

Complete campaigns to earn Lollo Stamps, which you exchange for Lollo Helper special deliveries.

### Login events

Gifts of Bygone Dreams is a 7-day login event open since the Version 3.8 update. Whither the Wind is a second login event from December 7. Both end on December 21 at 03:59, so start Gifts of Bygone Dreams by December 14 to collect all 7 days.

### Wuthering Waves Battle Rush

A community contest for combat videos, with submissions closing on December 10 (PT). Contest rewards are not guaranteed.

## Before Version 4.0

Version 3.8 maintenance and bug-fix compensation (Astrite x300 and Crystal Solvent x2, plus a further Astrite x300) stays claimable in your mail until the next version update. Claim it before the Version 4.0 maintenance on December 22. Accounts needed a character and the Mail feature unlocked before November 12, 04:00 (UTC+8) to qualify.
