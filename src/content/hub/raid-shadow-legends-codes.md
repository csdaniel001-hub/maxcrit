## Which starter code should you choose?

Your first Champion should address a gap in your roster. If you already selected Kael at the start, spending your single code on another Kael offers less immediate variety. Miscreated Monster is a practical general progression choice when your team needs survivability. Harvest Jack offers crowd control and is a Legendary Champion, but rarity alone should not decide which code you use.

Coldheart is a specialist option for dungeon bosses; Demytha is worth considering if you intend to build a dedicated Clan Boss team later. Mordecai offers area HP Burn for Spider's Den progression. Uugo is a support option for accounts aiming toward Hydra. These are role-based editorial recommendations, with equipment and teammates ultimately determining how effective any Champion is.

## How to redeem RAID promo codes on mobile and PC

1. Open RAID and copy your User ID from Settings > Info.
2. Visit the [official redemption portal](https://redeem.raidshadowlegends.com/en/).
3. Enter your User ID and chosen code, then confirm.
4. Collect the reward from your Bastion Inbox. Skins and Skin Fragments may be applied directly to your account.

On Plarium Play, the Promo Codes tab is accessible from the left-side Bastion panel. Mobile players should use the web portal: Plarium's current support guidance reports the in-app option disabled on iOS and Android, although some older instructions still describe Android in-game entry.

## Redemption limits and common errors

Plarium requires new-player offers to be claimed within 72 hours of registration on an eligible unique device. Each account can redeem only one starter code. A successful promo code redemption starts a 24-hour cooldown, and you can make up to five activation attempts per hour. Copy each code exactly and avoid spending attempts on expired strings.

If you are a new player, claim your chosen starter offer first. Redeeming a general promo code beforehand consumes a day of your limited eligibility window. If you get an error, confirm your account age, device eligibility and region before trying a different code.
