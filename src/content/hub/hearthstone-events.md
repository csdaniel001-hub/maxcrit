December's calendar combines a new Battlegrounds season, a limited team Brawl and three consecutive reward events. The dates below come from the announced 37.2 schedule. Times are date-only except for the streamer event.

## December and January calendar

| Event | Dates | Reward or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Battlegrounds Season 15 | Dec 1 onward | Rating reset; earned unclaimed Season 14 rewards automatically collected |
| Carnival Card Back | Dec 1–Jan 12 | Free in-game shop claim; excludes mainland China |
| Battle of Y'Shaarj | Dec 1–22 | Remnant of the Empire, Whispers of the Old Gods and Arcane Seal (regular and Golden); two regular and two Golden expansion packs; Godslayer Thorim skin |
| Streamer 2v2 preview | Dec 2, 16:00–20:00 UTC | Twitch Drops on Thijs, Tars, Theo or Reqvam's channel: one pack per hour watched, two total |
| Heroic Tavern Brawl | Dec 2–9 | 1,000 Gold entry; Standard Brawliseum; run ends after three losses |
| 2v2 Tavern Brawl | Dec 9–23 | Free entry; first win awards one expansion pack |
| Winter's Veil | Dec 22–29 | The First Corrupted (regular and Golden), two expansion packs, random unowned Year of the Raptor Lite Hero Skin |
| New Year's event | Dec 29–Jan 12 | Fallen Horusath (regular and Golden), three expansion packs, two Tavern Tickets, random unowned Year of the Raptor Lite Hero Skin |
| New Year login gift | Jan 1–31 | One Reign of the Black Empire pack |

## What to play first

For collection progress, prioritize the reward track closing December 22 before starting the next one. The 2v2 Brawl uses preconstructed decks December 9–16 and player-built decks December 16–23. Either opponent reaching zero Health wins the match for your team. The Heroic Brawl has a separate paid entry; budget Gold accordingly.

See the [37.2 patch breakdown](/news/hearthstone-37-2-battlegrounds-season-15.html) for the new Battlegrounds mechanics, and the [Hearthstone patch tracker](/hub/patch-trackers/hearthstone-patch-notes.html) for balance history.
