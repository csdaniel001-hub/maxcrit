## How to redeem Brown Dust 2 codes

1. Complete the tutorial and check your current in-game nickname.
2. On Android or PC, open More and choose Register Coupon.
3. Enter the code and confirm, then claim your mail.

### Redeem online

1. Visit [the official Brown Dust 2 coupon form](https://redeem.bd2.pmang.cloud/bd2/).
2. Enter your current in-game nickname and coupon code.
3. Submit and claim the reward in-game. This is the preferred route on iOS.

## Why is my code not working?

Before blaming the coupon, compare it letter by letter, capitals and spaces included. A clean entry that still fails points to an expired or capped coupon, one your account has already used, or one meant for a different server. Only type coupons into the game or the official coupon form.
