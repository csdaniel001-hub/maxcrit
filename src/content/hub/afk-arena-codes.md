## How to redeem AFK Arena codes

1. In the game, open your profile and copy your UID.
2. Go to the [official Lilith gift code portal](https://cdkey.lilith.com/afk-global) and pick the edition you play.
3. Enter your UID and complete the verification step the portal shows.
4. Enter a code and confirm.
5. Collect the reward from your in-game mailbox.

Each code can be claimed once per UID. If you play both editions, use the Classic codes on your Classic UID and the Companions codes on your Companions UID.

## Why might a code fail?

The most common cause is using a code from the other edition. After that, check capitalization, the deadline in the table and whether the UID belongs to the right server. Never give your password to a third-party code site; the official portal only asks for your UID and its own verification.
