250% Energy Regen while her field is active is the number a Shorekeeper build works toward. The Shorekeeper is a Spectro Rectifier support whose value depends on how reliably the party reaches the strongest stage of her Stellarealm, so more healing or personal damage loses its worth if it delays her Resonance Liberation or keeps the team from its full CRIT buffs. The setup here assumes Sequence 0.

Those CRIT buffs are general-purpose, which is why the team table below runs from Camellya to Zani to Augusta. Specialist supports can still be the better pick for some carries, and the team section covers when.

## Energy Regen and Echoes

Her fully developed field can grant up to 12.5% CRIT Rate and 25% CRIT DMG. The full bonus requires 250% Energy Regen while the field is active, and the out-of-combat stat screen can show less.

| Setup | Pre-buff target | In combat with field passive |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fallacy of No Return active | 230% | 230 + 10 + 10 = 250% |
| Different Echo without ER bonus | 240% | 240 + 10 = 250% |

The first row assumes you actually cast Fallacy and that the passive is active. Building only to 230% without either bonus does not reach the cap. Extra Energy Regen beyond the cap can still help energy recovery, but does not increase those capped CRIT buffs.

| Echo slot | Support-first setup |
|---|---|
| Sonata Effect | 5-piece Rejuvenating Glow |
| Main Echo | Fallacy of No Return |
| 4-cost | Healing Bonus for comfort or CRIT DMG for Intro damage |
| Both 3-cost | Energy Regen until target is met |
| Both 1-cost | HP% |

Echo farming follows the same logic. Keep the cheapest pieces that satisfy the ER target before chasing double-CRIT rolls. Rejuvenating Glow grants team ATK through healing, and Fallacy supplies an extra team buff. Bell-Borne Geochelone is a survivability alternative; recalculate the ER target when switching. Moonlit Clouds supplies 10% Energy Regen from its two-piece effect and, with Impermanence Heron, is an alternative for buffing the next damage dealer after Outro; Rejuvenating Glow benefits the whole team through healing. In Jingran teams without a shield applier, WuWaBuild lists 5-piece Halo of Starry Radiance as a niche alternative to Rejuvenating Glow.

## Rectifiers

Stellar Symphony is the signature and helps cover the stat requirement while adding team utility. Variation supplies ER and Concerto on Skill, making it an especially attractive four-star choice. If neither is available, craftable Rectifier#25 or Call of the Abyss, whose recipe is a Rinascita exploration quest reward, can serve as no-gacha alternatives. Those options lack Variation's Concerto convenience, so don't assume identical rotation timings.

## Team setups

| Team | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Hsin / Suoming / The Shorekeeper | Unison core with general CRIT amplification and healing; Lily is a specialist alternative |
| Jingran / Iuno / The Shorekeeper | Iuno supplies her carry synergy while Shorekeeper handles healing and field buffs |
| Augusta / Iuno / The Shorekeeper | Iuno-supported hypercarry with general CRIT buffs and sustain |
| Aemeath / Lynae / The Shorekeeper | Current damage core with general-purpose field support |
| Camellya / Sanhua / The Shorekeeper | Main damage dealer, short support turn, healer and CRIT buffs |
| Zani / Phoebe / The Shorekeeper | Frazzle-focused core with sustain |
| Sigrika / Qiuyuan / The Shorekeeper | Echo Skill-focused core, general buffs and healing |

These examples cover current and established roster archetypes. Lily is also a specialized healer for Echo Skill and Unison teams. Shorekeeper's general-purpose CRIT buffs can fit many rosters; specialist supports may be preferable when the main damage dealer's own mechanics demand a different kind of buff.

## Make the field work

Start by charging Shorekeeper's Concerto using her Basic Attacks, Forte and Skill. Use Fallacy, cast Resonance Liberation and switch through her Outro into the next teammate. That teammate's Intro upgrades the field once; a second Intro from the next character upgrades it again. Plan around this sequence instead of casting Liberation and swapping aimlessly without full Concerto.

At S0, returning with Intro Skill Discernment ends the Supernal Stellarealm. Time this return near the end of its useful window; S1 removes the field-ending effect. Prioritize Intro Skill for its guaranteed-CRIT Discernment damage. Wutheringlab recommends Liberation next for healing, while Prydwen prioritizes Resonance Skill next. Choose based on your healing needs. Compare rotations by whether the next Liberation is available on time; the displayed ER number alone can mislead.

If resources are tight, level the weapon and the ER main-stat Echoes before spending Tuners on offensive pieces. A working rotation and full field buffs are much more useful than an impressive personal CRIT line. Start every Shorekeeper build from that 250% in-combat target.
