Wild Rift Patch 7.3 has received seven sets of official patch notes since September. This page lists them in order, with the date of each and what it changed, so you can see how the current game got here. Everything comes from Riot's patch notes. Riot releases content throughout each patch, so a change can arrive a few days after its notes.

## Every 7.3 patch at a glance

| Patch | Notes posted | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 7.3 | September 21 | Hwei, the marksman overhaul, jungle changes, Ranked Season S23, new items |
| 7.3a | September 29 | Champion and item adjustments, Smite, Nexus and Turret Plating tuning, AAA ARAM |
| 7.3b | October 14 | Champion balance, item and rune updates |

## Patch 7.3: the system changes

The first patch rebuilt how marksmen and critical strikes work:

- **Critical strikes**: base Critical Strike Damage went from 175% to 200%. Infinity Edge's passive now raises it to 230%, up from 200%.
- **Attack Speed**: the cap rose from 2.5 to 3 attacks per second, and each champion now has an individual Attack Speed ratio.
- **Lifesteal**: a new stat for basic attacks and on-hit damage. It replaces the old vamp on Mercurial Scimitar, Vampiric Scepter, Bloodthirster, Blade of the Ruined King and Gunmetal Greaves.
- **Items**: Fiendhunter Bolts, Yun Tal Wildarrows, Hexoptics C44, Rapid Firecannon and Hearthbound Axe were added, Immortal Shieldbow and Stormrazor returned, and Statikk Shiv became an on-hit item.

The jungle changed as well. Smite starts at 600 true damage and upgrades to 1000 and then 1400. Monster levels now follow the average team level and no longer follow game time, and the first Rift Scuttler spawns at 1:30.

Ranked Season S23 began on September 23. Players from Platinum through Diamond now get five bans per team, up from three, and there is a new Legendary S rating. The ranked reward skin is Glorious Eminence Milio, and the Wild Pass features Pencil Sharpener Kog'Maw.

## Patch 7.3a

The first follow-up touched champions on both sides of the marksman changes.

- **Champions**: Hwei, Samira, Rammus, Malphite, Tristana, Draven, Caitlyn, Senna, Syndra, Swain, Yuumi, Viego, Fiddlesticks and Nasus.
- **Items**: Yun Tal Wildarrows, Whispering Circlet, Diadem of Songs and Death's Dance.
- **Systems**: Smite, the Nexus and Turret Plating.

## Patch 7.3b

| Champion | What changed |
|---|---|
| Volibear | Frenzied Maul's Attack Damage ratio and empowered healing went up |
| Jhin | Whisper's Critical Strike Damage went up, and its damage cap arrives at level 10 |
| Tryndamere | Battle Fury gives a flat Critical Strike Chance per Fury, and Spinning Slash deals more damage |
| Yasuo | Way of the Wanderer's Critical Strike Damage and shield went up |
| Hwei | Damage and shields went down on several abilities |
| Nocturne | Less Health per level and a longer Paranoia cooldown |
| Yunara | Less damage on Cultivation of Spirit and a longer Transcend One's Self cooldown |
| Senna | Less Critical Strike Damage on Absolution and less base damage on Piercing Darkness |

Youmuu's Ghostblade, Locket of the Iron Solari, Statikk Shiv and Yun Tal Wildarrows all got cheaper, while Thornmail's price went up. Among the runes, Grasp of the Undying and Aery were weakened and Cheap Shot's cooldown was shortened.

## Patch 7.3d

- **Stronger**: Camille, Lee Sin and Akali gained Attack Speed per level. Lee Sin's Dragon's Rage and Miss Fortune's Bullet Time deal more damage.
- **Weaker**: Malphite's Granite Shield and Thunderclap, Samira's Flair and Inferno Trigger ratios, Ashe's Frost Shot bonus damage, and Singed's Health and Movement Speed all went down. Ekko deals less damage to monsters.
- **Reworked numbers**: Shyvana and Nasus had changes in both directions.
- **Items**: Fiendhunter Bolts and Essence Reaver went up, Divine Sunderer got cheaper, and Whispering Circlet, Diadem of Songs and Stormrazor went down.

Turrets now ramp up less on consecutive attacks, and minions take less damage when their team is ahead.

## Patch 7.3f

The newest patch added Sylas, the Unshackled. It nerfed Kalista, Shen, Kassadin and Eclipse, buffed Yasuo, Lucian, Rumble and several mage items, and slowed tank junglers through Smite. Our news story on 7.3f has the exact numbers.

## What this page doesn't cover

Patches 7.3c and 7.3e are about AAA ARAM and event content, so they have no entry of their own here. The exact numbers for each change are in Riot's notes, linked in the sources. The Reindeer Mini Pass, with Reindeer Smolder, is listed in the 7.3 notes for December 3.
