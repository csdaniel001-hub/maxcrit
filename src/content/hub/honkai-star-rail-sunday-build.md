Your carry's action order decides Sunday's build, so settle it before you chase a single CRIT DMG roll. He is an Imaginary Harmony support whose Skill gives one ally and its summon an immediate turn.

The lineups further down pair him with Aglaea, Jing Yuan, Himeko • Nova, an E2 Acheron and Phainon, so an account with one of those carries has a clear use for him. Pick the Speed configuration first; the gear follows.

## Choose a Speed configuration

| Configuration | Example | Why use it |
|---|---|---|
| Slower than carry | Carry 135 SPD, Sunday 134 SPD | Carry acts naturally, then Sunday advances it. |
| Hyperspeed | Sunday 160+ SPD, carry with base-Speed damage boots | Sunday supplies frequent turns without demanding SPD boots on the carry. |

Prydwen treats hyperspeed as a niche build that usually trails the slower-than-carry setup, and Icy Veins recommends it only when the team is built for it.

In a simple turn order, 135 SPD takes 74.07 AV and 134 SPD takes 74.63 AV. The difference lets the carry act first. Treat this as a starting example: summons, memosprite mechanics, buffs and action advance can change the sequence. Aglaea in particular needs tuning around her own Speed changes.

## Equipment

| Slot | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Relics | 4-piece Sacerdos' Relived Ordeal |
| Planars | Lushaka, the Sunken Seas; Penacony, Land of the Dreams with Imaginary carries; Broken Keel at 30% Effect RES |
| Body | CRIT DMG |
| Feet | SPD |
| Sphere | HP% or DEF% |
| Rope | Energy Regeneration Rate |

Mix two-piece SPD sets if necessary to reach the planned turn order. For Lushaka, put the intended ATK-scaling carry in the first party slot. Penacony only boosts allies of Sunday's Type, so use it with Imaginary carries. Broken Keel gives all allies 10% CRIT DMG once Sunday reaches 30% Effect RES; his minor Traces and the set's own 10% cover most of that, and Effect RES substats close the gap. Lushaka and Sprightly Vonwacq also add 5% Energy Regeneration, which Broken Keel lacks.

The in-game Build Guide also lists Messenger Traversing Hackerspace and Eagle of Twilight Line. Both change turn order, so retune Speed after equipping either. During Version 4.7, Grab Your Opening Gifts includes Asat Pramad's Gift, which creates one custom max-level 5-star Relic from sets released up to 4.7; Sacerdos' Relived Ordeal SPD boots make his Speed tuning easier.

| Light Cone | Tradeoff |
|---|---|
| A Grounded Ascent (S1) | Signature supports frequent Ultimates and restores SP after repeated targeted abilities. |
| But the Battle Isn't Over (S1) | Standard alternative, but the one-turn damage buff and less reliable Energy economy are limitations. |
| Past and Future (S5) | Accessible Forgotten Hall option with a next-acting ally damage bonus; no Energy assistance. |
| Dance! Dance! Dance! | Situational team advance; avoid if its timing disrupts a tuned carry. |

A three-action Ultimate cycle is easier with the signature. Without it, do not assume that every rotation will recharge in exactly three turns. When in doubt, choose Energy reliability over a few extra CRIT substats.

## Teams

| Lineup | Application |
|---|---|
| Aglaea / Sunday / Tribbie / Huohuo | Memosprite-focused carry with additional amplification and Energy support. |
| Jing Yuan / Sunday / Robin / Huohuo | Advance Jing Yuan and Lightning-Lord, with Robin supporting the team. |
| Himeko • Nova / Sunday / Mortenax Blade / Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae | Official in-game lineup; Sunday's Skill and Ultimate target Himeko • Nova, with Mortenax Blade as the Nihility amplifier. |
| Acheron E2 / Sunday / Jiaoqiu / Gallagher | Debuff-based carry with her reduced Nihility requirement satisfied. |
| Phainon / Sunday / Cerydra / Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae | Official in-game lineup and Prydwen's most-used MoC team; Sunday advances Phainon, while Cerydra, a Harmony character, buffs alongside him. |

The in-game Build Guide pairs Jing Yuan with Tribbie and Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae, and Aglaea with Robin and Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae; either Harmony partner works in those lineups.

The Acheron lineup is specifically for E2; an E0 Acheron needs two other Nihility characters to fully activate The Abyss. Acheron gains nothing from Sunday's Energy restoration and uses his immediate action and CRIT buffs instead.

## Rotation and upgrades

In a slower-than-carry build, let the carry finish its turn before using Sunday's Skill on it. Cast his Ultimate on that carry to maintain The Beatified and make future targeted Skills more SP-efficient. The Skill cannot advance another Harmony character.

For upgrades, prioritize Skill, Ultimate and Talent, with Basic ATK last, and unlock all major Traces for Energy and debuff removal. Then check the actual turn order after every equipment change, especially when using Messenger Traversing Hackerspace or teamwide action advance effects. With Sunday, the turn order is the build.
