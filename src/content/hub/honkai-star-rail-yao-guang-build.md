Yao Guang makes Elation teams more dangerous without competing for the main damage dealer's turns. Her build needs to solve two problems: keeping her Ultimate available and providing enough Speed and Elation to improve the party's damage. At E0, a fast, well-equipped Yao Guang is more useful than one geared like a conventional Physical DPS.

## Relics and stats

| Slot | Starting build |
|---|---|
| Relics | 4-piece Diviner of Distant Reach; Messenger Traversing Hackerspace as the alternative |
| Planar ornaments | Lushaka, the Sunken Seas or Sprightly Vonwacq; Broken Keel once she reaches 30% Effect RES |
| Body | CRIT DMG or CRIT Rate |
| Feet | SPD |
| Sphere | HP% or DEF% |
| Rope | Energy Regeneration Rate |

Prioritize SPD substats, followed by CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG. Elation damage does not directly scale with ATK, so a defensive sphere is preferable. Lushaka's first-slot ATK buff helps allies with ATK conversions and adds nothing to Elation damage directly.

During Version 4.7, Grab Your Opening Gifts includes Asat Pramad's Gift, which creates one custom max-level 5-star Relic from sets released up to 4.7. Diviner of Distant Reach SPD boots are the most direct use for her.

Amaze-In Grace adds 30% Elation at 120 SPD and another 1% for every point above 120, counting up to 200 extra SPD, so Speed past 120 also raises her Elation. Diviner of Distant Reach and Sprightly Vonwacq also check 120 SPD, and Diviner's larger CRIT Rate bonus checks 160. A practical goal is 160 SPD. Prydwen's best-in-slot target is 200 SPD, which needs her signature. With no action advance or delay, action value is 10,000 / SPD: 62.5 AV at 160 SPD and 50 AV at 200 SPD, which allows three natural actions in 150 AV. Prydwen also tunes Aha to 134 SPD, a breakpoint that needs a second Elation ally, and Version 4.7 raises the Aha Instant's base SPD when Aeon ★ Aha is in the team. Keep a working four-piece set unless a Speed change reaches one of these breakpoints.

## Light Cones

| Light Cone | When to use it |
|---|---|
| When She Decided to See (S1) | Signature; helps with SPD, team CRIT and Energy economy. |
| Mushy Shroomy's Adventures (S5) | Forgotten Hall shop option for Elation teams; weaker Ultimate economy. |
| See You at the World's End! (S5) | Free from the 4.7 Trailblaze Mission; raises only her own CRIT DMG (10% per Elation character at S5, plus 20% while Aeon ★ Aha is on the field), with no SPD, Energy or team buff. |

The signature smooths her rotation, and the build works without it. Do not assume that a different Elation cone provides the same Energy generation.

## Teams and gameplay

| Team | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Silver Wolf LV.999 / Yao Guang / Aeon ★ Aha / Pearl | Dedicated Elation carry, with an established sustain partner. |
| Sparxie / Yao Guang / Aeon ★ Aha / Pearl | Skill Point-consuming carry; exploit Great Boon's extra activation on attacks that spend SP. |
| Evanescia / Yao Guang / Trailblazer (Elation: Lightning) / Pearl | Elation-focused alternative without the playable Aeon. |
| Silver Wolf LV.999 / Yao Guang / Trailblazer (Elation: Lightning) / Gallagher | Budget support and sustain options; Silver Wolf LV.999 remains a limited character. |
| Aventurine • Waveflair / Yao Guang / Aeon ★ Aha / Pearl | Official in-game lineup: three Elation characters sharing her Zone and Great Boon, with Pearl healing the team. |

Outside Elation, Prydwen notes that she fits an Archer team with Sparkle.

Use Skill to establish her Elation-sharing Zone. Use Basic ATKs between refreshes to conserve SP and generate Punchline. Her 180-Energy Ultimate supplies an additional Aha Instant using a fixed 20 Punchline without spending the existing pool. Level Ultimate and Elation Skill first, then Skill and Talent, with Basic ATK last, as the in-game Build Guide recommends; unlock her major Traces for resource and buff uptime.

Version 4.7 adds Faces of Elation ★ Mind Reading, active when Aeon ★ Aha makes the Path of Elation ascend. At Aha E0, Great Boon's DMG multiplier rises by 20% of its original value, and after her Ultimate Yao Guang regenerates a fixed 12 Energy and gains 10 Bliss. At Aha E2 those become 40% and 30 Energy, with the same 10 Bliss. The fixed Energy refund shortens the wait for her 180-Energy Ultimate, so Aha's Eidolons affect her Ultimate economy as well as her damage. Avoid an ATK-only build, and don't spend all your SP refreshing her Zone early.
