Six Wild Shards can transform a deck you have spent months leveling. Your next Evolution should solve a problem that comes up regularly in the deck you already play, whether that is stopping a ground push, keeping a tank alive long enough for its support troops to connect, or creating more pressure at the bridge.

For Giant beatdown players, Giant is an obvious candidate to investigate this season. Royal Ghost and Goblin Cage make sense for different reasons, while Mirror rewards decks built to exploit its unusual copying mechanic. Battle Ram players should pay particular attention to the upcoming balance decisions before spending their Shards.

## How to get and spend Wild Shards

An Evolution unlocks once you have six Shards assigned to its card. Wild Shards let you fill the remaining requirement for an Evolution of your choice. You can earn them through the Shop and Pass Royale. You can hold only six unassigned Wild Shards, and any extra overflow, so sitting at six while more are due from the Shop or Pass Royale wastes them.

Card-specific Evolution Shards have additional sources, including challenges, events, Trophy Road, Evolution Boxes, Magic Lucky Chests and Seasonal Lucky Chests. Once a card's Evolution is unlocked, additional Shards specific to that card convert into copies at an Arena-dependent rate.

Suppose you have four Giant Shards and six Wild Shards. Spending two on Giant completes the unlock and leaves four available for another card. Before committing them, inspect the remaining rewards you expect to collect this season. An upcoming Giant reward could save those two Wild Shards entirely.

## Make sure your deck can use the Evolution

The Evolution Slot unlocks at 600 trophies (Arena 3), and the Wild Slot becomes available at 3,000 trophies (Arena 10). An unlocked Evolution behaves like its ordinary card when placed in a regular deck slot.

When a card occupies an eligible slot, its normal deployments advance the charge shown by the diamonds on the card. The next deployment after the diamonds fill uses its evolved form. The cycle then starts over. Event formats using restricted decks may limit access, and Mega Draft challenges normally exclude Evolutions, although specific challenges can include them.

Having an unlocked Evolution therefore creates a second decision: which card deserves the slot? If the new choice displaces your most dependable defensive tool, the offensive improvement needs to compensate for that loss. Consider the matchups you struggle against before changing both slots at once.

## Which Evolution fits your deck?

| Your existing deck | Candidate | What the Evolution adds |
|---|---|---|
| Giant beatdown | Giant | Rage-enhanced movement and attacks once he is wounded, plus a Rage trail for supporting troops |
| Royal Ghost bridge pressure | Royal Ghost | Two Souldiers summoned with damage when Ghost becomes visible |
| Goblin Cage defense | Goblin Cage | Captures eligible enemy troops and damages them inside the Cage |
| Mirror-focused deck | Mirror | Copies your last card in evolved form, one level higher, when that card has an Evolution |
| Battle Ram bridge spam | Battle Ram | Its charge can now be interrupted by Zap and similar interactions; wait for December balance decisions |

Royal Ghost already fits decks that use him to threaten a lane or convert surviving defenders into a counterpush. His Souldiers provide additional bodies when he appears. If you also use Battle Ram, compare whether your losses come from weak bridge pressure or insufficient defensive support before choosing which card to evolve.

Goblin Cage is a useful candidate when it already anchors your defense. Its capture mechanic grabs an enemy troop and damages it inside the Cage. The August balance patch changed its cycle requirement from one to two, so don't assume it will be available for every push. The October 2025 update also excluded Mega Knight during his jump from the capture interaction.

Battle Ram remains a natural candidate for an established bridge spam deck. However, specialist tracker RoyaleAPI's November 20 work-in-progress report identifies its later-bounce damage as a proposed December nerf. Treat that proposal as provisional and wait for the final balance list if Battle Ram is your main reason for spending six Wild Shards today.

## Giant or Mirror in King vs. King?

Evolved Giant becomes enraged once he has taken enough damage, leaving a trail of Rage that boosts the troops following him, and each of his attacks makes that trail bigger. Try to preserve the units supporting your Giant so they can exploit that trail as the push moves forward. An unsupported Giant may die before the rest of the deck benefits.

Giant can be unlocked in Pass Royale or with Wild Shards. If you're taking him from the Pass, your Wild Shards can go to another unlock.

Evolved Mirror reproduces your last played card in its evolved form, one level higher, as long as that card has an Evolution, even if the card isn't in your Evolution Slot or your Collection. A card with no Evolution receives the ordinary Mirror treatment at the increased level. Mirror therefore offers ways to access Evolutions beyond your other dedicated slots, provided your sequencing allows you to copy the intended card.

Mirror Evolution arrived in the Shop in the season's second week and can also be unlocked with Wild Shards. Its value depends on how frequently you can afford to repeat a useful play. A deck that needs an immediate defensive response after committing to offense may find its previous card a poor target for Mirror. Giant players have a simpler investment decision because the Evolution strengthens a card already central to their game plan.

## Decide before spending

Start with the three or four cards you deploy most often in meaningful situations, then narrow the options to cards you would keep in your deck even without an Evolution. Compare their available Shards, current levels and the role each would occupy. A card needing two more Shards may be an excellent investment when a six-Shard alternative provides only a small improvement.

The King vs. King Royal Market runs through December 7. Review its remaining rewards and your Pass Royale rewards before using flexible Shards. Saving them keeps your options open while the December balance changes are pending, but only up to six: if you're already at six and more are due, assign some before they overflow.
