Zhao is an Ice Defense Agent who fills a support slot through team buffs, parries and Ether Veil: Wellspring. She was offered as a free limited Agent in the Version 2.5 benefits event. She was also available on the Version 2.5 Priceless Heart Signal Search.

## How Zhao supports a damage dealer

Zhao's appeal is the combination of short field time, offensive support and the ability to parry attacks. She does not need to play as a traditional Ice carry to make a meaningful contribution. The key is to bring her onto the field when her gauge is ready, activate her Veil and return control to the Agent who can spend the team's buffs. This makes her particularly convenient when you need a second support for endgame and already have a specialist assigned elsewhere.

At full Core investment, Wellspring activation grants the team 1,000 ATK for 50 seconds. The Veil itself provides 5% Max HP and lasts 40 seconds. Crystallization's DMG Bonus requires Zhao to be within an Ether Veil; it therefore has a different duration constraint from the ATK buff. Keep the two effects separate when planning when to swap. An ATK buff can remain active even when the Veil has expired.

Zhao enters combat with 100 Frostbite Points, her maximum, subject to the Investigation Zone cooldown. Switching her in while fighting at full gauge triggers the entry ability, consumes the resource and establishes Wellspring. The active Agent's attacks can contribute six points once every three seconds. That gives Zhao a way to recharge without staying on the field. Her EX Special, Ultimate and Chain Attack provide additional points. The gauge and available Energy tell you when to act; the goal is to avoid unnecessary field time when the carry is ready to attack.

## HP investment and stats

When her Additional Ability is active, Zhao supplies a teamwide DMG bonus while in an Ether Veil. It starts at 10%, then increases by 1 percentage point for each additional 400 initial Max HP above 15,000, up to 40% at 27,000 HP. An Attack, Anomaly or Support teammate satisfies the composition condition.

| Initial Max HP | DMG bonus in Ether Veil |
|---:|---:|
| 19,000 | 20% |
| 23,000 | 30% |
| 25,000 | 35% |
| 27,000 | 40% |

This table uses exact multiples of the 400-HP increment to isolate the buff. The difference between 25,000 and 27,000 HP is five percentage points of additive DMG bonus. If reaching the cap requires giving up a substantial amount of Energy Regen, assess Ether Veil downtime first. A weaker buff that is active during a carry's burst may be more useful.

### The value of an attainable HP target

At 23,000 HP, Zhao supplies 30% conditional DMG Bonus, which is already three quarters of the maximum stated bonus. At 25,000 she supplies 35%. If an Energy Regen disc allows the squad to maintain the Veil during an entire carry burst, it can be a better starting point than a maximum-HP build that cannot repeat the setup. Treat the cap as a long-term equipment target. Zhao can join a team well before reaching it.

To illustrate the diminishing impact of another five percentage points of additive DMG Bonus, assume the carry already has 60% other applicable additive DMG Bonus. Adding Zhao's 35% takes this isolated multiplier to 1.95; adding the full 40% takes it to 2.00. That is about a 2.56% relative gain in that one multiplier. This simplified calculation excludes other damage categories, enemy modifiers and any buff downtime. It explains why a costly last HP upgrade may matter less than the support rotation.

The Core Passive also grants Zhao personal CRIT Rate based on initial HP, at maximum Core Skill level, 1.4 percentage points per 1,000 initial HP. At 25,000 HP that represents 35 percentage points. This is a separate effect from Crystallization. It is useful for her own attacks, but she is still primarily built to support the carry. Avoid counting an in-combat Max HP increase toward a requirement explicitly based on initial HP.

## W-Engines and Drive Discs

| W-Engine | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Half-Sugar Bunny (P1) | Signature with HP and Energy support, plus offensive team utility. |
| Original Transmorpher (P5) | A-Rank option with useful HP stats; usual F2P pick. |
| Tusks of Fury (P1) | Premium alternative if already owned; can help with Daze and team damage, but makes the HP target harder. |

Four-piece Bunny in Wonderland is the set built for Zhao's role. Its two-piece gives 10% HP, and its four-piece gives the squad 6% DMG per stack, up to 3 stacks for 25 seconds, triggered by a Defense equipper's EX Special or a squad member's Defensive or Evasive Assist. Pair it with two-piece Yunkui Tales when HP is short, or Swing Jazz or Moonlight Lullaby for Energy Regen.

Before Bunny in Wonderland arrived, the standard choices were four-piece Astral Voice in Attack teams and four-piece Proto Punk in Anomaly or double damage-dealer teams. Both still work if you already have good pieces, and Proto Punk is the closer match to what Bunny in Wonderland does.

| Disc | Main stat |
|---|---|
| 4 | HP% |
| 5 | HP% |
| 6 | Energy Regen (HP% if required and rotation stays reliable) |

Prioritize HP% and then flat HP substats until the initial HP target is reached; Mobalytics and Genshin-Builds both list them in that order. CRIT substats only add to Zhao's own damage, so take them after the HP target.

### Choose discs for the complete setup

Equip four Bunny in Wonderland pieces first and use the remaining slots to meet the actual stat requirement. With an HP-focused W-Engine, try an Energy Regen Disc 6 before deciding to farm another HP main stat. With a weapon that lacks HP, use the HP two-piece and compare both Disc 6 options. Check the character sheet after replacing gear and use the initial Max HP value for the table above.

Original Transmorpher makes sense for players who already own Zhao and want to build her without another limited banner. Its relevant stat contribution is available even when the weapon's defensive effects do not match the encounter. Half-Sugar Bunny can ease the competition between HP and resource recovery. Tusks of Fury is a possible reuse option on an account that already has it; it is more difficult to meet the HP goal, so the comparison should include the full disc set. Do not purchase another expensive engine solely because Zhao lacks a perfect sheet stat.

Bunny in Wonderland provides team utility. Keep its activation opportunity in the opening route.

## Teams

| Squad | Role |
|---|---|
| Ye Shunguang, Dialyn, Zhao | Premium Attack lineup that exploits Ether Veil support and burst windows. |
| Alice, Yuzuha, Zhao | Physical Anomaly support option when another off-field buffer is needed. |
| Ye Shunguang, Astra Yao, Zhao | Common Ye Shunguang variant with Astra Yao as the second support. |
| Ye Shunguang, Trigger, Zhao | Ye Shunguang variant with Trigger as the Stunner, used less often. |

The two Ye Shunguang variants cover players without Dialyn, and the Alice lineup is a flexible roster option whose value depends on the encounter. A specialist support can outperform Zhao when a carry requires a particular damage type, Anomaly buildup or a specific Stun rotation. She is especially useful when the better specialist is assigned to another endgame team.

### Where Zhao fits and where she does not

Ye Shunguang with Dialyn is a natural example of the supporting role. The main attacker needs a prepared damage window and the Stunner needs enough time to establish it. Use Zhao's opening Veil, build toward the Stun with Dialyn, and ensure the carry enters with the relevant buffs and resources. Avoid switching Zhao in during the middle of a committed attack chain unless the remaining buff duration demands it.

Alice with Yuzuha shows why an Ice Defense unit can still support a Physical damage dealer. The relevant effects are general ATK and DMG buffs, and Alice already has a Support teammate for her own Additional Ability. Zhao's Ice attacks can influence the Anomaly sequence, so avoid accumulating an unwanted status directly before Alice needs a particular Assault interaction. The aim is support uptime with minimal interference in the carry's resource loop.

For Ye Shunguang with Astra Yao, establish both supports before the carry's burst; with Trigger, preserve the Stun window for the carry. In Anomaly teams, an Energy Regen Disc 6 can help Zhao maintain Wellspring. Open with Entry Skill, Quick Assist into the Anomaly driver and let passive hits rebuild Frostbite. Use EX Special when the gauge needs another twenty points.

## Wellspring rotation

Frostbite Points come from EX Special and Ultimate (20 points each), Chain Attacks (5) and the passive 6 points every 3 seconds while the active Agent hits. Once the gauge is full, switch Zhao in during combat to trigger her Entry Skill and activate Ether Veil: Wellspring. An Assist Follow-Up also activates it from a full gauge. Take the Quick Assist back into the damage dealer; Final Verdict keeps charging while Zhao is off-field. Plan the next swap around the main damage window and the Veil duration.

### Skill priority

Max the Core Skill first, because it carries the team ATK buff and the HP-based CRIT Rate. After that the two specialist guides differ. Prydwen ranks Special Attack first, then Chain Attack, then Basic Attack, with Assist and Dodge last. Mobalytics puts Basic Attack and EX Special right after the Core Skill, Assist and Ultimate next, and leaves Dodge at level 1. Special Attack is near the top of both lists, so level it next.

In practice, get the support loop working with Original Transmorpher and HP discs first. Pull for the signature only if buff uptime or the HP budget is still the team's problem.

## Repeatable Veil maintenance

A passive-only refill gives 6 points every 3 seconds when the active Agent keeps hitting. Seventeen eligible triggers would supply at least 100 points. Depending on when the first trigger occurs, that takes roughly 48 to 51 seconds from an empty gauge in an uninterrupted illustrative sequence. Wellspring lasts 40 seconds, so passive generation alone should not be assumed to guarantee full uptime. EX Specials and Ultimates (20 points each) and Chain Attacks (5 points) can bridge that gap. If another compatible Ether Veil is present, inspect the actual buff state before assuming Veils stack.

When a boss is about to enter Stun, check three conditions: the current Wellspring timer, Frostbite Points and Zhao's available Energy or Decibels. If the Veil will cover the burst, stay with the Stunner or carry. If it will expire before the important attacks, make the refresh first. Use the Quick Assist back into the intended damage dealer; squad order can affect who receives it. Watch the on-screen prompt before swapping.

## Mindscape Cinema and account investment

M1 gives the team 15% All-Attribute RES ignore for 50 seconds after Zhao switches off-field. This is especially relevant to her intended short-field-time playstyle. M2 ties to her healing: it raises Zhao's ATK by 20% and the other squad members' ATK by 15% for 50 seconds. Its trigger needs to occur in the actual rotation. M4 adds Decibels on Veil activation and buffs several of her own attacks. M6 raises the CRIT Rate from her Core Passive to 125% of its value and stops her attacks from consuming Final Verdict's stored charge, which moves her toward an on-field damage role. That is an expensive direction for a character whose main job is enabling the carry.

At M0, prioritize the Core Skill and useful HP gear. Consider the signature if the HP and resource budgets are difficult to reconcile with owned options. M1 is an optional offensive upgrade once the rest of the squad is built; it does not address missing core teammates or inconsistent field execution. Players who obtained Zhao during her original free distribution can get a great deal of practical account value without spending premium currency on either upgrade.
