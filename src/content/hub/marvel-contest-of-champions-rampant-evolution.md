Alliance Quest enlistment for Rampant Evolution opened November 23, with the season and Raid Event beginning November 30. Kabam's new Doctor Doom-themed map brings Iron Man (Infamous) into both miniboss slots and Doctor Doom into the final encounter. The eligible attacker pool has grown to 69 Champions.

## Attacker pool and new buff

Spider-Man (Symbiote), Howard The Duck and Captain Marvel are the 3 new additions. The Mutanous Technology attacker buff also targets Professor X, Omega Sentinel and Magneto (House of X). Their changes are specific to their kits and do not apply to the entire roster.

| Champion | Mutanous Technology benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Professor X | Starts with 2 Cerebro Charges when he would start with 1; personal Prowess Buffs last 9 seconds longer. |
| Omega Sentinel | Her personal non-damaging debuffs pause during opponent Specials and for 2 seconds after them. Each Armor Up consumed on activating a Special extends inflicted or refreshed debuffs by 2 seconds. |
| Magneto (House of X) | Prowess from hitting and being hit lasts 5 seconds longer, with a cap increased by 3 stacks. |

Use the new options to broaden your alliance's route assignments, but do not assume one of the buffed Champions automatically counters the final boss. The node package still governs the matchup.

## Iron Man and Doctor Doom boss nodes

Both Iron Man minibosses use the same 4 modifiers. Galvanize cycles between Charge-Up and Overcharge every 15 seconds: attacks into his block during Charge-Up apply a Shock dealing 200% of the attacker's Attack over 6 seconds; all his attacks become Unblockable in Overcharge and Ability Accuracy modification cannot prevent it. Immovable Object awards a Steadfast Buff at each 10 combo hits, allowing 1 Unblockable hit to be blocked and adding 7.5% Block Proficiency during defender Specials per buff. Intercepts provide a Guaranteed Critical Passive for 7 seconds, and repeating a combo finisher twice in succession brings a 200% Auto-Block chance.

For these fights, favor intercept windows, track the cycle and vary your combo finishers. Entering Overcharge without a way to handle Unblockable attacks is an avoidable risk. The Steadfast Buff can cover a hit, but do not treat it as permanent protection.

Doctor Doom has 4 different modifiers. Knocking him down has a 50% chance of activating the Aura of Haazareth for 6 seconds. Power Snack immediately nullifies a triggered attacker Buff and gives Doom 25% of a power bar, with a 7-second cooldown. Punishing his Specials gives you a Grit Passive for 10 seconds, paused during your Specials and while your Striker is active. Finishing his Special also starts a 10-second timer if none is running; knock him down to clear it, or suffer Reversed Controls for 6 seconds when it expires.

There is a trade-off here: knockdowns clear the reversal timer but can trigger the Aura. Plan your next safe opening instead of assuming every knockdown is harmless. Buff-heavy attackers also need to account for Power Snack when managing Doom's power.

## Section identities and Hard map modifiers

| Section | Mechanics on both maps |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | If either fighter is cornered, the attacker gains 20% Stifle every 2 seconds for 5 seconds; stacks pause while either remains cornered and expire individually. A Special knockdown grants the defender 5% Inexorable for 6 seconds. |
| 2 | Defender begins with indefinite Brace. Interrupting a Heavy disables it for 16 seconds, paused during attacker Specials/Heavies, unaffected by Ability Accuracy. A Special starts a 15-second timer; using another Special within it causes 8 seconds of Unsteady (attacks Glance). |
| 3 | Each attacker Dodge gives defender an indefinite +20% Attack Fury. At 7, defender gains 5 seconds of Unstoppable; all Fury stacks clear when it ends. Defender Specials grant Stun Immunity for 4 seconds plus 2 per unique defender Buff, paused during defender Specials. |

Every Doombot carries its section identity buffs plus the reversal timer node. Hard map adds 50% Attack and 300% Health to defenders. The mechanics are shared with the normal map, so plan assignments based on node compatibility first and use rank and survivability to decide which members are ready for Hard.

For Section 2, coordinate players comfortable interrupting Heavy Attacks, and space Specials around the timer. Section 3 asks for control of dodges and the defender's Buff count. Ask each member to check their Champion's kit against the route and boss before locking in the assignment; these recommendations are derived from the announced nodes.
