Chasca is an Anemo bow carry whose damage profile depends heavily on the elements of her party. She is available on the Names Forged Beneath Blazing Skies Chronicled Wish until December 15 at 14:59 server time. At C0, party composition is one of the first things to solve before optimizing a weapon.

## How converted shells change the build

During her Elemental Skill, Chasca can load six shells per full Charged Attack. At C0, up to four can convert into eligible teammate elements when the relevant conditions are met. Pyro, Hydro, Electro and Cryo are the elements to consider when assembling the party. An Anemo damage Goblet only benefits a portion of this mixed damage profile.

| Slot | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Set | 4-piece Obsidian Codex |
| Sands | ATK% |
| Goblet | ATK% by default |
| Circlet | CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG |
| Substats | CRIT, ATK%, EM, situational ER |

Upgrade the Skill first because it governs the enhanced attacks, then Burst. Normal Attack levels are low priority for this playstyle. With Codex's active 40% CRIT Rate bonus, 60% displayed CRIT Rate reaches the cap before other buffs.

Marechaussee Hunter is an alternative in a Furina team, but Codex is the straightforward general recommendation. Elemental Goblets can become competitive when the team biases conversion heavily toward a particular element; ATK is more dependable across mixed lineups.

## Bow choices

| Weapon | Practical role |
|---|---|
| Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage | Signature option |
| Aqua Simulacra | Premium alternative if its proximity condition is met |
| Flower-Wreathed Feathers | Charged Attack option, especially with refinements |
| Song of Stillness | Craftable option with healing |
| Chain Breaker | Craftable alternative, check party-dependent passive |

Pick the bow together with the artifact set. An existing CRIT DMG weapon can be easier to balance with Codex than a CRIT Rate weapon that pushes the active total above 100%. Song of Stillness is attractive if the team can trigger its healing condition reliably.

## Choose the three support elements

| Team | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Chasca, Bennett, Furina, Ororon | Mixed Pyro, Hydro and Electro reaction lineup |
| Chasca, Bennett, Furina, Citlali | Pyro, Hydro and Cryo reaction lineup |

Bennett supplies ATK and healing; Furina adds off-field Hydro and damage buffs. Ororon supplies Electro, while Citlali is an alternative for Cryo and shielding. These lineups have different reaction profiles and sustain demands. With Furina, monitor team HP and Fanfare generation instead of assuming Bennett always provides enough healing for maximum buff uptime.

Apply support abilities before entering Chasca's Skill, then hold the attack input to load and release full volleys. Movement during charging helps reposition, but enemy movement and reaction order can still alter results. Use the Burst according to rotation and available energy.

## Evaluate roster fit before pulling

Chasca can exploit a broad range of elemental supports, but that flexibility does not mean every support is equally useful. At C0, filling a slot with a non-converting element can reduce the value of her shell mechanic. If a proposed team needs a specific shield or healer, compare the resulting loss of eligible elemental slots against the practical survivability gain.

This roster check is more useful than choosing a bow solely from a theoretical damage ranking.
