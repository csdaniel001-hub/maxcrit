An Echo with a promising main stat can still consume a substantial part of your weekly upgrade materials before you know whether it improves your build. In Wuthering Waves, the final five levels of a 5-star Echo require more EXP than the first fifteen combined. That cost difference is why a staged upgrade process helps more than blindly leveling every potential replacement to +25.

## What the next tuning slot costs

A 5-star Echo unlocks another substat opportunity every five levels. The following cost comparison uses the leveling table documented by WuWaBuild. WuWa Field Guide lists the same cumulative EXP milestones, and Vortex Gaming gives the same 50-Tuner total for a fully tuned +25 Echo. It assumes every unlocked slot is tuned and excludes any refund.

| Target level | EXP for this step | Total Premium Tuners |
|---|---:|---:|
| +5 | 4,400 | 10 |
| +10 | 12,100 | 20 |
| +15 | 23,100 | 30 |
| +20 | 39,500 | 40 |
| +25 | 63,500 | 50 |

The last step consumes about 44.5% of all EXP needed for a +25 Echo. A different candidate can reveal two substats at +10 for 16,500 EXP and 20 Tuners, compared with 63,500 EXP and 10 Tuners for the final slot on a +20 candidate. The cheaper option depends on which resource you are short of. If Tuners are scarce, revealing lots of early slots also has a cost.

## Set an upgrade threshold for your account

First decide whether this Echo is filling an empty slot or replacing a strong piece. Check the required Sonata set, main stat and the character's actual substat priorities. A piece with enough Energy Regen to make a rotation work can be more valuable than a small CRIT upgrade on a character who otherwise misses a Liberation.

| Current build situation | When to continue |
|---|---|
| Missing a usable piece with the correct main stat | Accept weaker early rolls if the main stat and set complete the build |
| Equipped piece already has useful substats | Continue only if the candidate can realistically beat the existing piece |
| Energy Regen requirement not met | Give useful ER rolls additional weight before judging damage stats |
| Strong established build | Be selective early and reserve expensive final levels for genuine upgrades |

At +10 and +15, compare the revealed substats and remaining slots against what your current piece already provides. A replacement needing three useful rolls with only two slots remaining cannot meet that target. Stop there unless it has a different role or a needed main-stat improvement. For a starter character, however, finishing the main stat may be worthwhile even with weaker substats.

## Recycling and planning the next batch

Recycling a leveled Echo recovers part of its EXP and some of the Tuners already spent. The refund does not make testing candidates free. Check the preview before confirming, especially if your Tuner supply is already low. Repeatedly finishing candidates and discarding them can drain resources even with recycling.

Keep the old equipped Echo locked until the new one is ready. Recycle the clearly failed candidates first, and check other characters' needs before you treat a partially leveled piece as fodder.

Version 3.8 improves the Echo Backpack's Batch Management filters and changes selection behavior when filters are adjusted. Recheck selected items before discarding them. Kuro's new Resonator Ascension Planner also includes a Recommended Lineup section in testing; use it to identify the team you are actually building around before choosing Echo priorities.
