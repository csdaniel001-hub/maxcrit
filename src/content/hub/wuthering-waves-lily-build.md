Lily's specialized buffs pay off most when the carry deals Unison or Echo Skill damage. She is a five-star Spectro Pistols support introduced in Wuthering Waves Version 3.8, and her gearing has two separate priorities: meeting the Energy Regen requirement and picking a Sonata Effect that matches the team's damage type.

Hsin, Sigrika and Galbrena are the carries her team table is built around. Get the ER right first, because her ER conversions set the size of her ER-dependent team bonuses.

## Energy Regen and weapons

Lily's official kit caps its Energy Regen conversions at 260%. Fairytale's Finale, Phantasm's End and Eternal Bloom gain CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG from ER above 100%. Dream Aroma and Silvered Dream scale from ER above 190%; at 190% or below, their ER-dependent team bonuses are zero. Build past 190% first, then work toward 260% to maximize these bonuses. Her ordinary Outro amplification still applies independently.

| Weapon | Reason to consider it |
|---|---|
| Lullaby | Signature, designed to cover Energy Regen and support needs |
| Cadenza | Four-star Energy Regen alternative with Concerto recovery |
| Static Mist | Standard five-star option if already owned; Wutheringlab ranks it below Cadenza for Lily |

Wutheringlab lists Cadenza's level-90 Energy Regen at 51.84%. With base 100% and two 32% ER main-stat Echoes, the subtotal is 215.84%. That leaves 44.16 percentage points before main Echo bonuses. Formrender contributes 10% ER continuously from the main slot, leaving 34.16 points, or about 6.83 per Echo across five pieces. Fallacy of No Return contributes 10% only while its Echo Skill buff is active; without that effect the gap is 44.16 points, about 8.83 per Echo. Actual substat rolls and other bonuses change the gap.

If you already have Cadenza, try the lower-cost route before spending pulls on Lullaby. Assess the energy deficit and how quickly you can pass field time back to the carry. The signature is worth considering when it relieves a real gearing or rotation bottleneck.

## Echo sets by team type

| Setup | General and Echo Skill | Unison |
|---|---|---|
| Five-piece set | Rejuvenating Glow | Flower of Tinged Yearning |
| Main Echo | Fallacy of No Return | Formrender |
| Main stats | HP% or Healing Bonus, ER and HP% | HP% or Healing Bonus, ER and HP% |

Begin with two Energy Regen 3-cost pieces and HP-focused 1-cost pieces. When you approach the target, compare the actual combat stats and available weapon before replacing an ER main stat. The Unison setup depends on teammates who can use Unison Response; Rejuvenating Glow remains the general-purpose choice.

## Rotation and skill investment

Build Lily's Forte resources, then use Fairytale's Finale or Phantasm's End to gain Sillage. Cast Resonance Liberation and use Outro within the six-second Sillage window to grant Silvered Dream, which benefits the active Resonator. Blooming Fragrance also grants 25% All DMG Amplification for 30 seconds. Plan the two teammates' Intro Skills during Cradle Lullaby to accumulate up to four Dream Aroma stacks; each stack grants up to 7% team ATK at 260% ER.

Avoid returning Lily to the field early: swapping her in removes Cradle Lullaby and Dream Aroma, and Eternal Bloom ends Slumberland. Time her return around the end of the 25-second Cradle Lullaby window. Level Forte Circuit first. For extra healing, Wutheringlab puts Resonance Liberation next, while WuWaBuild puts Intro Skill next and Liberation last. The fixed support buff percentages do not increase with skill levels; reach the ER targets through equipment.

If you already own The Shorekeeper, the practical reason to add Lily is to support an appropriate specialized team or free your existing healer for another lineup. Whether to pull depends on which carries you own, so check them against the team table above. See the [Wuthering Waves banner schedule](/hub/banners/wuthering-waves-banners.html) for availability.
