## Event details

### Riftborne Assault: Pink Rathian / Coral Pukei-Pukei

- **Event window**: 2026-11-23 09:00 to 2026-11-29 23:59 (Player local time).

Hunt featured monsters and clear limited quests. Increased material rewards in Hunt-a-thons, Elder Dragon Interceptions and group hunts. Riftborne monsters require field 8-star unlock.

- **Rewards**: Monster materials, Riftscales and style customization progression.

- **Eligibility**: Complete the prologue; 8-star field unlock for Riftborne encounters.

### Carnival countdown login quests

- **Event window**: 2026-11-23 09:00 to 2026-11-29 23:59 (Player local time).

Log in and claim the special quests in the Special Quests tab.

- **Rewards**: Up to six Wyvern Gem Shards.

- **Eligibility**: Complete the prologue.

### Season 11 Wave 3 Exchange Token acquisition

- **Event window**: 2026-11-23 09:00 to 2026-12-15 23:59 (Player local time).

Hunt large monsters and slay or repel elder dragons to earn exchange tokens.

- **Rewards**: Exchange rewards include Wyvern Gem Shards; combined Wave 3 login and exchange campaigns offer up to 35 shards, not 35 from this event alone.

## Before the event ends

Check the event timer on your own server, finish the required activities and claim any unlocked rewards. Dates may change after publication; a campaign window does not guarantee every activity is available every day.
