## Garage planning

The Holiday Pursuit's five lock-ins are the free Aventador, the LB Silhouette Mazda RX-7, the ABT Audi RS7-R "Daniel Abt Edition", the Zenvo Aurora Tur and the Aston Martin DBR22. Skyborne Sprint has the longest list, 14 cars including the free Hellcat. Forged Fury needs the Plymouth Barracuda "TorC" Weaver Customs and the Weaver Customs Chevrolet Camaro 'Bank Roll'. SIX: Brawn lists the Dodge Charger Scat Pack and Buick GNX. Flachbau Legacy lists the Porsche 911 S/T (992) and 911 Turbo 3.3 Cabriolet "Flachbau" (930).

Challenger's Run V feeds into Chapter V: the Charger Tantrum appears in the later event's lock-in list. Their dates overlap, so the first event's completion can matter for the next one. Before spending rare parts, compare your owned garage against the full [official lock-in lists](https://www.csr-racing.com/news/6-9-0-blog-update). A free car is only one part of a multi-car event.
