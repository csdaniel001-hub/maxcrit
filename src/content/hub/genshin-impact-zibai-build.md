Zibai, "White Horse's Fleeting Spring", is a 5-star Geo sword user and an on-field DPS. Her rerun is on the Harmonious Abundance wish until December 15 at 14:59 server time, so this is the window to decide whether she fits your account. She plays very differently from older Geo carries, and building her like one wastes resources.

## How Zibai deals damage

Most of her damage comes from Lunar-Crystallize, the reaction triggered when Geo hits an enemy that already has Hydro on it. Having Zibai in the party turns Hydro Crystallize into Lunar-Crystallize and raises the party's Moonsign level by 1.

Her Skill puts her into Lunar Phase Shift mode, where her Normal and Charged Attacks become Geo damage that other infusions can't override. When the party is at Ascendant Gleam, which takes two Moonsign characters, her fourth Normal Attack adds an extra hit that counts as Lunar-Crystallize damage. That is why her team matters more than her weapon: without Hydro on the enemy and a second Moonsign character, her strongest hits never happen.

Her damage scales mainly with DEF, and her direct Lunar-Crystallize hits also use Elemental Mastery.

## Stats and artifacts

| Slot | Pick |
|---|---|
| Set | 4-piece Night of the Sky's Unveiling |
| Sands | DEF% |
| Goblet | DEF% |
| Circlet | CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG, whichever balances your ratio |
| Substats | CRIT, then DEF%, then Elemental Mastery, then Energy Recharge |

Night of the Sky's Unveiling gives 80 Elemental Mastery from 2 pieces. The 4-piece bonus adds 15% CRIT Rate at Nascent Gleam or 30% at Ascendant Gleam for 4 seconds, whenever a party member triggers a Lunar reaction while she is on the field. It also raises the whole party's Lunar Reaction damage.

Count that 30% before choosing a Circlet. If her CRIT Rate is already high without it, a CRIT DMG Circlet usually gives you more.

The DEF% Goblet beats a Geo DMG Goblet because her key hits are Lunar-Crystallize reaction damage, which scales with DEF.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Why |
|---|---|
| Lightbearing Moonshard (5-star) | Her signature: CRIT DMG, DEF +20%, and Lunar-Crystallize damage +64% for 5 seconds after her Skill |
| Harbinger of Dawn (3-star) | High CRIT for a cheap sword, but only while her HP stays above 90% |

Harbinger of Dawn is only worth it with a shielder such as Zhongli in the team, because its bonus switches off once she takes damage. Lightbearing Moonshard is on the Epitome Invocation wish until December 15.

## Teams

| Team | Notes |
|---|---|
| Zibai, Linnea, Columbina, Illuga | The full Lunar-Crystallize team |
| Zibai, Illuga, Columbina, Gorou | Gorou in place of Linnea |
| Zibai, Illuga, Columbina, Zhongli | Shield for Harbinger of Dawn users |

Columbina applies Hydro from off the field, deals her own Lunar-Crystallize damage and boosts Lunar damage. Illuga is the 4-star Geo support on this banner: he adds damage based on his Elemental Mastery and boosts her Geo and Lunar-Crystallize hits. Linnea shreds Geo RES, raises Lunar-Crystallize damage and heals.

If you're missing pieces, Chiori, Xilonen, Aino, Yelan and Xingqiu are the other options KQM lists for her team. Without Columbina, Aino, Yelan or Xingqiu are the Hydro choices among them.

## Should you pull for her?

Pull for Zibai if you own Columbina or Linnea, or plan to get Linnea on the same banner. If you have neither, budget for a second Moonsign character and a Hydro applier first, because she depends on both. Illuga is on both character banners as a rate-up 4-star, so wishing for Zibai or Linnea is also the best chance at him.
