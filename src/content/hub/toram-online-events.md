## Event details

### 16 Million Downloads Boss Battle: Period III

- **Event window**: 2026-11-22 00:00 to 2026-11-26 before maintenance (Publisher schedule).

Enter the portal at Government Office and defeat a celebration boss. A level 260 battle joins the previous challenges. Stay inside the battlefield to qualify.

- **Rewards**: 40 Orb Shards for a qualifying win in this period; distribution scheduled for December.

- **Eligibility**: Complete an eligible event battle.

### Limited Special Emblems

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 after maintenance to 2026-11-27 05:00 (JST (UTC+9)).

Log in, play for over 60 minutes, and defeat 100 monsters. Claim through Menu > News > You’ve got Emblem; resets at 05:00 JST.

- **Rewards**: 70 Ticket Pieces, 50 Orb Shards and 3 Teleport Tickets per completed daily set.

### Limited crafting and refinement recipes

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 after maintenance to 2026-12-10 before maintenance (Publisher schedule).

Visit the Blacksmith or Synthesist in Sofya City, El Scaro or Hora Diomedea; select Limited-Time recipes.

- **Rewards**: Gold, Silver and Bronze Pom Refine items: success bonuses of 15%, 10%, 5% and degradation prevention chances of 95%, 85%, 75%.

### Potum Hood and celebration gift

- **Event window**: 2026-11-12 (announcement date) to 2026-11-26 before maintenance (Publisher schedule).

Log in; claim via Menu > Community > Mailbox > Present.

- **Rewards**: Potum Hood, consumables including 10 Life Potions, 10 Mana Potions, 15 Revive Droplets, 10 Fairy Sewing Tools and 15 Teleport Tickets.

- **Eligibility**: New players must finish the tutorial.

## Before the event ends

Check the event timer on your own server, finish the required activities and claim any unlocked rewards. Dates may change after publication; a campaign window does not guarantee every activity is available every day.
