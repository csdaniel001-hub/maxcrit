Mitya, Aeon • Aha, Lily, Hedone and Narcissus headline the current versions of the big gacha games, and Sunbringer leads Zenless Zone Zero 3.4's Signal Search channels from December 2.

Each section below covers who is on the banners, what comes next and the free pulls to collect first. All of it comes from the publisher's own version announcement or store release notes, with a link to our full story on that version.

## Genshin Impact 7.2, Hour of Ruin

Version 7.2 has been live since November 4. Mitya is a 5-star Electro Catalyst user built around Superconduct, which he turns into Stellar-Conduct for the whole party. Valeriy is a 4-star Electro Sword user who shields the team and boosts Electro DMG.

| Phase | Event Wishes |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Mitya (new 5-star), Valeriy (new 4-star), Furina (rerun) |
| Phase 2 | Zibai and Linnea (reruns), plus a Chronicled Wish with certain Natlan characters |

Free before you pull: 560 Primogems for finishing the 7.2 Archon Quest in its period, and a 4-star character of your choice from the main event. [Full story on Genshin Impact 7.2]({{ '/news/genshin-impact-7-2-hour-of-ruin-mitya-valeriy.html' | rel }}).

## Honkai: Star Rail 4.7, Now, Lay Laughter to All Stars

Version 4.7 reached the App Store on November 10 with one new character: Aeon • Aha, an Elation character of the Quantum type. The same version adds a Planarcadia Trailblaze Mission and a new Currency Wars season, Voyage Into the Canvas. [Full story on Honkai: Star Rail 4.7]({{ '/news/honkai-star-rail-4-7-aeon-aha-planarcadia-finale.html' | rel }}).

## Wuthering Waves 3.8, Lilies in a Dying Dream

Version 3.8 arrived on November 12 with Lily as the new Resonator and runs its Convenes in two phases.

| Phase | Featured Resonator Convenes | Featured Weapon Convenes |
|---|---|---|
| Phase I | Bloom of Yesterday's Dreamsoil, When the Runes Glitter, Wanderer Knows No Far and Near | Lullaby, Solsworn Ciphers, Emerald Sentence |
| Phase II | Voices on Azure Feathers, Blessings From Dewy Winds, Thousand Futures Mirrored in Snow | Azure Oath, Firstlight's Herald, Frostburn |

Starpath Reverbs II and Tideforge Reverbs II run alongside them. In both you pick a 5-Star Resonator or Weapon as your target and can change it whenever you like, which makes them the safer choice if you are saving for Version 4.0. [Full story on Wuthering Waves 3.8]({{ '/news/wuthering-waves-3-8-lilies-in-a-dying-dream-lily.html' | rel }}).

## Zenless Zone Zero 3.4, The Finality of the Day (from December 2)

| When | Signal Search channel | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| All of Version 3.4 | Sunbringer, with the W-Engine Dragonfire Mirage | S-Rank Agent |
| All of Version 3.4 | Robin (Honkai: Star Rail crossover) | S-Rank Agent |
| Second half of 3.4 | Kafka (crossover) | S-Rank Agent |
| Second half of 3.4 (rerun) | Alice, with the W-Engine Practiced Perfection | S-Rank Agent |

Every player who logs in after the update gets Robin for free, so her channel only matters for duplicates. The real choice is between Sunbringer, available all version, and Kafka in the second half. [Full story on ZZZ 3.4]({{ '/news/zenless-zone-zero-3-4-december-2-honkai-star-rail-crossover.html' | rel }}).

## Reverse: 1999 3.9

Version 3.9 (November 5) adds two 6-Star characters, Hedone (Mineral Afflatus) and Narcissus (Beast Afflatus). You can claim up to 100 summons during the version, 14 of them just for logging in, and Moldir and Noire receive Character Enhancements. [Full story on Reverse: 1999 3.9]({{ '/news/reverse-1999-3-9-hedone-narcissus-summer-gold-sea.html' | rel }}).

## Blue Archive

The Special 3-Star Fest Student Choice Ticket from the November 10 update stays available until December 7, so you can pick a Fest student without pulling for one. [Full story on Blue Archive's Act 2]({{ '/news/blue-archive-act-2-vol-1-flame-and-shadow-fest-choice-ticket.html' | rel }}).
