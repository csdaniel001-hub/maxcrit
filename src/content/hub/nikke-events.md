Sky on the Rock has taken over from the fourth-anniversary story campaign in Goddess of Victory: NIKKE. It brings a new recruit and a seven-day login event, and all three of its events close at the same moment.

Dates below come from the official announcements as of November 24, 2026. Times are listed in UTC+9, as the game gives them.

## Current event schedule

## Event details

### SKY ON THE ROCK

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 after maintenance to 2026-12-03 04:59:59 (UTC+9).
- **What to do**: clear event stages and boss challenges, then spend the currency in the event shop. Memory Films can unlock archived stories.
- **Rewards**: Recruit Vouchers, skill enhancement and development materials.
- **Shop deadline**: 2026-12-03 04:59:59 UTC+9.

The shop closes with the event itself, so leftover currency has no grace period.

### FLIGHT D-DAY

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 after maintenance to 2026-12-03 04:59:59 (UTC+9).
- **What to do**: log in on seven separate days.
- **Rewards**: Recruit Vouchers and development materials.

### Sky Special Recruit

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 after maintenance to 2026-12-03 04:59:59 (UTC+9).
- **What to do**: recruit with eligible Gems or Advanced Recruit Vouchers. Sky has a reported 2% individual rate, with 200 Gold Mileage Tickets for exchange.
- **Rewards**: a chance at SSR Sky. She is not a guaranteed free reward.

## Before the event ends

The login event is the one to start early, since seven separate days leave little room near the end. Then finish the stages, empty the shop and claim any unlocked rewards before the shared timer runs out. Dates can change after publication, and a campaign window does not mean every activity is open every day.
