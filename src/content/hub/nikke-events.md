## Event details

### SKY ON THE ROCK

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 after maintenance to 2026-12-03 04:59:59 (UTC+9).

Clear event stages and boss challenges. Exchange earned currency in the event shop; Memory Films can unlock archived stories.

- **Rewards**: Recruit Vouchers, skill enhancement and development materials.

- **Separate claim deadline**: 2026-12-03 04:59:59 UTC+9 (shop)

### FLIGHT D-DAY

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 after maintenance to 2026-12-03 04:59:59 (UTC+9).

Log in on seven separate days.

- **Rewards**: Recruit Vouchers and development materials.

### Sky Special Recruit

- **Event window**: 2026-11-19 after maintenance to 2026-12-03 04:59:59 (UTC+9).

Recruit with eligible Gems or Advanced Recruit Vouchers. Sky has a reported 2% individual rate, with 200 Gold Mileage Tickets for exchange.

- **Rewards**: Chance to obtain SSR Sky; not a guaranteed free reward.

## Before the event ends

Check the event timer on your own server, finish the required activities and claim any unlocked rewards. Dates may change after publication; a campaign window does not guarantee every activity is available every day.
