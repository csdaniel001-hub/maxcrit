## How to redeem Kingshot rewards

1. Tap your avatar and copy the Player ID.
2. Open the [Kingshot Gift Code Center](https://ks-giftcode.centurygame.com/).
3. Sign in with your Player ID, enter a code and confirm.
4. Claim the message in your in-game mailbox.
5. On Android, the profile Settings menu may also provide Gift Code redemption.

## Why might a code fail?

Check capitalization and whitespace, account eligibility, region and the campaign deadline. Some offers are single-use, have a fixed claim limit or require an updated game client. Never share your password with a third-party code generator.

## Where to find new rewards
