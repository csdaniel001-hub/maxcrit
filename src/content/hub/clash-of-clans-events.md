The dates come from Supercell's official calendar as of November 24, 2026. That calendar gives days without hours, so the countdown on your own server is the one to trust near a deadline.

## Event details

### Snackable Troops

- **Event window**: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-26 (Publisher calendar; hours not given).

Use limited backup troops when attacking; supplies are finite.

- **Rewards**: Temporary troop access.

### November Clan Games

- **Event window**: 2026-11-22 to 2026-11-28 (Publisher calendar; hours not given).

Complete challenges to contribute points to the clan; backup troops accompany the first four days.

- **Rewards**: Clan Games reward tiers; exact reward selection in game.

### Fully Forged Challenge

- **Event window**: 2026-11-24 to 2026-12-01 (Publisher calendar; hours not given).

Play the themed Titan Foundry hero challenge.

- **Rewards**: Challenge rewards.

### November Gold Pass

- **Event window**: 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-30 (Publisher calendar; hours not given).

Progress through the November track; Grand Titan is the featured Grand Warden skin.

- **Rewards**: Track rewards; featured skin subject to pass eligibility.

## Before the season ends

The November Gold Pass runs the whole month and closes on November 30, with Grand Titan as its featured Grand Warden skin. The Fully Forged Challenge is the last to close, on December 1. With no end hours published, aim to finish each event and claim its rewards the day before it closes. Dates may change after publication, and a campaign window does not guarantee every activity is available every day.
