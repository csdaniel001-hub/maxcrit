The number shown on a Honkai: Star Rail Warp screen is only half of your budget. You also need to know whether the last 5-star on that banner type was featured. Two accounts with identical Stellar Jade balances and pity counts may need very different numbers of Warps to secure the same character.

## Calculate the maximum remaining cost

For ordinary Character Event Warps, the hard-pity ceiling is 90 and the base featured roll on an unguaranteed 5-star is 50%. Ordinary Light Cone Event Warps use an 80-Warp ceiling and a 75% featured chance. Losing the featured roll makes the next 5-star in that banner type guaranteed to be featured.

Let p be the pulls since the last 5-star on the relevant banner type. With hard pity H, the maximum remaining cost is H minus p if guaranteed, or twice H minus p if unguaranteed. Each Special Pass corresponds to 160 Stellar Jade if purchased with Jade.

| Target and current state | Warps remaining at worst | Jade equivalent |
|---|---:|---:|
| Character, zero pity, unguaranteed | 180 | 28,800 |
| Character, 60 pity, unguaranteed | 120 | 19,200 |
| Character, 60 pity, guaranteed | 30 | 4,800 |
| Light Cone, 50 pity, unguaranteed | 110 | 17,600 |
| Light Cone, 50 pity, guaranteed | 30 | 4,800 |

These are spending ceilings under the ordinary rules. They do not predict the actual number of pulls or model the chance of reaching the target early. Check the current in-game Details for special or collaboration Warps before using this calculation there.

## How Celestial Invitation changes the character pool

Version 4.7 Character Event Warps include seven custom-selected Celestial Invitation characters in the available pool. If the featured roll fails, your selection affects the possible outcome. The Version 4.3 official rules also describe a special case: when the featured character is eligible and included in your selection, the featured character has a 25% chance on the fallback roll. Under those rules, the combined chance of obtaining that character on an unguaranteed 5-star is 62.5% (50% plus 50% of 25%). Obtaining the featured character resets the guarantee, regardless of which roll produced it.

The 62.5% case only applies when the featured character can be added to your Celestial Invitation selection, which is not true of every featured character. Even in the 62.5% case, reserve up to 180 Warps from zero pity if you cannot afford to miss the target.

## Track the counters separately

In Version 4.7, A Comedy of Stars and Indelible Coterie share guarantee progress, and Brilliant Fixation and Coalesced Truths share a different counter. Progress carries across phases and future Event Warps of the same type. Character progress cannot be applied to a Light Cone Warp, or vice versa. Standard and collaboration Warps also have their own rules.

Pulling for a rerun 4-star can unexpectedly produce a 5-star and consume the pity or guarantee you meant to reserve. If you would regret receiving the featured 5-star on that banner, save the Special Passes instead.

## Create a budget before choosing a banner

Count your existing Special Passes first. Add only the full pulls you can exchange from saved Jade. Then subtract the worst-case requirement for your main target.

For example, an account with 18 Special Passes and 12,000 Jade has 93 pulls available. At 60 character pity, a featured guarantee means the desired character requires at most 30 pulls. The remaining budget is at least 63 pulls if you stop as soon as you obtain that character. If the same account has no guarantee, it is 27 pulls short of the 120-Warp ceiling. A 27-pull shortfall corresponds to 4,320 Jade.

| Budget item | What to include |
|---|---|
| Already available | Saved Special Passes plus whole pulls from Jade |
| Confirmed future income | Unclaimed rewards you can actually finish before the deadline |
| Conditional income | Endgame rewards or tasks you may not complete |
| Reserve | Maximum pulls needed for the next target you refuse to miss |

The 4.7 Journey Highlights screen tracks earned and remaining Jade and Special Pass rewards, making it a useful place to audit potential income. Count unclaimed rewards only once and leave uncertain rewards outside the guaranteed budget. Check the [current event calendar](/hub/events/honkai-star-rail-events.html) and [banner schedule](/hub/banners/honkai-star-rail-banners.html) together, because rewards arriving after a Warp closes cannot pay for that target.

A 5-star received early frees resources. It does not justify continuing to pull automatically. Recalculate after every 5-star and decide whether the next target still fits your reserve.
