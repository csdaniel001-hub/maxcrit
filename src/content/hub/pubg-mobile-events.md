## Event details

### Arctic Chronicles

- **Event window**: 2026-11-11 02:00 to 2027-01-12 20:59 (UTC).

Play Erangel, Livik or Vikendi with the Ancient Beast and themed encounters.

- **Rewards**: In-match supplies and mode rewards.

### S33 Classic and Casual Season

- **Event window**: 2026-11-13 02:00 to 2027-01-14 23:59 (UTC).

Play eligible matches and climb the seasonal progression ladder.

- **Rewards**: Rank-dependent season rewards.

### S33 Ultimate Royale

- **Event window**: 2026-11-23 02:00 to 2027-01-12 23:59 (UTC).

Join the competitive ruleset if eligible.

- **Rewards**: Rank rewards.

### Metro Royale Season

- **Event window**: 2026-11-13 02:00 to 2027-01-12 20:59 (UTC).

Play Metro Royale, including seasonal PvE content.

- **Rewards**: Extraction loot and progression rewards.

### Popularity Annual Gala battle phase

- **Event window**: 2026-11-18 09:00 to 2026-12-20 09:00 (UTC).

Registered entrants compete in the battle phase; registration closed Nov 18. Results scheduled Dec 20 23:59 UTC.

### Crazy Chicken Day campaign

- **Event window**: 2026-11-13 02:00 to 2027-01-11 23:59 (UTC).

Active sessions are Friday through Sunday; Tuesday Nov 24 is outside the recurring session.

## Before the event ends

The Popularity Annual Gala battle phase closes first, on December 20 at 09:00, with results scheduled for 23:59 that day. Everything else runs into the new year: Crazy Chicken Day on January 11, Arctic Chronicles, Ultimate Royale and Metro Royale on January 12, and the Classic and Casual season on January 14. Season rewards depend on rank, so those January dates are the ones to plan a final climb around. Keep in mind that dates can shift after publication and that Crazy Chicken Day only runs on its weekend sessions.
