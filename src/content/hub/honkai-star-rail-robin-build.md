Robin turns a finished round of ally turns into another round of attacks. That one trick is why the original Robin, the Physical Harmony support from Version 2.2, is worth building: her Ultimate pulls the other three forward, every ally attack refills her Energy, and inside Concerto each of those attacks also adds a Physical hit of her own.

## What Robin adds to a team

Robin is at her best when several characters contribute meaningful attacks, and Feixiao teams are the clean example: they cash in on the extra turns at the start of Concerto and on the attacks between those turns. A hypercarry still gets her broad buffs and the team advance, but with fewer separate attacks you have to budget her Energy more carefully. A team that mainly deals Break or Elation damage should check first whether that damage actually benefits from her ATK and CRIT buffs.

At level 10, Pinion's Aria provides 50% DMG Bonus for three of Robin's turns. Tonal Resonance adds 20% CRIT DMG and restores two Energy to Robin per eligible allied attack. Her Impromptu Flourish Trace grants another 25% CRIT DMG specifically to follow-up attacks during Concerto. Follow-up carries collect all three bonuses, which gives them more reason to run Robin than a character who only attacks on their own turn.

At level 10, Concerto grants 200 ATK plus 22.8% of Robin's own ATK. Here is what that looks like from the equipment screen, before external buffs:

| Robin ATK | ATK granted during Concerto |
|---:|---:|
| 3,000 | 884 |
| 3,500 | 998 |
| 4,000 | 1,112 |
| 4,500 | 1,226 |

Adding 500 ATK to Robin contributes 114 ATK to each recipient in this example. That is useful, particularly with two ATK-scaling damage dealers, but it does not justify losing a complete Ultimate in a timed fight. For an ally with 3,000 ATK before the song, the difference between the 3,500 and 4,000 Robin setups is about 2.9% of their ATK after the buff, before other combat effects. The calculation isolates ATK and is not a total damage prediction.

Robin's own additional Physical hit uses fixed CRIT values. Its value scales with the number of eligible attacks instead of the number of visible hits in one animation. Keep that distinction in mind when comparing a multi-hit character against a character who performs separate follow-up attacks. The Ultimate's countdown has fixed 90 SPD, so stacking SPD on Robin does not make Concerto finish sooner.

## Relics, Planar Ornaments and stats

| Slot | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Relics | 2-piece Musketeer of Wild Wheat + 2-piece Prisoner in Deep Confinement; 4-piece Wavestrider Captain if allies' abilities can target Robin twice to trigger it |
| Planar ornaments | Sprightly Vonwacq; Lushaka, the Sunken Seas if Robin is not first in the lineup |
| Body | ATK% |
| Feet | ATK% or SPD to meet your opening turn order |
| Sphere | ATK% |
| Rope | Energy Regeneration Rate |

Start with 120 SPD for Vonwacq. Robin has an opening action advance from her Trace, so speed requirements depend on the rest of the party. Check the opening sequence against especially fast allies before replacing ATK boots. Combine ATK and SPD two-piece sets if that makes the sequence work with fewer sacrifices.

Since her Ultimate's additional damage has fixed CRIT stats, a CRIT body is a poor default investment for a support. ATK improves the buff as well as Robin's additional damage. Energy downtime is the other major constraint, and a higher character-sheet ATK value offers little help during a Concerto window that never arrives.

### How to budget SPD and Energy

The first equipment pass should establish a reliable opener. At 120 SPD, Robin's unmodified action interval is about 83.33 AV. Her 25% opening advance reduces that first interval to approximately 62.5 AV in isolation. A teammate with 160 SPD also has a 62.5 AV interval before any opening effects. That illustrates why an apparently adequate Robin can still be beaten to the first turn by a fast carry. Vonwacq's opening advance provides additional room, but other teammates' advances and encounter-specific effects still need checking.

Start by equipping the Energy rope and your best ATK pieces. Count SPD from existing substats before farming an entirely new set. If you can reach your required opening order on ATK boots, keep them. Use SPD boots if your current pieces cannot deliver that sequence consistently. There is little reason to give up more ATK for SPD once the opening order works.

An Energy rope supplies 19.44% Energy Regeneration Rate at maximum five-star enhancement. An ERR planar gives another five percentage points. In a simplified example, 40 points of eligible base Energy become about 47.78 with the rope alone or 49.78 with both. Flowing Nightglow at its maximum signature ERR stacks can increase this further. The purpose of the comparison is to show how several modest sources can change the number of actions needed; actual recovery depends on the specific Energy effect and its timing.

## Light Cones

| Light Cone | When to use |
|---|---|
| Flowing Nightglow (S1) | Signature with Energy support; particularly useful when her second Concerto is late. |
| But the Battle Isn't Over (S1) | Standard 5-star option when extra Energy is important. |
| For Tomorrow's Journey (S5) | Event cone if owned; offers an ATK bonus. |
| Poised to Bloom (S5) | Useful when two or more characters share a Path. |

For Tomorrow's Journey was originally distributed through an event. If both it and an Energy-focused cone are available, compare the number of ally actions between Ultimates in the same encounter. A second Concerto can outweigh a modest improvement in the first one.

### Choose the cone around the second Ultimate

If your first Concerto is ready but the next one arrives after your carry's burst window, prioritize Energy. If both cones already deliver the same number of useful Ultimates, compare ATK buffs and Robin's additional damage. Test against the same enemy with identical teammates. Note the point where Concerto ends and the number of teammate attacks required to recharge it. An extra Ultimate in one encounter does not imply that the same cone wins every fight.

Poised to Bloom has a composition condition: two characters sharing a Path. A double-Hunt composition satisfies it; a team with four distinct Paths does not. A cone that works in the former can become a weaker choice in the latter. For Tomorrow's Journey is useful when Energy is already handled by the team. If the event cone is absent from your inventory, do not delay building Robin solely to acquire it.

## Teams and practical rotations

Robin fits any team that attacks often: every ally attack restores 2 Energy to her through Tonal Resonance, and during Concerto each attack also adds her own additional Physical hit. The lineups below are split by how strongly the sources back them. Competitive teams appear in two independent guides and in real usage data for the carry. Viable teams are documented, but the carry's guides rank Robin behind other supports or the team sees little use.

### How to play each team

The rotations below are our reading of Robin's kit applied to each lineup and have not been tested in play. They share one rule from Prydwen: cast Concerto right after the last character ahead of Robin has acted, so the 100% team advance isn't wasted. Robin's A2 trace advances her 25% at the start of a fight, so she usually acts first.

- **Follow-up (Feixiao, Topaz)**: open with Robin's Skill for the team DMG bonus. Let Feixiao, Topaz and Aventurine take their natural turns. Cast Concerto as soon as the last of them has acted, then spend the advanced turns on attacks that set off follow-ups, since each follow-up restores Energy to Robin. Recast the Skill when its duration runs out; her A6 trace also gives Energy on every Skill.
- **Aglaea**: open with Robin's Skill, then let Sunday buff Aglaea and Aglaea take her turn. Prydwen syncs a fast Aglaea's turns to Robin's 90 SPD Concerto countdown, so cast Concerto after Sunday and Aglaea have acted and let Aglaea's Joint ATK, which counts as two attacks, feed Robin's Talent. Use Huohuo's Ultimate when Robin is short of Energy for the next Concerto.
- **DoT (Kafka, Black Swan)**: open with Robin's Skill, let Black Swan and Kafka apply their damage over time on their natural turns, then cast Concerto after both have acted so they act again inside the song. Huohuo's Ultimate covers Robin's Energy if Concerto comes back slowly.
- **Yunli**: cast Robin's Skill before Yunli's natural turn, so its 50% DMG bonus covers both that turn and the advanced one. Cast Concerto right after Yunli acts. The advance gives her another action inside the song, and Robin's Talent restores Energy on each of her attacks. Keep Skill Points for that advanced turn.
- **Phainon**: open with Robin's Skill, let Sunday and Cerydra take their turns, then cast Concerto. Mobalytics credits Robin's Ultimate with giving Phainon and the other Action Advance supports more turns, which speeds up his next Ultimate transformation.
- **The Herta and Anaxa**: same opening and Concerto timing as the follow-up team, with Robin's Skill up before The Herta's and Anaxa's turns. Prefer Tribbie if you own her.

## Concerto timing

Open with Pinion's Aria. Let allies spend their natural turns when it is safe to do so, then use Vox Harmonique, Opus Cosmique (160 Energy) to bring the other three characters forward. Robin cannot take ordinary turns during Concerto. Her allies' attacks produce additional Physical damage, so keep their damage windows inside the song.

For a simple timing example, three allies each sitting at 10 action value from their next turn would collectively skip only 30 AV when advanced. If they have just acted and each has 70 AV remaining, the advance skips 210 AV across the three separate gauges. This example sums individual gauge distances; the battle clock does not advance by 210 AV. An urgent enemy turn or cycle boundary can still justify an earlier Ultimate.

## Traces and skill priority

Level her Ultimate first, then Skill, then Talent, and leave Basic ATK for last. This is Prydwen's order, and it follows the kit: the Ultimate carries the ATK buff and the additional hits, and the Skill carries the 50% DMG buff.

| Major Trace | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Coloratura Cadenza (A2) | Advances her action by 25% at the start of battle | 1 |
| Impromptu Flourish (A4) | During Concerto, allies' follow-up attacks deal 25% more CRIT DMG | 2 |
| Sequential Passage (A6) | Her Skill regenerates 5 extra Energy | 3 |

Prydwen ranks the Traces in this order. Unlock all three and make the opening turn order consistent before chasing additional ATK rolls. If auto-battle spends the Ultimate before your damage dealers act, play the opening manually.

## Eidolons and pull priorities

Robin's core function is available at E0. E1 adds 24% All-Type RES PEN during Concerto, improving damage across the squad even when individual carries use different elements. E2 gives allies 16% SPD during the song and increases the Talent's Energy recovery by one per trigger. With ten eligible attacks, that is ten extra base Energy before applicable regeneration modifiers. E4 adds a teamwide crowd-control cleanse at Ultimate activation and increased Effect RES during the song. E6 concentrates substantially more personal damage into a limited number of additional hits per song.

For an account with frequent Energy problems, first improve the relic rope, cone and attack cadence. The signature becomes attractive if that investment creates another useful song. For a player whose Energy is already stable and whose account has a complete second endgame team, E1 is the more natural offensive luxury to evaluate. Neither upgrade should precede an essential missing teammate simply because it appears high on a damage chart.

## Diagnose a disappointing clear

If Robin acts after the carry, fix the opening SPD or planar set. If the song runs out before your planned burst, inspect the countdown and the carry's available resources. If the team stalls immediately after the action advance, reserve SP before pressing Ultimate. If her second song comes too late, examine attack count and Energy recovery before farming more ATK. Finally, if a different support consistently helps your particular damage type more, use Robin in the other team.

Almost everything on that list comes back to timing and Energy. Once the song lands on time and comes back quickly, extra ATK is the finishing touch.
