Summoners War's v9.4.0 update on November 20 brought Summoner Mastery, and how much of your old progress counts depends on the category. This tracker keeps the rules and the other v9.4.0 changes in one place, and will add future versions as they land.

## v9.4.0, November 20, 2026

Mastery uses six rules for crediting records:

| Record type | What counts |
|---|---|
| Immediate | Current progress applies automatically at the update |
| Best record | Your highest grade and ranking to date count retroactively |
| New record | Earlier results don't count; challenge the content again |
| Accumulated | Tracking starts at the update |
| Ongoing season | Progress from the current season continues, plus post-update results (8 wins before + 2 after = 10) |
| Trial of Ascension | Normal and Hard floors must be cleared again; Hell Tower Tokens apply after one post-update Hell play |

Meeting a higher tier also completes the lower ones: a 24-second Giant's Keep best completes the 60, 50, 40, 35 and 25-second requirements. For collection and progression categories, a Collab Monster and its Original Monster count as one Monster. Your score and grade update immediately, while the overall ranking refreshes periodically.

Before repeating a dungeon just for Mastery, check which rule its category uses: a Best record category already counts your old clear, while a New record category doesn't.

## Other v9.4.0 changes

| System | Change |
|---|---|
| Gateway > Teleport | Lists content you can enter during Repeat Battle, with remaining attempts or tickets |
| Monster Collection | Shows where Monsters are obtained: Scenario, Arena Shop, Guild Shop, Ancient Coin Shop, Secret Dungeons, Fusion Hexagram |
| Repeat Reappraisal | Rune Score added as a target setting |
| Dimension Arena | Season info on tap, battle counts next to rewards, Scroll count on the Summon button, [Dimension Arena] label in chat |
| Android | Fixed the Dimension Hole map's distortion effect on some devices |
