Gear Miyabi like most Anomaly characters and you miss the part of her kit that makes her charged attacks reliable. Hoshimi Miyabi is a Frost Anomaly agent who wants CRIT Rate to accelerate Frost buildup, so farming Anomaly Proficiency alone leaves her short. The following setup assumes Mindscape 0.

She also rewards a team that repeatedly generates Disorder. In team terms, you also need a Support agent or Section 6 teammate to switch on Bask in Frost, and every lineup below meets that condition.

## Drive Discs and CRIT targets

| Slot | Recommended starting point |
|---|---|
| Four-piece set | Branch & Blade Song |
| Two-piece set | Woodpecker Electro or Polar Metal |
| Disc 4 | CRIT Rate, or CRIT DMG once CRIT Rate is comfortable |
| Disc 5 | Ice DMG Bonus or ATK% |
| Disc 6 | ATK% as a starting choice |

Branch & Blade Song grants 30% extra CRIT DMG at 115 or more Anomaly Mastery and a conditional 12% CRIT Rate after Freeze or Shatter. Miyabi's buildup conversion caps at 80% CRIT Rate. CRIT Rate above 80% still improves her direct critical hit consistency. Icy Veins recommends getting as close to 100% as practical.

For example, if the stat screen shows 70% CRIT Rate before the set's conditional effect, the active bonus would bring her to 82%. Before that bonus activates, she has only 70%. Choose your Disc 4 around how consistently you maintain the buff, and count it once. A two-piece Woodpecker set helps when CRIT is scarce; Polar Metal is an alternative after the rate is comfortable. Either way, CRIT comes first on this character.

## W-Engines

| W-Engine | Acquisition and role |
|---|---|
| Hailstorm Shrine | Limited signature, directly supports her CRIT-focused damage |
| Fusion Compiler | Standard S-Rank alternative, useful existing investment |
| Electro-Lip Gloss | Battle Pass option; Icy Veins rates S5 close to S1 Fusion Compiler |
| Rainforest Gourmet | Craftable A-Rank, rewards Energy consumption |
| Weeping Gemini | Craftable A-Rank, builds Anomaly Proficiency from team Anomalies |

The signature is an attractive upgrade because other Anomaly engines rarely solve her CRIT stat requirements. If using a craftable engine, compensate with better CRIT discs before pursuing marginal Anomaly stats. Rainforest Gourmet and Weeping Gemini have different buff conditions; don't treat maximum stacks as permanent.

Bask in Frost requires another Support agent or a teammate from Section 6. Each example satisfies the condition. Nangong Yu is an Ether Stun agent whose official kit increases Anomaly Buildup Rate for Anomaly agents and can trigger Polarity Disorder on Stunned enemies; because she is a Stun agent, Bask in Frost comes from the Support slot in her lineups. Ice and Frost have separate Anomaly gauges, so an Ice teammate can contribute to Disorder even though their damage types look closely related. Soukaku also shares Miyabi's faction, whereas Lucy keeps the Additional Ability enabled in the budget lineup. Prydwen prefers Lighter to Piper in its Sons of Calydon setup when available. The Piper lineup remains an accessible Disorder option.

## Rotation: spend the gauge intelligently

At six Fallen Frost points, hold Basic Attack for the full level-three Shimotsuki. At M0, Miyabi starts with three points; an EX Special adds two and her Ultimate adds three. Disorder adds two when the Additional Ability is active, while Frostburn - Break can also add one subject to its cooldown.

The resource ceiling means sequencing matters. At five points, using Ultimate immediately wastes two of its potential points; when practical, spend a full gauge first or fill the last point through Frostburn - Break. At four points, a single EX Special completes the gauge without needing a second EX. These examples describe resource accounting. Adjust the order to enemy movement and buff windows.

Prioritize Core Skill and Basic Attack, then invest in Ultimate and other important damage skills. Use the charged attack during a useful damage window. Don't force a long combo while the enemy is moving away.
