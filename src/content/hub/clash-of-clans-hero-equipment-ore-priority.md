An equipment upgrade pays off every time you use that hero and ability in battle. Spend Ore on the loadout your regular army needs first. A high-level Fireball sitting unused in the Blacksmith contributes nothing to your usual air attacks.

November's Town Hall 19 update also changes the budget available to returning players. Star Bonus rewards now depend on Town Hall, so climbing the Ranked ladder is no longer necessary to access the higher Ore rewards associated with your own Town Hall.

## How much Ore should you save?

The Blacksmith opens at Town Hall 8 and equipment caps depend on its progression. The following costs assume an existing Common or Epic with the standard upgrade schedule, starting at level 1.

| Target | Shiny | Glowy | Starry |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Common 18 | 27,260 | 1,920 | 0 |
| Epic 18 | 27,260 | 1,920 | 110 |
| Epic 27 | 56,060 | 3,720 | 480 |
| Epic 18 to 27 only | 28,800 | 1,800 | 370 |

The final nine Epic levels consume roughly 77% of the Starry Ore needed for the complete upgrade. A stockpile of 370 Starry could instead cover three separate level-1-to-18 Epic upgrades, leaving 40 Starry. That comparison assumes sufficient Shiny and Glowy Ore and unlocked equipment. It illustrates the opportunity cost of maxing a single item early.

## Which equipment should you upgrade first?

Choose a loadout according to the jobs your heroes perform. These are example investment paths; each pairing serves a particular attack plan.

| Hero | Example loadout or investment path | What it adds to your attack |
|---|---|---|
| Grand Warden | Eternal Tome with Healing Tome or Rage Gem | Protection during the main push, followed by recovery or extra damage output |
| Archer Queen | Giant Arrow with Healer Puppet | Long-range opening damage and sustain for air attacks that benefit from a prepared funnel |
| Barbarian King | Earthquake Boots with Spiky Ball | Wall opening and damage across multiple targets |
| Minion Prince | Dark Orb with Metal Pants | Slows targets along its path and provides protection during exposed sections |
| Royal Champion | Electro Boots with Haste Vial | Repeated close-range damage and faster attacks during a dive |
| Dragon Duke | Fire Heart with Flame Blower | Passive sustain and directional damage; test the funnel before investing further |

At lower Town Halls, use available Common alternatives while the second item remains locked. The Warden's Eternal Tome can benefit several troops at once, making it a useful early investment for armies that keep the main force together. For a Queen opening with Giant Arrow, compare its next damage upgrade with the hitpoints of the buildings you intend to remove. A damage increase that crosses that threshold changes the attack plan; smaller increases may be less urgent.

## Make an upgrade decision before spending

1. Pick the army you expect to use for the next several weeks.
2. List the two equipment slots for each hero you deploy. Identify the ability that currently fails to achieve its intended job.
3. Compare the next ability breakpoint and its cost in the Blacksmith. For example, an additional Magic Mirror clone or a longer Eternal Tome window can change the timing of an attack.
4. Upgrade that item until it performs the job reliably in practice attacks. Save further Ore for the other heroes until their loadouts also work.
5. Revisit expensive Epic levels when their additional damage, duration or survivability solves a specific problem.

Fireball is a particularly composition-dependent purchase. It throws at the closest defense, so its value depends on which defenses sit along the Warden's path. A player using a Warden-led Fireball attack has different upgrade priorities from a player using Eternal Tome to protect an air army. Avoid treating one equipment level as a mandatory breakpoint across every Town Hall and base layout.

## How to plan for the Blizzard Axe

Supercell announced Legendary Equipment on November 17. The first item, Blizzard Axe, will be earnable for free through a Crafting Event from Town Hall 8. Its announced level cap is 33, with levels 28–30 gated at TH16 and 31–33 at TH18, alongside Blacksmith requirements.

The announced full upgrade bill is 79,760 Shiny, 5,100 Glowy and 705 Starry. Future Epic releases will also have cheaper Glowy and Starry requirements: 3,550 and 430 respectively at level 27. Older Epics are planned to receive reductions gradually, with no fixed schedule announced.

Keep an Ore reserve if the Axe interests you. Unlocking the item and upgrading it are separate decisions. Continue collecting your Star Bonus, Hero Journey rewards and Clan War Ore in the meantime. Those sources support existing loadouts while you wait for the new item's gameplay reveal.

Follow the [Blizzard Axe announcement](/news/clash-of-clans-legendary-equipment-blizzard-axe.html) and [Town Hall 19 patch tracker](/hub/patch-trackers/clash-of-clans-town-hall-19-patch-notes.html) for changes to the equipment economy.
