Warframe has a publisher-listed permanent promo code for its Carry The Fire with Sprite Glyph. CARRYTHEFIRE is the strongest option in this shortlist because Digital Extremes explicitly describes its availability as permanent. SPECIALEFFECT is also reported as available by a specialist tracker. Some codes may already be deactivated, used up or region-locked.

## Current Warframe codes

| Code | Reward | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CARRYTHEFIRE` | Carry The Fire with Sprite Glyph | Permanent, publisher-listed |
| `SPECIALEFFECT` | Glyph | Reported available |

These offers are for cosmetics, and the listed glyphs do not provide combat power, Platinum or equipment upgrades. There are many creator glyph codes in circulation; this shortlist focuses on charity-related offers.

## How to redeem Warframe promo codes

Open the [official Warframe promo code page](https://www.warframe.com/en/promocode) and sign in to the account you actually play on. Select the appropriate platform if the login process asks for it. Enter the code in the redemption field and submit it. Check the redemption result before closing the page, then look for the cosmetic on your account in the game.

Use the same account on mobile that you intend to receive the cosmetic. Signing into a different account may direct the redemption to a profile you do not use. If you maintain separate platform accounts, resolve any uncertainty over which one you are logged into before claiming the offer.

## Permanent codes and limited promotions

A publisher designation of permanent availability means the CARRYTHEFIRE campaign has no stated end date. It does not imply unlimited repeat claims of the same cosmetic on one account. The tracker gives no expiry date for SPECIALEFFECT. Try it while it is listed as active.

Warframe also runs charity campaigns and in-game item promotions that may use a different acquisition method. An item offered through the Market should not be entered as a promo code just because its announcement appears alongside a code.
