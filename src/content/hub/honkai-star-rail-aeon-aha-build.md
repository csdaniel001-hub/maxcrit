Aeon ★ Aha is a Quantum Elation support whose biggest selling point is what THEY do for an established Elation roster. A Comedy of Stars runs until December 22 at 15:00 server time, while the current Silver Wolf LV.999, Sparxie and Yao Guang reruns end on December 2 at 11:59. That difference in deadlines matters if you still need a teammate.

## Relics and stat priorities

| Slot | Recommended starting point |
|---|---|
| Relics | 4-piece Dreamlit Actor |
| Planar ornaments | 2-piece God's Moment of Joy |
| Body | CRIT Rate; CRIT DMG if already sufficiently balanced |
| Feet | SPD |
| Sphere and rope | HP% or DEF% |
| Substats | SPD first, then CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG |

Aim for 150 SPD. The major trace grants 120% Elation at 125 SPD and 150% at 150 SPD. Further Speed can still affect turn timing, but it no longer increases that conversion bonus. StarGuide and Prydwen both recommend this set combination.

A build with a slightly weaker CRIT ratio that reaches the second Speed threshold is a sensible starting point. Check the actual turn order before replacing an otherwise usable piece. High in-combat CRIT Rate also means that additional CRIT Rate can be wasted, so judge the final value with buffs active and don't chase a fixed sheet number.

## Light Cones and Wishpower

| Light Cone | Investment decision |
|---|---|
| Upon the First Echo of "Aha" | Signature; additional base SPD and team offensive support |
| Mushy Shroomy's Adventures | Available from the Light Cone Manifest in the Forgotten Hall; strong accessible alternative |

The Ultimate costs eight Wishpower. Aha earns one point by using Basic ATK, Skill or Elation Skill, and the designated Emanator contributes another at the start of their turn. Use Skill on your main Elation damage dealer as the default: that ally benefits from the designation, which remains in place until you change it. Build toward Faces of Elation without assuming a fixed number of turns across different teams.

Prioritize the major traces, especially A4 and A6, and level the Talent and Skill early. Avoid spending Trailblaze Power on an Energy Regeneration Rate rope just because most supports use one. Aha's Ultimate has a different resource requirement.

## Teams and pull decisions

| Core to consider | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Silver Wolf LV.999, Yao Guang, Aeon ★ Aha, Pearl | Candidate based on documented carry, support and sustain synergies |
| Silver Wolf LV.999, Sparxie, Aeon ★ Aha, Pearl | Keeps Yao Guang available for another team |
| Evanescia, Yao Guang, Aeon ★ Aha, Pearl | Under Faces of Elation, Evanescia gains extra Energy overflow, 10% DEF Ignore and higher Ultimate multipliers from accumulated Certified Banger; Yao Guang covers Aha Instants and Certified Banger |

Pearl and Yao Guang can accelerate the opening Wishpower buildup. Aha can be less immediately useful to an account without a developed Elation team, even if the character is individually strong. The second lineup also depends on the remaining roster and should not be read as a universal upgrade over Yao Guang.

Aventurine • Waveflair needs a different roster. His solo-Elation package permits Aha as the only other Elation character, so adding Pearl or Yao Guang disables that configuration. Consider Waveflair, Aha, Mortenax Blade and Hyacine instead. Treat it as its own team archetype. It gives no reason to drop Pearl from a Silver Wolf LV.999 or Evanescia team.

If you already own a functioning core, Aha at E0 with the shop Light Cone is a practical first investment. Otherwise, consider securing the missing Phase I teammate before December 2 instead of using all available Warps on Aha's signature. The [Star Rail banner schedule](/hub/banners/honkai-star-rail-banners.html) is the starting point for checking availability.
