Phase II of Genshin Impact 7.2 runs three event wishes at once, all from November 24, 18:00 to December 15, 14:59 server time. That is a lot of 5-star choice for one set of Intertwined Fates, so this guide sorts the options by what they add to an account, not by raw strength.

## What's on the banners

| Wish | 5-star | 4-star rate-ups |
|---|---|---|
| Harmonious Abundance | Zibai (Geo, Sword) | Illuga (Geo), Aino (Hydro), Ningguang (Geo) |
| Ya-hoho! Compendium | Linnea (Geo, Bow) | Illuga, Aino, Ningguang |
| Epitome Invocation | Lightbearing Moonshard (Sword), Golden Frostbound Oath (Bow) | Portable Power Saw, Range Gauge and three standard 4-star weapons |
| Names Forged Beneath Blazing Skies (Chronicled Wish) | Varesa (Electro), Chasca (Anemo), Xilonen (Geo), Mualani (Hydro), Kinich (Dendro) and their five weapons | Ifa, Iansan, Ororon, Kachina |

Zibai's and Linnea's wishes share pity with each other. The Chronicled Wish has its own pity, which it shares only with the Lightrace Wish. On your server, the banners close on December 15 at 06:59 UTC in Asia, 13:59 UTC in Europe and 19:59 UTC in America.

## Zibai and Linnea are a package

Zibai is an on-field Geo DPS whose damage comes from Lunar-Crystallize, the reaction Geo triggers on an enemy with Hydro on it. She builds DEF rather than ATK, and her strongest hits need the party's Moonsign at Ascendant Gleam, which takes two Moonsign characters. Linnea is a Geo support built for exactly that: she shreds Geo RES, raises Lunar-Crystallize damage and heals.

So the decision is about the team. If you already own Zibai, Linnea is the upgrade that makes her team work. If you own neither, pulling Zibai alone means you also need a Hydro applier and a second Moonsign character. Aino, the Hydro 4-star on this banner, is one option for the Hydro side. If you don't want to build a Lunar-Crystallize team at all, skip both.

## The Chronicled Wish: one pick from five

| Character | Role | Pull if | Skip if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xilonen | Geo support, off-field | Your main teams use Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo | Your carries are Anemo, Dendro or Physical |
| Varesa | Electro on-field DPS | You like plunge-based play and have Iansan or Chevreuse | You want a low-effort rotation |
| Mualani | Hydro on-field DPS | You have a Pyro applier such as Xiangling or Mavuika for Vaporize | You have no off-field Pyro |
| Chasca | Anemo bow on-field DPS | You have two or three Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo teammates | Your bench is mostly Geo, Dendro or Anemo |
| Kinich | Dendro on-field DPS | You can keep Burning (Emilie) or Burgeon going | You can't support either reaction |

Xilonen is the easiest recommendation. Her Skill creates three Source Samplers, and each Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo teammate turns one of them into that element, shredding enemy RES for it by up to 36% at C0. Her Burst also heals the active character once at least two Samplers have been converted. That makes her useful across many existing teams instead of one.

The four DPS options each need a specific team around them. Pick the one whose supporting characters you already own.

## How the Chronicled Wish guarantee works

- **Pick a target first.** Choose your designated 5-star with Chart a Course before wishing. A character target means every 5-star you can get is a character.
- **90-wish pity.** You get a 5-star within 90 wishes, with a 50% chance it's your target.
- **One Fate Point.** Missing gives you 1 Fate Point, and your next 5-star is then guaranteed to be the target.
- **Worst case: 180 wishes**, or 28,800 Primogems from zero.
- **Pity carries over to the next Chronicled Wish, but Fate Points don't.** They reset to 0 when this wish ends on December 15, and changing your target also resets them.

That last point matters most. If you lose the 50/50 and can't reach the next 5-star before December 15, the guarantee is gone. Only start the Chronicled Wish if you can afford the worst case, or are fine walking away with whichever 5-star you get first.

## Characters or weapons?

Get the character working with a decent weapon before you spend on a signature. Xilonen, for example, can run Favonius Sword or Flute of Ezpitzal, and Mualani can use The Widsith. A missing teammate holds a DPS back far more than a missing signature weapon.
