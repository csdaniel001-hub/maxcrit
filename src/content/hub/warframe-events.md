Warframe's November deals and freebies all close around November 30, but at different times. The Black Friday store sale and the TennoGen discount end at 16:00 UTC that day, while the Movember promotion is listed as running until November 30 with no time given.

## Current calendar

| Activity | Start | End | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black Friday sale | Nov 16, 16:00 UTC | Nov 30, 16:00 UTC | Same times on Apple, Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, Steam and the PC Launcher |
| Featured TennoGen items | Nov 16, 11 a.m. ET | Nov 30, 11 a.m. ET (16:00 UTC) | 50% off across platforms |
| Movember | Running now | November 30 | Free cosmetics with code MOVEMBER2026 |

## Free Movember cosmetics

Enter MOVEMBER2026 on the [official promo code page](https://www.warframe.com/en/promocode) for a Movember Sirius and Orion Community Display by the artist VEROZ, plus three moustache display frames: Shopkeep, Tusker and Master. Digital Extremes gives November 30 as the end date without an hour, so redeem it before then.

## Black Friday discounts

| Bundle | Discount |
|---|---:|
| Starter Weapon Pack | 50% |
| Eat & Run Collection | 30% |
| Constellations Complete Collection | 20% |
| The Old Peace Complete Collection | 40% |

Four Premium Bundles leave for good after the sale: The Old Peace Complete Collection, The Old Peace Uriel Bundle, The Old Peace Gemini Collection and The Shadowgrapher Bundle. If you want any of them, this is the last chance.

The featured TennoGen items are the Armalyst Syandana, the Protea Kollapsar Skin and the Gauss Nitrolyst Skin.
