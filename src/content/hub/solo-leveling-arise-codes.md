## How to redeem Solo Leveling:Arise codes

1. Open the game menu with the four-square icon.
2. Select Settings and open Account Settings.
3. Choose Redeem Code if available and enter the coupon.
4. Select Use and check your Mailbox.

### Redeem online

1. In Account Settings, copy your Member code.
2. Open [Netmarble’s coupon portal](https://coupon.netmarble.com/sololv).
3. Enter your Member code and coupon, submit, then collect the mail.

## Why is my code not working?

Look first for a typo, a missing capital or an extra space. Codes like HUNT3RGU1DE mix numbers and letters, so read them carefully. A clean entry that still fails may have expired, hit its cap, been used already or been issued for another server. Use only the in-game menu or Netmarble's coupon portal.
