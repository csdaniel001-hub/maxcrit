Schemacraft moves the important decisions in Limbus Company out of the fight. Combat Progression is automated, so instead of picking every Skill you draft a team, set Deployment Orders and Defense Strategy, decide when to spend E.G.O, and choose a route and rewards between battles. The beta arrives with the November 26 update and lets you field Identities you don't own, with lower upgrade caps than the ones you do.

Project Moon calls this a playable beta that will keep changing after launch. This guide follows the rules in its November 24 notice and will be updated as the mode changes.

## How to unlock Schemacraft

Clear all stages of Chapter 9.5 "Mnestic Experience" and Chapter 10 "The Gaze Bearing". The Schemacraft button on the Drive tab then becomes active. Runs cost no Enkephalin or Enkephalin Modules.

Schemacraft has its own pass. It does not raise Limbus Pass levels or EXP, and nothing you do in it counts toward Limbus Pass Missions.

## Manual Team or Randomized Team

Your Starting Team holds up to 4 Sinners. Each Identity has a Cost based on its rarity and release date.

| Starting Team | Cost budget | Pass EXP |
|---|---|---|
| Manual Team | 6 | Normal |
| Randomized Team | 10 | +40% |

A Randomized Team fills itself automatically within its 10 Cost budget. You can use Reshuffle Randomized Team up to 3 times between daily resets; some milestones in a run recharge it, but never above 3.

Pick a Manual Team when you want a specific lineup or want to learn how one plays. For pass progress the Randomized Team is the stronger start on paper: more Cost and 40% more EXP. The bonus only pays off if the team survives far enough to earn EXP, so 3 Reshuffles a day are worth spending until the roll gives you a team that can work together.

## Owned and unowned Identities

You can draft Identities and E.G.O you haven't obtained, but they upgrade less.

| | Owned on your account | Not owned |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Up to Uptie IV in the mode | Up to Uptie III |
| E.G.O | Your current Threadspin Tier | Threadspin Tier I |

Collaboration-limited E.G.O you haven't obtained won't appear as rewards. When two Identities fill the same role, check whether the one you want relies on something only Uptie IV unlocks. An unowned copy can't reach it.

## Dice rolls and Schema Deformation

You move between nodes, called Representations, by dice roll. Each roll gives 1 to 3, which sets the most nodes you can travel. After the 5th roll in a Schema zone, Schema Deformation applies and strengthens the enemies.

Every roll past 5 makes the remaining fights harder, so a long detour has to be worth it. Elite Encounters give better rewards than regular ones, but their enemies are empowered too.

## What you control in combat

Before an Encounter, every Sinner needs a Deployment Order, and you can't start without one. Defense Strategy sets how each Identity defends, and the Briefing shows the E.G.O resources your team needs next to what your Identities can generate.

Once the fight starts, turns run automatically in Speed order, and faster units may act more than once per turn. Defense Skills follow each Identity's settings. You start with 4 Identities on the field, which grows to 8 fielded plus 4 Backup Units over the run.

SP carries over between Encounters. Defeated Sinners come back for the next Encounter at 0 SP. Backup Units get bonus SP when they come in. Identities that only use Minus Coins get negative SP modifiers instead, and Identities whose Coin types change with SP get matching modifiers.

E.G.O is your direct intervention:

- **Tap** the icon to queue it with automatic targeting, and tap again to cancel.
- **Drag** it onto an enemy to choose the target.
- **Drag down** to Overclock.

Each battle allows up to 2 retries, and forfeiting a stage uses one. Losing or forfeiting with no retries left ends the run. A run also ends when you clear the final boss of Schema 5 or choose Forfeit Schemacraft Run in the options menu. Pause and Exit lets you continue later or restart with a new team.

## Plan your team around E.G.O resources

E.G.O resources do not carry over between battles, unlike SP. Each battle you start with half the number of Attack Skills of each Sin Affinity across your whole team, rounded down, and Backup Units count. Five Gloom Attack Skills across the team give 2 starting Gloom resources; six give 3.

That makes Sin Affinity spread part of drafting. An E.G.O whose cost your team's Affinities barely cover may not be usable when you need it, and the Briefing is where that shows. Sapling of Light abilities can't be used in Schemacraft.

## Reward Packs, Slivers and other nodes

Regular Encounters offer Reward Packs themed around a Sinner, faction or keyword, and each pack guarantees at least 1 matching Identity. A Reshuffle can swap 1 Reward Pack for another from the pool, or refresh a pack's Identities and E.G.O once.

A duplicate Identity raises its Uptie by 1 or to the reward's tier, whichever is higher. An Identity you don't want can become 1 to 3 Slivers for that Sinner, or Universal Slivers if the Sinner is already at the cap.

| Node | What it offers |
|---|---|
| Elite Reward | Replace Identity, Recruit Identity, or Upgrade (3 Slivers) |
| Boss reward | 1 of 2 options (Reshuffles, Uptie increases or Universal Slivers), each with an added Sinner Pack or Random Pack; options vary by Schema |
| Temptation Representation | Specific Identities and E.G.O for 0 to 2 of your remaining dice rolls; without enough rolls, Deformation applies instead |
| Event Representation | Benefits paired with drawbacks that strengthen enemies |
| Respite Representation | 1 of 3: Reshuffles +, Uptie Tier + for 1 Identity, or Bonus Rolls + (carry over to the next Schema) |

Respite and Universal Sliver upgrades stop at Uptie III in Schemas 1 to 3 and allow Uptie IV in Schemas 4 and 5. Uptie Tier + can still lift an Identity to Uptie III early on, but the step to Uptie IV for owned Identities waits for Schemas 4 and 5. Temptation picks cost the rolls that keep you clear of Deformation, so take one when it fills a real gap in the team.

## Schemacraft Pass and the weekly cap

Pass EXP is capped at 1,000 per week, resetting every Thursday at 06:00 KST (Wednesday 21:00 UTC). Higher zones and harder Encounters give more EXP, Randomized Teams add 40%, and EXP stops once you reach the highest Pass level unlocked so far.

Pass Level 10 gives the Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange] (Schemacraft Beta). It picks one Announcer from those in Yisang Exchange on the Extraction menu that were released up to the end of Season 8. Walpurgis Night Announcers are excluded.

## First-run checklist

- Roll a Randomized Team and use the 3 daily Reshuffles until the team shares Sin Affinities.
- Compare E.G.O costs with your team's Affinities in the Briefing before each battle.
- Set Deployment Orders and Defense Strategy before every hard Encounter.
- Stay within 5 rolls per Schema zone unless a detour gives something the team lacks.
- Save Uptie IV upgrades for Schemas 4 and 5, where they're allowed.
