Your first visit to Summoner Mastery after updating to v9.4.0 may leave you with a surprisingly uneven score. A veteran account can have strong dungeon records and a large monster collection while still showing unfinished Trial of Ascension milestones. Before rebuilding teams or spending Mana Stones on runes, check which achievements actually need another run.

The most useful approach is to separate permanent progression from temporary events. You can work on dungeon speed records next month; a missed seasonal battle deadline is a different problem.

## How Summoner Mastery works

Go to Summoner's Tower, select Profile and open Mastery. Com2uS introduced ten scoring categories, with your own score updating immediately and ranking information refreshed periodically. Achievement records persist after completion, including rune records after the qualifying rune is sold. Collaboration monsters and their corresponding original monsters share collection credit. Our [v9.4.0 update story](/news/summoners-war-v9-4-0-summoner-mastery-siege-season-23.html) covers the rest of the patch.

Use the score as an account audit. A low category can tell you where you have left progression unfinished, but a high category does not necessarily mean your current PvP or farming teams are well built. Check the specific requirements before committing resources.

## Which Mastery records count retroactively?

| Record | What to do after updating |
|---|---|
| Best dungeon clear times | Check whether your historical best already satisfies the milestone. |
| Trial of Ascension Normal and Hard | Clear the required floors again. |
| Trial of Ascension Hell | Enter and play Hell once to register previously earned Tower Tokens. |
| Eligible ongoing seasonal records | Play the relevant content at least once to register carried-over progress. |
| Post-update-only objectives | Complete the objective after installing the update. |

One rule makes the catch-up process easier: completing a higher milestone also satisfies the lower ones. If your account can clear Normal 100F, there is no need to stop at the intermediate Mastery targets simply to collect credit. The same principle applies to nested dungeon time thresholds.

For example, if you are trying to improve your Giant's Keep record, start by comparing your current best with the next uncompleted threshold. Changing your entire team for an improvement that still misses that threshold has limited immediate value for Mastery. Build toward a repeatable farming team first, then test changes separately when you have the runes to support them.

## Best progression priorities for returning players

1. Audit the Mastery screen and identify milestones that your existing roster can already handle.
2. Revisit unfinished Normal and Hard ToA floors during your current reset cycle. Start Hell if its historical tokens have yet to register.
3. Re-enter eligible ongoing seasonal content before deciding that older results have been lost.
4. Reserve rune upgrades, skill-ups and monster development for objectives that also improve an actual farming or PvP team.

Players who are still developing a reliable dungeon team should avoid sacrificing farming consistency for a single faster Mastery record. A stable team is a better foundation for future rune upgrades. Endgame players with established farming teams can focus more selectively on individual speed thresholds or collection gaps.

## What to finish before the December reset

The November events overlap with the new account progression system. Use the following deadlines to plan your sessions.

| Content | Deadline or start | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Dimension Arena Beta Season 1 | Ends Nov 30, 6:59 AM PST | Review remaining participation rewards and your seasonal progress. |
| Siege Battle Season 23 | Starts Nov 30, 11 AM server time | Coordinate guild preparation and defense availability. |
| SWC Special Battle | Ends Dec 6, 6:59 AM PST | Play before the battle window closes. |
| SWC Special Battle Pass sale | Ends Dec 13, 6:59 AM PST | Check your earned rewards and the in-game purchase terms before spending. |

The SWC event provides World Finals support monsters with their equipment already assigned, in a single pre-ban format. It offers a useful opportunity to observe unfamiliar drafts without first investing in equivalent monsters on your own account. Use the opponent's opening choices to identify their intended strategy. A tournament lineup is only useful if you understand its draft matchups.

For players in Central Europe, the SWC battle cutoff converts to December 6 at 3:59 PM CET. Siege uses the game's server clock, so do not automatically apply the SWC conversion to the guild schedule.

## Keep Repeat Battle running while you catch up

The new Gateway > Teleport menu allows you to enter eligible other modes while Repeat Battle continues. Use it to check available attempts before choosing a second activity. Certain event dungeons only appear when active.

If you have a reliable farming team, this is a practical way to fit Mastery catch-up and time-limited content into the same session. Check your remaining event attempts first, then work on the permanent objectives you identified in your initial audit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does selling a rune remove a Mastery achievement?

No. Once the achievement has registered, selling that rune does not erase the record. Keep runes that your actual teams still need.

### Do I have to clear every lower ToA milestone individually?

No. A qualifying higher floor also grants credit for the lower thresholds. Normal and Hard still require fresh clears after the update.

### Is the SWC Special Battle Pass sale deadline the same as the event deadline?

No. The battle period ends December 6, a week before the announced sale cutoff. Check the event and reward screens before buying the pass after the battle period closes.
