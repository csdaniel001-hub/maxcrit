The Free Fire World Series Global Finals 2026 reach their last two days at BITEC LIVE in Bangkok: Last Chance on November 27 and the Grand Finals on November 28. Running alongside is Storm Arena, an in-game campaign with free cosmetics in Battle Royale and Clash Squad.

## FFWS Global Finals schedule

| Stage | Dates | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Knockout Stage 1 | November 6 to 15 | All 24 teams play round robin; the bottom 4 are eliminated |
| Knockout Stage 2 | November 19 to 22 | 12 teams play each day; 2 advance daily |
| Last Chance | November 27 | 12 teams compete for the last four Grand Finals spots |
| Grand Finals | November 28 | Champion Rush, with no match limit |

This is the first Global Finals with 24 teams instead of 18, playing for a USD 1.2 million prize pool. Last year's champion was Thailand's BRU, at the 2025 finals in Jakarta.

The 24 slots went to Southeast Asia (8, covering Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam and Malaysia), India and Brazil (3 each), Bangladesh and Latin America (2 each), and Nepal, Pakistan, the United States, MENA and Africa (1 each), plus 1 for the Esports World Cup champion.

The finals stream on YouTube, Facebook, Kick and TikTok, with commentary in English and local languages. Start times for your region are posted on the official Free Fire Esports channels.

## Storm Arena rewards

Storm Arena started on November 6, and Garena's campaign art gives the event period as November 6 to 29. Its free rewards include an MP5 gun skin, an emote and a Monster Truck vehicle skin, and FFWS-themed events add jerseys and avatars for all 24 teams. The Champion Gloo Wall and Backpack go live through the FFWS Web Event after the Grand Finals.

Storm Reputation, earned through strong match performance, upgrades your Player Card through five stages: First Time, Big Time, Show Time, Peak Time and Prime Time. Each upgrade adds animations, celebration poses and caster voice lines, and cards can be customised with team badges, poses and decorations.

The campaign also brings the Cosmic Overlord Bundle, a storm-themed take on the Dark Matter Bundle with male and female outfits.
