Brant combines Fusion damage, healing, shielding and a useful Outro buff for teammates. His central attack is Returned from Ashes, a Forte Circuit strike treated as Basic Attack damage. He needs a very different stat budget from a conventional Sword DPS.

## How Bravo turns into damage and sustain

Brant has an unusual division of labour. In a Fusion team he may occupy the slot normally reserved for a dedicated sustain, while still contributing a large attack of his own. His damage and healing should be judged across the full team rotation. A higher personal damage build is less valuable if your next Resonator enters without an Outro or is forced to dodge because the shield was never produced.

Ocean Odyssey stores up to 100 Bravo. His normal attacks, Intro and ordinary Resonance Skill can contribute to the gauge. Healing occurs at quarter-gauge milestones, so the team benefits before the full bar is reached. At 100 Bravo the skill changes to Returned from Ashes. The anchor attack consumes Bravo and supplies a shield. Although it replaces a Resonance Skill button, its damage belongs to the Basic Attack category. This is the reason to favour Basic Attack DMG rolls and weapon effects over blindly stacking Skill DMG on Brant.

To the Horizon, his Liberation, heals and enters Aflame. During that state, normal attacks and Anchors Aweigh! build Bravo at twice their normal efficiency, while the ATK conversion increases. Returned from Ashes ends the state after the attack. Prepare the gauge, land the anchor and then transition into the next teammate instead of spending the empowered state on long periods of movement or empty attacks.

## Energy Regen breakpoints

Brant's base Forte conversion adds 12 ATK for every percentage point of Energy Regen above 150%, capped at 280% total Energy Regen. His Liberation changes this conversion while its enhanced state is active, so the following table describes the base conversion only.

| Total Energy Regen | Extra ATK from base conversion | Tidebreaking Courage condition |
|---|---:|---|
| 200% | 600 | Below threshold |
| 240% | 1,080 | Below threshold |
| 250% | 1,200 | Reached |
| 270% | 1,440 | Reached |
| 280% | 1,560 | Reached |

The first farming milestone is 250% when equipping Tidebreaking Courage. The set's large conditional damage bonus makes the difference between 249% and 250% more consequential than an ordinary one-point substat upgrade. After that, balance Energy Regen and CRIT rolls as you approach the 280% conversion cap. These numbers use the total character stat, including the base 100%.

### Aflame changes the calculation

While Aflame is active, the conversion becomes 20 ATK per point of ER above 150%, still stopping at 280% ER. The two conversion effects replace one another.

| Total ER | Normal additional ATK | Aflame additional ATK |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 225% | 900 | 1,500 |
| 250% | 1,200 | 2,000 |
| 265% | 1,380 | 2,300 |
| 280% | 1,560 | 2,600 |

Between 250% and 280%, you can gain another 600 ATK during Aflame. The extra thirty points of ER are therefore real damage investment, but the 250% set condition is already satisfied. Beyond 280%, the conversion gives no further ATK. Extra ER can still improve resource recovery, depending on your rotation. Replace excessive ER rolls with useful CRIT or damage bonuses when Liberation already recharges on schedule.

## Weapons and Echoes

| Sword | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Unflickering Valor (R1) | Tailored signature; simplifies the ER target and supports Basic Attack damage. |
| Bloodpact's Pledge | Free five-star Sword earned through Chapter II Act IV; use for its base ATK and ER, since its Rover-specific passive does not benefit Brant. |
| Emerald of Genesis (R1) | Baseline S0 option on Molten Rift when ER is short. |
| Red Spring (R1) | Reuse for its Basic Attack DMG passive if owned. Check that your Echoes still reach 250% ER with it before running Tidebreaking Courage. |
| Blazing Brilliance (R1) | Limited five-star weapon that genshin-builds lists as an alternative to the signature. Check that your ER total still reaches your set threshold after equipping it. |
| Overture | Budget ER option while gearing; assess its lower personal damage before committing resources. |

Use Tidebreaking Courage with Dragon of Dirge as the main Echo when the threshold is met. Molten Rift with Nightmare: Inferno Rider is a fallback for builds that cannot reach it. A more support-focused option is Moonlit Clouds with Impermanence Heron, sacrificing some personal damage for team utility.

| Echo cost | Main stat |
|---|---|
| 4 | CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG |
| 3 | Energy Regen |
| 3 | Energy Regen, or Fusion DMG if the ER budget still works |
| 1 + 1 | ATK% on each |

Keep both ER three-cost Echoes while building without the signature. Before swapping one for Fusion DMG, remove it in the equipment screen and calculate whether the replacement preserves your threshold. Even at high ER, check that his Liberation is available each rotation.

### Farm Echoes with an ER budget

Make two loadouts in the equipment screen. In the first, use double ER three-cost Echoes and reach at least 250%. In the second, substitute a Fusion main-stat piece only if the complete build remains above the threshold. Compare the actual character sheet, including the weapon and two-piece bonus. A single three-cost ER main stat is worth a substantial amount of the budget; several excellent CRIT rolls on the replacement cannot recreate a missing set activation.

At 250% total ER, a hypothetical extra 10-point roll adds 200 ATK during Aflame. At 278% ER, the same roll only adds 40 ATK through the conversion because the cap is reached after two points. That makes a CRIT substat much more attractive in the latter situation. Use this approach to decide which piece to tune instead of requiring identical substats on all five Echoes.

Brant's signature helps solve two different problems at once: meeting the ER budget and improving the Basic Attack damage that matters for the anchor. With Bloodpact's Pledge you retain a useful ER stat without paying for a limited sword, even though the Rover-specific passive is unusable. Emerald of Genesis offers another alternative when the ER set is not practical. It can be a sensible reuse of existing gear, especially if your Echoes already carry strong ER substats. Overture can establish a working rotation while you save resources for a five-star sword; the lower offensive ceiling matters more when Brant is the main damage dealer.

For a practical tuning target, start with enough ER for the set and a dependable Liberation. Aim for balanced CRIT stats using the four-cost Echo and substats, accounting for weapon and sequence bonuses. Avoid chasing an exact endgame CRIT pair at the expense of consistent anchor attacks. As an illustration that uses the displayed CRIT DMG as the multiplier, a character at 70% CRIT Rate and 230% CRIT DMG has an average critical multiplier of 0.3 + 0.7 × 2.3 = 1.91, excluding all other effects. At 80% and 210%, the corresponding factor is 1.88. The comparison shows why the two stats need to be considered together.

## Teams and rotation order

| Team | Role allocation |
|---|---|
| Lupa, Brant, Changli | Mono-Fusion; Brant supplies sustain and an anchor burst. |
| Lupa, Brant, Galbrena | Fusion core with Galbrena; prioritize her damage window. |
| Lupa, Brant, Encore | Fusion alternative built around Encore's field time. |
| Shorekeeper, Lupa, Brant | Brant as main damage dealer, with a dedicated healer. |
| Shorekeeper, Brant, Changli | Quickswap pair with dedicated healing. |
| Shorekeeper, Brant, Carlotta | Brant as sub-DPS; Carlotta receives his Skill DMG amplification. |

The Course is Set! grants the incoming Resonator 20% Fusion and 25% Resonance Skill DMG amplification for 14 seconds, ending early if they leave the field. Route the handoff accordingly. Lupa's Outro timing also matters: Prydwen's Lupa guide recommends buffing Brant's anchor attacks or Changli's closing Forte sequence, while Galbrena and Encore benefit from a full buffed rotation. The plans below are simplified from these mechanics and have not been tested frame by frame in the game.

For the Lupa/Brant/Changli core, prepare Brant's Bravo through short field windows, then use Lupa's buff sequence and hand off to Brant for the prepared anchor. Finish Brant's Concerto and pass his Outro to Changli for her closing Forte and Liberation sequence. Keep Changli on the field through the intended burst; avoid losing Brant's outgoing buff to an unnecessary swap. Rebuild the gauges on the next loop.

With Galbrena or Encore, use Brant's healing and anchor before preparing Lupa's Outro, then pass that Outro into the main damage dealer's complete rotation. This is a conservative route that prioritizes Lupa's coverage; do not force two mutually exclusive Outro handoffs onto one attack window.

For Shorekeeper/Lupa/Brant, establish Shorekeeper's support, cycle Lupa's buffs, and hand off into a prepared Brant anchor window. Brant has more field time in this lineup, so prioritize his ER and CRIT budget. In the Shorekeeper/Brant/Changli team, establish the sustain setup, prepare Brant's anchor and hand off to Changli through his Outro; add quickswaps only after the basic loop is reliable.

For Carlotta, begin with Shorekeeper, then build Brant's Bravo and Concerto, land Returned from Ashes and use his Outro into Carlotta. Carlotta is a Glacio Resonator, so the 25% Resonance Skill DMG amplification is the part of his Outro that applies to her. Moonlit Clouds is an option when Brant is primarily supporting her.

## Rotation and Forte skill priority

Start with Intro Skill, use Resonance Skill if required for equipment activation, then cast Resonance Liberation. Build Bravo with Mid-Air Attacks, using their follow-ups until the Forte gauge is ready. Land Returned from Ashes and switch using the Outro after reaching full Concerto. Dragon of Dirge's active animation can be placed before the main damage sequence or swap-cancelled where practical.

Level Forte Circuit first, then Resonance Liberation and Basic Attack. Intro Skill and the ordinary Resonance Skill come last. If you keep missing the anchor attack, improve execution before farming more CRIT. A build that consistently finishes its Forte and Outro sequence can deliver more practical value than one with better stats and unfinished rotations.

## Resonance Chain and weapon investment

At S0 Brant already provides the core shield, healing and Outro. S1 improves his damage and offers temporary enemy stagnation during Returned from Ashes, which can help the anchor connect. S2 adds 30% CRIT Rate to the important aerial and anchor attacks and introduces additional explosions triggered through the Outro recipient's Skill hits. Adjust Echo CRIT Rate if obtaining S2 causes those attacks to exceed the useful cap. S3 directly raises the anchor's damage multiplier by 42%; its value is concentrated in a move you need to land reliably. Later sequences add sustain or further Basic Attack damage, but they demand much greater investment.

If you cannot reach 250% ER without seriously weakening the rest of the build, first compare a free ER weapon with your existing Echo inventory. The signature is an attractive investment for a Brant damage-focused account because it helps both the set requirement and the correct damage category. If you primarily need Brant to sustain and buff another Fusion carry, a usable S0 build may already solve that account problem. Reserve premium currency for the teammate whose absence limits the whole composition.

## Fixing failed rotations

Watch Bravo during the aerial sequence. If it stops increasing, check whether the target moved out of range or you ended the chain prematurely. If the anchor lands but the next character has no Intro, look at Concerto independently of Bravo; the two gauges solve different problems. If Liberation is unavailable next time, confirm that each previous hit connected and that you did not leave before the expected resource generation. If your support buffs end early, shorten setup or change the order of your teammates. A gear upgrade will rarely fix a rotation that consistently misses the anchor.
