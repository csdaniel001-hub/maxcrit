## How to use this list

Each code can be claimed once per character. If you play several characters in different states, each one has its own Player ID and can redeem separately. Rewards land in your in-game mail, so open the message after redeeming or the items won't reach your inventory.

## How to redeem Whiteout Survival codes

1. In the game, tap your avatar in the top-left corner and copy your Player ID.
2. Open the official Gift Code Center at [wos-giftcode.centurygame.com](https://wos-giftcode.centurygame.com/).
3. Sign in with your Player ID, enter a code, complete the verification step and confirm.
4. Claim the reward message in your in-game mail.
5. On Android you can also redeem in the game: tap your avatar, open Settings, then Gift Code.

## Why might a code fail?

Check capitalization and stray spaces first. After that, the usual causes are a code that has already been claimed on that character, a campaign that ended or hit its claim limit, or an offer limited to certain regions or states. Never give your password to a third-party site: the Gift Code Center only needs your Player ID.

## Where to find new codes

New Whiteout Survival codes appear on the game's official X account, Facebook page and Discord server, and in in-game mail announcements.
