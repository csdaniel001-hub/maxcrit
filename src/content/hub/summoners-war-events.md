Summoners War's weekly mission event runs from November 15 to January 24 and pays out 12-Year Scrolls along the way, with a Transcendence Scroll for players who complete 50 missions. Siege Season 23 starts on November 30.

## Event calendar

| Activity | Starts | Ends | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly missions | Nov 15, 6:00 PM | Jan 24, 2027, 6:59 AM | PST |
| Weekly mission rewards | During the event | Jan 25, 2027, 6:59 AM | PST |
| Siege Battle Season 23 | Nov 30, 11:00 AM | Feb 26, 2027, 10:59 PM | Server time |
| Siege Season 23 tournament | Mar 1, 2027, 11:00 AM | Mar 20, 2027, 11:59 PM | Server time |

## Weekly mission rewards

Each week opens up to 6 missions, each with its own reward, and a new set unlocks every Sunday at 7:00 AM PST. Missions for weeks that haven't opened yet don't count, so you can't get ahead.

| Total missions | Reward |
|---|---|
| 15 | 12-Year Special Scroll x10 |
| 20 | 12-Year L&D Scroll (Select) x1 |
| 35 | 12-Year L&D Scroll (Select) x1 |
| 40 | 12-Year Legendary Scroll x1 |
| 45 | Legendary All-Attribute Scroll x1 |
| 50 | Transcendence Scroll x1, Splendid Blessing of Summon x1 |

There are about ten weeks of missions at up to 6 each, so the 50 threshold leaves some room for a missed week, but not many.

## What counts and what doesn't

- **Arena**: Arena Battle and Rival Battle count; Arena Interserver Battle and Dimension Arena don't.
- **World Arena**: Normal Battle and Goodwill Battle don't count.
- **Monster Subjugation**: only your first entry in Round 1 and in Round 2 counts.
- **Tartarus' Labyrinth**: each successful stage attack counts; Time Limit stages count when the battle ends.

Three missions count earlier progress: Week 1's Trial of Ascension (Hell) mission if you cleared the version that opened November 14 PST, January Temple of Chaos battles finished before that mission opens, and Battle Training Ground stars for the Week 9 mission.
