Xilonen is a 5-star Geo sword support whose build is unusually forgiving. Her resistance reduction depends on her Skill talent and team composition, while DEF improves her healing. That makes a functional support build considerably cheaper than chasing a damage dealer's artifact rolls. She returns on the [Version 7.2 Chronicled Wish](/hub/banners/genshin-impact-banners.html) until December 15 at 14:59 server time. For investment decisions, see the [Phase II pull guide](/hub/guides/genshin-impact-names-forged-beneath-blazing-skies-pull-guide.html).

## How to activate her support effects

Xilonen changes her Source Samples according to the other characters' elements. Pyro, Hydro, Cryo and Electro teammates contribute their respective elements. In a support lineup with at least two such teammates, her Skill followed by two Normal Attacks fills her Nightsoul gauge and activates the relevant resistance reduction. Her Burst provides active-character healing in this configuration.

A lineup containing Xilonen, two other Geo characters and just one Hydro teammate cannot use the same support sequence as a mixed-element team. Check the party before spending resources on gear. Her C0 kit also lacks interruption resistance, so replacing a shielder may make a fragile carry harder to play.

## Artifacts and stat priorities

| Slot | Recommended main stat |
|---|---|
| Sands | Energy Recharge if needed for Burst uptime; DEF% otherwise |
| Goblet | DEF% |
| Circlet | CRIT Rate with Favonius; DEF% or Healing Bonus otherwise |

Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City is the usual first choice. A qualifying reaction grants 12% Elemental DMG Bonus for 15 seconds; triggering it in Nightsoul's Blessing adds 28% for 20 seconds. Both components overlap for 15 seconds, giving a total 40% bonus during that window. Apply the carry's element to the enemy before Xilonen triggers the reaction. Resistance reduction and the artifact damage bonus are separate effects, so activating one does not automatically activate the other for every element in the party.

If another teammate already wears Scroll, consider Instructor for reaction teams or Noblesse Oblige for ATK-scaling carries. Instructor grants 120 Elemental Mastery for eight seconds after a reaction, while Noblesse requires a Burst. Conventional Elemental DMG bonuses also have less value for damage that does not scale with them, including Lunar Reaction damage. Choose the set based on what the carry actually deals.

## Weapons and the DEF stopping point

| Weapon | When to use it |
|---|---|
| Peak Patrol Song | Premium team damage buff; aim for 3,200 DEF when its effect is active at R1 |
| Favonius Sword | Team needs energy; equip enough CRIT Rate to trigger its passive reliably |
| Flute of Ezpitzal | Craftable DEF option when energy is already sufficient |
| Skyrider Sword | Inexpensive Energy Recharge placeholder |

Peak Patrol Song adds 8 percentage points of party Elemental DMG Bonus per 1,000 DEF at R1 once its two-stack condition is met, capped at 25.6%. The weapon also grants the wielder 8% DEF and 10% All Elemental DMG Bonus per stack. The 3,200 DEF target is her effective DEF after the two stacks are active. The following examples show the nominal buff at different DEF values, not a measured increase in team DPS.

| Effective DEF | R1 party Elemental DMG Bonus |
|---:|---:|
| 2,000 | 16.0% |
| 2,500 | 20.0% |
| 3,000 | 24.0% |
| 3,200 | 25.6% |
| 3,500 | 25.6% |

If your equipped weapon is Favonius, the 3,200 DEF target has no special significance. Build enough healing and energy for your team, then stop farming. Extra DEF beyond the signature cap still affects healing, but it does not increase that weapon's capped party buff.

## Energy and rotation

Energy Recharge depends on whether Xilonen catches her Skill particles, how often she uses her Skill and whether the team generates additional energy. The two-piece Scroll bonus restores 6 Energy when a nearby party member triggers a Nightsoul Burst, reducing her energy needs. Favonius procs and catching her own Geo particles can help further. Test Burst availability across repeated rotations in your actual team: if it is ready when healing is needed, additional ER has little value. If it is consistently late, prioritize ER before extra DEF.

For a support rotation, apply the desired aura, switch to Xilonen, use her Skill and perform two Normal Attacks. Add her Burst when healing is needed. A team already sustaining itself can omit the Burst, reducing the need to chase ER rolls. In Furina teams, healing is more important because it helps build Fanfare.

## Teams and upgrade priorities

| Example lineup | What Xilonen contributes |
|---|---|
| Neuvillette, Furina, Kazuha, Xilonen | Hydro resistance reduction, healing and elemental damage support |
| Mualani, Xiangling, Sucrose or Kazuha, Xilonen | Hydro support and Nightsoul interaction; Mualani and Xiangling already provide two converted Samples, leaving room for Anemo support |
| Navia, Bennett, Xiangling, Xilonen | Geo support with two eligible teammates; Navia still needs shard generation |

These are example configurations, not a version-wide team ranking. Xilonen's limited off-field Geo application matters in Navia teams, while replacing Kazuha in other lineups can cost grouping utility.

Raise the Skill first, then the Burst if you need more healing. Normal Attack talent investment is unnecessary for this support build. Once the artifact set, energy and weapon conditions are met, spend the next batch of Resin on the character doing most of the team's damage. A marginal healing upgrade is unlikely to solve an underbuilt carry.
