Pokémon GO has two publisher-confirmed public offer codes for avatar footwear as of November 25, 2026. Both are redeemable online and have published end dates. The Gengar and Jigglypuff Crocs clogs are separate offers that come with a matching Crocs purchase. We haven't redeemed either public code ourselves, and an offer may be deactivated before its end date, and some are region-locked or simply used up, so a failed code is no reason to skip the other one.

## Current Pokémon GO offer codes

| Code | Reward | Published expiry | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `POKEMONGOxCROCSxELECTRIFYING` | Pikachu Classic Clog avatar item | Dec 31, 2027 | Publisher-confirmed |
| `ADIDASxPOKEMON` | adidas avatar shoes | Jan 15, 2027 | Publisher-confirmed |

The [official Crocs announcement](https://pokemongo.com/news/pokemon-crocs-2026) publishes the Pikachu string. The [adidas announcement](https://pokemongo.com/en/news/pokemon-x-adidas-2026) confirms the shoes code. We checked the published offers on November 25.

## Purchase-linked Crocs offers

Niantic describes the Gengar Classic Clog and Jigglypuff Classic Platform Clog as offers tied to buying the matching Crocs model, with a redemption deadline of December 31, 2027. If you bought either pair, use the code supplied with your purchase.

## How to redeem Pokémon GO codes

1. Open the [official offer redemption page](https://store.pokemongo.com/offer-redemption) in your mobile browser or on a computer.
2. Sign in using a login method linked to your Pokémon GO Trainer account.
3. Paste the code, preserving the capitalization shown in the table.
4. Submit it and open Pokémon GO to see the reward. For clothing, look in the avatar wardrobe.

The web redemption route applies to both Android and iOS. If a login method is missing from the sign-in page, use another method linked to the same Trainer account. Avoid creating a second account by accident. Sponsored offer redemption on child accounts requires the applicable parental permission.

## Other ways to claim rewards

The adidas partnership also has location-based Timed Research at participating stores, separate from the public shoes offer. Players interested in the jacket, cap and research rewards need to follow the location and completion deadlines in the event announcement.

Duolingo's collaboration uses individual reward codes, each issued to an eligible participant. Read our [Duolingo event coverage](/news/pokemon-go-duolingo-duos-pokemon-party.html) for the participation rules. For upcoming raids, events and research windows, use the [Pokémon GO event calendar](/hub/guides/pokemon-go-events-calendar-november-2026-february-2027.html).

## Why an offer code may fail

An expired offer, an already claimed reward, an incorrect login or an account-specific eligibility rule can prevent redemption. Check the Trainer account before trying again. Web Store discount codes and purchase promotions can also have separate checkout requirements; use the offer redemption page for the avatar codes above.
