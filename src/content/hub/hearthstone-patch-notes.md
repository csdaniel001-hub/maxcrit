Hearthstone's next major update is scheduled for December 1. This selected patch history separates released balance changes from upcoming mechanics, which matters when crafting cards before a patch.

## Patch history

| Version | Date | Status | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 37.2 | Dec 1 | Announced | Battlegrounds Season 15, new events and Arena changes |
| 37.0.2 | Nov 3 | Released | Standard, Arena and Battlegrounds balance pass |
| 37.0 | October | Released | Reign of the Black Empire launch (live October 20) |

## 37.2 upcoming changes

| Mechanic | Change |
|---|---|
| Resilient | Incoming attack damage halved, rounded up |
| Mission | One-time reward for a minion's completed objective |
| Elemental Invocation | Tavern spells grant stats; Elementals also gain a temporary Bonus Keyword until next turn |
| Bonus Keywords | Stealth rotates out as Resilient enters |
| Timewarped Tavern | Returns for Season 15 |
| Arena drafting | Reign of the Black Empire cards appear at the same rate as other sets, after being offered more often since launch |

## Selected 37.0.2 balance changes

| Card | Previous | Updated |
|---|---|---|
| M.O.T.H.E.R | 9 Mana | 10 Mana |
| Wrath of C'Thun | 7 split damage | 6 split damage |
| C'Thun, Eye of the Empire | Triple abilities after all four triggers | Quadruple abilities |
| Tinker | 2 Mana | 1 Mana |
| Flamespitter | 2/2 | 3/2 |
| Egg of Khelos | Khelos had Taunt | Taunt removed |

The two-week full dust refund for the cards changed in 37.0.2, plus Gargantuan Slitherer, Ritual of C'Thun, Tentacle Lash, Watch Over and Dark Deacon, has closed. Spawn of C'Thun is the exception: a bug kept it out of that window, and it becomes eligible for a full dust refund starting with 37.2.

The full patch notes are linked above. Our [Battlegrounds Season 15 news](/news/hearthstone-37-2-battlegrounds-season-15.html) and [events calendar](/hub/events/hearthstone-events.html) cover December in more detail.
