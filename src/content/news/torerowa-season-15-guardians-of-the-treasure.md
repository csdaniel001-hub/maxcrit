ASOBIMO opened TOREROWA Season 15, Guardians of the Treasure, on November 25 for mobile and Steam. Its extraction dungeon now extends to floor B35F, and the season introduces guild facilities and automatic translation for in-game chat. The season ends December 24.

TOREROWA drops as many as five competing parties into the same dungeon. Players enter in parties of three, fight monsters, collect items and encounter rival groups. Finding valuable loot only pays off when the party manages to extract with it, so the stronger monsters in the new floors add another consideration to a decision about when to leave.

The Guild Plaza is accompanied by guild missions, a league and an arena. Adventurers also have access to the Tavern and Workshop, while Treasure Gacha and Dress-up Gacha expand the game's collection features.

Chat translation supports English, Japanese, Korean, both forms of Chinese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish and Portuguese.

The game is free to download on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/torerowa/id6474421736) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.asobimo.torerowa).
