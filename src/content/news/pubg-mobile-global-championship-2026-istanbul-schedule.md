The last event of the year's PUBG MOBILE Esports circuit starts on November 26 in Istanbul, Türkiye: the 2026 PUBG MOBILE Global Championship. 39 teams compete for a $3,000,000 prize pool there until December 13, and 16 of them make the Grand Finals.

## Dates and format

| Stage | Dates (2026) |
|---|---|
| The Gauntlet | November 26 – 27 |
| Group Stage | November 29 – 30 and December 2 – 3 |
| Last Chance | December 5 – 6 |
| Grand Finals | December 11 – 13 |

- **The Gauntlet**: 16 top-ranked teams from regional qualifying play 12 matches. The top 6 go straight to the Grand Finals, and the other 10 drop into the Group Stage.
- **Group Stage**: 32 teams split into Group Green and Group Red, each playing 12 matches over two days. Three teams from each group reach the Grand Finals, and 4th to 11th place go to Last Chance.
- **Last Chance**: 16 teams play for the final three Grand Finals spots.
- **Grand Finals**: 15 qualified teams plus Host Country Invitee ULF Esports, so 16 teams play for the title.

If teams tie on points for a qualifying spot in the first three stages, a TDM-Tiebreaker decides it: a best-of-three 4v4 on Warehouse. A round goes to the first team to 15 eliminations, or to whoever has more when the clock runs out, and the last elimination settles a draw.

## How the Grand Finals are decided

The Grand Finals run up to 18 matches over three days under the SMASH rule. If no team has taken a SMASH win by the end of those matches, a 19th match, THE FINAL CIRCLE, decides the title.

The final day opens with a SUPREMACY match. Its winner earns the right to ban one map from the rotation for THE FINAL CIRCLE, which only matters if the event goes that far.

## Teams to watch

S2G Esports top the PMGC Points leaderboard after winning the 2026 PUBG MOBILE World Cup in Paris. 4Thrives Esports and Wolves Esports both won 2026 PUBG MOBILE Global Open events this season, with Wolves taking the SMASH Rule win at 2026 PMGO S2.

|---|---|---|---|

| Aurora Gaming | 4K Esports | iKURD ESPORTS | S2G Esports |

Nigma Galaxy, Bigetron by Vitality and Alpha7 make their first 2026 PMGC appearance in the Group Stage. Alpha7 arrive as defending champions from the 2025 PMGC.

## Where to watch

The official PUBG MOBILE Esports accounts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are the place to find where to watch, match schedules and rosters.

Mark November 26 and 27 if you only follow part of the event. Six of the 16 teams in The Gauntlet skip straight to the Grand Finals, so those two days settle a good part of the final lineup before most of the field has played a match.
