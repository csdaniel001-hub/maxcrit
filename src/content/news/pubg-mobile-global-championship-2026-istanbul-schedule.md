The 2026 PUBG MOBILE Global Championship, the last event of the year's PUBG MOBILE Esports circuit, starts on November 26 in Istanbul, Türkiye. 39 teams compete for a $3,000,000 prize pool until December 13.

## Dates and format

| Stage | Dates (2026) |
|---|---|
| The Gauntlet | November 26 – 27 |
| Group Stage | November 29 – 30 and December 2 – 3 |
| Last Chance | December 5 – 6 |
| Grand Finals | December 11 – 13 |

- **The Gauntlet**: 16 top-ranked teams from regional qualifying play 12 matches. The top 6 go straight to the Grand Finals, and the other 10 drop into the Group Stage.
- **Group Stage**: 32 teams split into Group Green and Group Red, each playing 12 matches over two days. Three teams from each group reach the Grand Finals, and 4th to 11th place go to Last Chance.
- **Last Chance**: 16 teams play for the final three Grand Finals spots.
- **Grand Finals**: 15 qualified teams plus Host Country Invitee ULF Esports, so 16 teams play for the title.

If teams tie on points for a qualifying spot in the first three stages, a TDM-Tiebreaker decides it: a best-of-three 4v4 on Warehouse.

## Teams to watch

S2G Esports top the PMGC Points leaderboard after winning the 2026 PUBG MOBILE World Cup in Paris. 4Thrives Esports and Wolves Esports both won 2026 PUBG MOBILE Global Open events this season, with Wolves taking the SMASH Rule win at 2026 PMGO S2.

The 16 teams in The Gauntlet:

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| AK Gold Esports | Sharper Esports | 4Thrives Esports | The MongolZ |
| Aurora Gaming | 4K Esports | iKURD ESPORTS | S2G Esports |
| Gaming Stars Esports | Hustler Crew | Wolves Esports | RoW Esports |
| XForce Rejects | All Gamers | Team SouL | Nongshim RedForce |

Nigma Galaxy, Bigetron by Vitality and Alpha7 make their first 2026 PMGC appearance in the Group Stage.

## Where to watch

For where to watch, match schedules and rosters, the release points to the official PUBG MOBILE Esports accounts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

The Gauntlet is the stage that sets up the rest. Six of its 16 teams skip ahead to the Grand Finals, so November 26 and 27 decide a good part of the final lineup before most of the field has played.
