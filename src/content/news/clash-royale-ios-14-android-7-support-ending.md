Clash Royale will stop running on iOS 14 or earlier and Android 7.0 or earlier with its December 1 update. From then on, the game needs iOS 15 or Android 8.0 at minimum. Supercell gives performance problems and the limits of older operating systems as the reason.

The cut-off is about the operating system, so check your phone's software updates first. If it can move to iOS 15 or Android 8.0 before December 1, you can keep playing on it.

If it can't, you'll need a compatible device to continue. Link your account to Supercell ID before you switch, and make sure you can still get into the email address attached to it, because that's how you recover your progress on the new phone. Supercell's FAQ covers account transfers in more detail.

Anyone planning to play the new [Crown Series](/hub/guides/clash-royale-crown-series-guide.html) Duels should sort this out before the first tournament weekend, since a phone that can't run the update can't enter.
