Riot's Wild Rift patch 7.3f, posted on November 11, 2026, adds a new champion and spreads balance changes across champions, mage items and the jungle. Riot notes that content is released throughout the patch, so not everything arrives on day one.

## Sylas and the champion changes

Sylas, the Unshackled is the new champion. In his story he's a revolutionary from Demacia's Dregbourne who uses other people's magic, which in game terms means stealing ultimates.

Nerfs:

- **Kalista**: Pierce base damage goes from 70/135/200/265 to 30/110/190/270. Riot says she was too dominant in Baron Lane, and the cut lands almost entirely on the early ranks.
- **Shen**: Stand United's cooldown rises from 110/100/90s to 120/110/100s, and the base shield drops from 170/340/510 to 160/260/360.
- **Kassadin**: Magic Resistance from 38 to 34 and Force Pulse's AP ratio from 60% to 50%, ahead of the Rod of Ages buff.
- **Irelia**: less flat damage on Ionian Fervor and less healing on Bladesurge, but more Attack Speed and a higher Bladesurge AD ratio. Riot wants her to build damage items, not win without them.

Buffs:

- **Yasuo**: Armor per level from 4.3 to 5.2, and Sweeping Blade damage up to 70/90/110/130.
- **Lucian**: higher AD ratios on Lightslinger's second shot and on Piercing Light.
- **Rumble**: The Equalizer goes to 800/1200/1600 + 220% AP.
- **Graves and Akshan**: more base Armor for Graves, more Magic Resistance for Akshan.
- **Viktor**: power moved from AoE into his skillshots and dueling.

## Items

Riot says burn builds dominate at lower skill and Magic Penetration builds at high skill, so it buffed the mage items in between: Luden's Echo, Rod of Ages (now 2600 gold), Lich Bane, Morellonomicon (2550 gold) and Hextech Rocketbelt. Liandry's Torment loses 10 Ability Power, from 70 to 60.

Eclipse drops from 65 to 60 Attack Damage after becoming the default fighter pick in Baron Lane and the jungle, and Goredrinker goes up to 45 Attack Damage and 400 Health to take its place. Support items Bulwark of the Mountain and Black Mist Scythe now earn 60 gold per minute instead of 75.

## Jungle

Riot says tank junglers were clearing camps fast enough to gank often without losing farm. Smite's burn damage drops to 18 - 158, its bonus Armor and Magic Resist ratios fall from 25% to 20%, and Conservation now gives 30 gold, or 55 when you're behind.

If you play Kalista or Shen, expect a harder early game, and if you've been building Eclipse on every fighter, Goredrinker is the item Riot is pointing you toward.
