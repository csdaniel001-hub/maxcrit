Fishing Inc: Legendary Catch has arrived on Android and iOS. The free-to-download incremental fishing game from BigBonk Studios and Snapbreak Games went live worldwide on November 25.

Fishing is the engine of the game's progression. Start with basic equipment, sell your catches for cash, then invest the proceeds in equipment and abilities that improve your chances of bringing in more valuable fish. Additional locations expand the range of species you can pursue as your character develops.

The collection side gives those upgrades a purpose beyond accumulating money. The Fish Log tracks discoveries, while rarer species and Legendary catches provide longer-term goals. Players who enjoy building up efficiency in incremental games have a familiar loop here, without the need for a competitive multiplayer premise.

The game is available from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.snapbreak.fishinginc) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishing-inc-legendary-catch/id6770534658). It is free to install and the Apple listing indicates in-app purchases, while Google Play also discloses advertising. BigBonk is a two-person independent studio, with Snapbreak handling the mobile publishing release.
