Jagex's Quest Month rolled into its third week on November 18, 2026, and the update is built for both halves of the Old School RuneScape playerbase: quest completionists get two new quests, and raiders get a way to learn the Fractured Archive without risking anything. Everything here applies on mobile too, since phones and desktop share the same worlds.

## Two quests you have to find yourself

Jagex didn't name the two quests, list their requirements or say where they start, so finding them is part of the event. What it did publish is the rewards.

- **Quest 1**: 3 Quest Points, 10,000 Slayer XP, a lamp worth 10,000 Prayer XP, and an untradeable bone storage item. It holds up to 28 bones, can be worn on the back, and can be bought back from the quest NPC for 10,000 coins if you lose it. Bones have to be taken out in the bank before you bury or offer them.
- **Quest 2**: 1 Quest Point, 10,000 Farming XP, 2,000 Cooking XP, 2,000 Herblore XP and a combat lamp worth 2,500 XP in a combat skill of your choice. It also unlocks a passive: allotment patches in the Farming Guild can no longer catch disease, and seed packs get a 1 in 100 chance to contain a Seed of Growth.

With these two, the total number of Quest Points in the game goes past 350. Jagex plans to tie an extra Slayer block slot to that milestone, and it will go to a player poll first.

## Fractured Archive Practice Mode

The Fractured Archive raid now has a Practice Mode, picked from the grouping interface. You can enter any room you've already reached in Normal Mode, and beating a boss from the start in Practice Mode unlocks the bosses after it. For example, defeating Ezekiel from the start of phase 1 opens Moia and Dreadnoth. A Skip Phase button jumps to any phase you've already seen.

Practice runs give no loot and don't touch your Normal Mode records. Instead of bringing supplies, you get the Guthixian Root, a reusable one-slot item that restores 20 HP, gives the Stamina effect, works as a Super Restore, Super Combat, Ranging and Magic potion, and cures poison and bleed.

The raid itself was adjusted too. Last week's 10% HP increase for duo scaling has been reverted, and the duo unique chance moves from 1 in 16 to 1 in 17. Special attacks such as the Crimson kisten and Dragon claws now deal the right damage to a stunned Ezekiel.

## Smaller changes, including mobile

- On mobile, the outdated Minimenu re-ordering setting has been removed. Jagex also listed a known issue where the mobile Stats screen shows the same number for current XP and the XP needed to level up, with a hotfix planned.
- Hardcore Worlds get a "Hardcore death broadcast minimum level" setting, and trading is now disabled in Death's Office.
- Grand Exchange buy offers use coins first when they cover the full cost.
- The Jad jumper and Beer belly sweater now keep you warm at Wintertodt.

Next week, Quest Month continues with The Graveyard, followed by a Fremennik quest that adds a new Agility course.
