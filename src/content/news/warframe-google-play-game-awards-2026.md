Nine months after landing on Android worldwide, Warframe has won Best Multiplayer in Google Play's US Best of 2026 awards. It's also back at The Game Awards after 10 years away, nominated for Best Community Support.

A multiplayer award on Google Play is a neat result for a game that only reached Android globally in February. It arrived with Cross Platform Play and Cross Platform Save, so existing players could bring their progress, builds and squadmates onto their phones, and new Android players walked into a community that was already there. That ready-made player base is a big part of why the multiplayer prize makes sense.

The trophy shelf may get busier in December. Warframe is up for three 2027 Pocket Gamer Awards: Mobile Game of the Year, Best Mobile Port and Best Mobile Game Character, with daily voting open until December 31. Digital Extremes has also picked up a Hollywood Music in Media Award, Best Live Concert for Visual Media, for TennoConcert, the live show performed at TennoCon 2026 in London, Ontario. The studio calls 2026 Warframe's highest-performing year.

If you want to back it at The Game Awards, Best Community Support voting closes on December 10 at 02:00 UTC, and the ceremony starts on December 11 at 00:30 UTC.
