Hearthstone Patch 37.2 arrives on December 1 and starts Battlegrounds Season 15, Warped Empire. The season brings back the Timewarped Tavern in place of Trinkets, adds three new keywords and two new heroes, and gives every minion type new cards. A 2v2 Tavern Brawl follows on December 9.

## Timewarped Tavern returns

On Turns 6 and 9 you visit the Timewarped Tavern and spend Chronum on special cards that Bob's Tavern doesn't sell. With Trinkets gone for the season, those two turns become the fixed points to plan your shop around.

## Three new keywords

- **Resilient**: a Resilient minion takes half damage when attacked, rounded up. It joins the Bonus Keyword pool, and Stealth rotates out of that pool for now.
- **Mission**: a minion with Mission has a goal that pays a one-time reward. Bot Launcher, Crafty Cookfire and Loti, Devilsaur Form are among the new Mission minions.
- **Elemental Invocation**: four Tavern spells, Invocation of Earth, Fire, Air and Water, give a minion stats. On an Elemental they also add a Bonus Keyword until your next turn, and Command the Elements grants all four.

Resilient halves the damage a minion takes when it's attacked, so it gets more out of high-Health minions that trade into big attackers. A Mission only pays once, which makes it worth weighing against a minion that helps your board right away.

## 2v2 Tavern Brawl, December 9 to 23

Teammates play at the same time on separate boards against separate opponents. Each player keeps their own Health, and bringing either opponent to 0 wins for your team. You can click the Portal to see your ally's board, or drag a card onto it to pass that card across; after passing, you wait until the end of your next turn to pass again.

| Week | Decks |
|---|---|
| December 9 to 16 | Preconstructed Decks |
| December 16 to 23 | Decks you build |

Your first win gives one Reign of the Black Empire Pack.
