The two big app stores published their year-end picks a day apart. Google announced its Best of Play 2026 winners on November 17, and Apple named its App Store Award finalists on November 18. Arknights: Endfield, with its real-time combat and factory-building, is the only game that appears on both.

## Google Play Best of 2026: the games

These are the US winners. Google ran separate posts for other markets, including Asia Pacific, and the local winners there differ.

| Award | Winner |
|---|---|
| Best Game | Arknights: Endfield |
| Best Cross-device Game | Moonlight Peaks |
| Best Multiplayer | Warframe |
| Best Pick Up & Play | Subway Surfers City |
| Best Indie | Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor |
| Best Story | Sea of Stars |
| Best Ongoing | Zenless Zone Zero |

Google's editors praised Endfield's story, real-time combat and the depth of its factory-building and management systems. Moonlight Peaks won the cross-device award for how well it moves between phone and longer sessions on Google Play Games on PC or a Googlebook. Its farming and town life come with werewolves to court.

Two of the seven are gacha games, Endfield and Zenless Zone Zero, and the Best Ongoing award is the one that rewards a live service for a year of updates. HoYoverse's Zenless Zone Zero got it while its 3.4 update is weeks away.

## Apple's App Store Award game finalists

Apple's 45 finalists span apps and games on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Apple Arcade, plus a Cultural Impact category. Winners will be announced in the coming weeks. These are the game categories:

| Category | Finalists |
|---|---|
| iPhone Game of the Year | BALL x PIT, DAVE THE DIVER, Gambonanza |
| iPad Game of the Year | Aniimo, Arknights: Endfield, Where Winds Meet |
| Mac Game of the Year | Big Walk, Blue Prince, Crimson Desert |
| Apple Arcade Game of the Year | Perchang World, PowerWash Simulator, Pusheen's Place |
| Apple Vision Pro Game of the Year | Cueva de las Manos, Ping Pong Club, Spatial Ops |
| Cultural Impact Game | and Roger, Consume Me, PancitoMerge, Sago Mini Jinja's Garden, Spilled!, Tiny Bookshop |

Apple singles out BALL x PIT for reworking the brick breaker, DAVE THE DIVER for its ocean exploration and Gambonanza for its twist on chess rules. The iPad list holds the big fantasy and action releases: Aniimo with its creature-filled fantasy quests, Endfield's battles on a faraway moon, and the martial arts of Where Winds Meet.

## What the two lists tell you

The two stores reward different things. Google hands out finished awards by play style, from multiplayer to story, plus a separate award for a game that kept updating all year. Apple's game shortlists are split by device, so the same game can only compete where Apple's editors placed it, and Endfield competes on iPad rather than iPhone.

For players, the overlap is the useful part. If you want one game that the editors of both stores picked this year, it is Arknights: Endfield. Whether it also wins on iPad is still open, and Apple will settle that when it names the winners.
