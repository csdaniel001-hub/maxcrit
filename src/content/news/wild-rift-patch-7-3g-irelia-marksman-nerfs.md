Wild Rift's November 25 patch is good news for Irelia and bad news for anyone who builds marksman boots. Patch 7.3g gives her more damage, healing and uptime, takes attack speed and on-hit healing off both marksman boot upgrades, and adjusts Vayne, Garen, Aurora, Smolder and Sylas, with a separate set of changes for AAA ARAM. Riot rolls content out across the patch, so not every change may be on your server yet.

## Champion changes

Irelia comes out on top. A higher base AD and a full second off Bladesurge mean a missed reset costs her less, and the extra healing keeps her in longer trades. Vayne's Silver Bolts now starts a touch lower and ends higher, so her power moves toward the late game, while Final Hour gives 10 more bonus AD at every rank.

| Champion | Main changes |
|---|---|
| Irelia | Base AD 58 → 62; Bladesurge cooldown 9/8/7/6 → 8/7/6/5 s; healing 9/11/13/15% → 11/13/15/17% |
| Vayne | Silver Bolts 6/7/8/9% → 5.5/7/8.5/10%; Final Hour bonus AD 30/40/50 → 40/50/60 |
| Garen | Judgement armor shred 10% → 15%; higher damage scaling |
| Aurora | Twofold Hex narrower on the initial cast, wider on the return |
| Smolder | Achooo! cooldown 13/12/11/10 s; Flap, Flap, Flap cooldown 22/19/16/13 s |
| Sylas | Chain Lash minion damage 30% → 35%; Kingslayer bonus-health healing 5% → 6.5% |

## Item and rune changes

For most ranked players the item list matters more than any single champion. Berserker's Greaves drop from 35% to 30% attack speed and Gunmetal Greaves from 50% to 45%, and Blessed Blade's on-hit healing falls to 5 on both, from 10 and 12. Ardent Censer and Guinsoo's Rageblade each lose 5% attack speed, down to 25%.

| Item or rune | Change |
|---|---|
| Berserker's Greaves | Attack speed 35% → 30%; on-hit healing 10 → 5 |
| Gunmetal Greaves | Attack speed 50% → 45%; on-hit healing 12 → 5 |
| Ardent Censer | Attack speed 30% → 25% |
| Guinsoo's Rageblade | Attack speed 30% → 25% |
| Immortal Shieldbow | Shield 350–650 → 380–700 (melee), 300–550 → 320–550 (ranged) |
| Harmonic Echo | Ability power 40 → 25 |
| Absolute Focus | Threshold 65% → 70%; AD 2–20 → 2–18; AP 4–40 → 3.3–30 |

Immortal Shieldbow goes the other way, with a bigger shield, especially for melee users. If your marksman build leaned on boot healing, Shieldbow is the item to look at again.

## AAA ARAM

The ARAM changes stay in ARAM. Bard gets 100 extra attack range, each basic attack cuts 2 seconds off his Meeps recharge, and his Attack Speed Augment restrictions are gone. Irelia's passive can now crit, and her first ability gains a 60% AP ratio with adaptive damage. Warmog's Armor rises from 900 to 1,200 health with a passive cooldown down from 15 to 10 seconds, and Original Heartsteel's charge damage goes from 140 + 3.5% to 140 + 5% max health.
