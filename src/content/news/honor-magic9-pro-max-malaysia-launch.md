For a phone built around its camera, the HONOR Magic9 Pro Max carries a lot of battery. HONOR launched the international model at an event in Kuala Lumpur on November 24, pairing an 8,000mAh silicon-carbon cell with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, and in Malaysia it costs RM5,999 for the 512GB model.

Preorders there run until December 3, and the phone reaches stores nationwide on December 4. Colours, configurations, prices and dates vary by market.

## The gaming-relevant specs

The chip is the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 Mobile Platform. The battery is 8,000mAh by typical capacity and 7,745mAh rated, and it charges at up to 100W wired and 80W wireless. The phone ships with MagicOS 11.

The battery is the figure mobile gamers should care about most. 8,000mAh is a big cell for a phone that isn't sold as a gaming phone, and on a flagship, battery is usually what ends a long session of Genshin Impact or Wuthering Waves.

## A camera phone first

Everything else about the launch points at the camera. The Magic9 Pro Max has Dual 200MP Imaging with ARRI Image Science, pitched as an end-to-end cinema workflow co-engineered with ARRI, and HONOR announced the HONOR Teleconverter G500, Teleconverter G200 and a SmallRig Mobile Video Kit alongside it.

That focus is the main difference from gaming phones such as the REDMAGIC 12 Pro, which run the same chip but add fans and shoulder triggers. If you want one premium phone for gaming and filming, the Magic9 Pro Max makes a strong case. If gaming comes first, a phone with a fan is built for long sessions in a way this one isn't.

## Malaysia preorder gifts

Deposit and full-payment preorders both come with a Harman Kardon Aura Studio 5 speaker, a magnetic swap case, three months of Google AI Pro, one year of liquid damage and crack protection, a one-year extended warranty and two years of battery replacement. Each account can preorder up to two units, and the window closes on December 3.
