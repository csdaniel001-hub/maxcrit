HONOR launched the international Magic9 Pro Max at an event in Kuala Lumpur on November 24. In Malaysia it costs RM5,999 for the 512GB model, with preorders until December 3 and nationwide store availability from December 4. HONOR says colours, configurations, prices and dates vary by market.

## The gaming-relevant specs

The phone runs on the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 Mobile Platform. The battery is an 8,000mAh silicon-carbon cell by typical capacity, 7,745mAh rated, and it charges at up to 100W wired and 80W wireless. It ships with MagicOS 11.

| Part | HONOR Magic9 Pro Max |
|---|---|
| Chip | Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 |
| Battery | 8,000mAh typical, 7,745mAh rated |
| Charging | 100W wired, 80W wireless |
| Software | MagicOS 11 |
| Malaysia price | RM5,999 (512GB) |

For mobile gamers the battery is the most useful number here. 8,000mAh is a big cell for a phone that isn't sold as a gaming phone, and battery is what usually ends a long session of Genshin Impact or Wuthering Waves on a flagship.

## A camera phone first

HONOR's launch is built around the camera. The Magic9 Pro Max has Dual 200MP Imaging with ARRI Image Science, and HONOR pitches it as an end-to-end cinema workflow co-engineered with ARRI. The HONOR Teleconverter G500, Teleconverter G200 and a SmallRig Mobile Video Kit were announced alongside it.

That positioning is the main difference from gaming phones such as the REDMAGIC 12 Pro, which run the same chip but add fans and shoulder triggers. The Magic9 Pro Max is the pick if you want one premium phone for gaming and filming. If gaming is the priority, a phone with a fan is built for long sessions in a way the Magic9 Pro Max isn't.

## Malaysia preorder gifts

Both deposit and full-payment preorders, open until December 3, come with a Harman Kardon Aura Studio 5 speaker, a magnetic swap case, three months of Google AI Pro, one year of liquid damage and crack protection, a one-year extended warranty and two years of battery replacement. HONOR limits preorders to two units per account.
