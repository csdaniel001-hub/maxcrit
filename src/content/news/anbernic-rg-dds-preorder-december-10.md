ANBERNIC has opened preorders for the RG DDS, a clamshell Android handheld with two screens. The official store lists it at $284.99, down from a regular $299.99, and says it "ships gradually starting December 10, 2026", from either China or the US.

It comes in Basalt Black, Glacier White and Phantom Blue, with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage or 12GB and 256GB.

## Two screens with different shapes

The top screen is a 4.7-inch 1200x720 IPS panel in 5:3, and the bottom one is a 4-inch 960x720 IPS panel in 4:3. Both are rated at 500 nits and 300 PPI. A retractable capacitive stylus is built in, and a Hall sensor puts the device to sleep when you close the lid.

The 4:3 lower screen suits older games and dual-screen emulation. In most Android games only one screen is used for play, so for those the RG DDS works like a 4.7-inch handheld with a second screen for maps, menus or a different app.

## Controls and extras

ANBERNIC lists capacitive analog sticks, a linear vibration motor, a 6-axis gyroscope, stereo speakers and a 3.5mm headphone jack. The software side includes over-the-air updates, Turnip graphics drivers and AI tools such as game guide lookup and live translation.

Active cooling with a fan and heat pipe is aimed at long sessions of 3D Android games, where heat is what limits sustained performance. The 12GB/256GB model leaves more room for large games that keep their data on internal storage.

## Who it suits

The RG DDS makes the most sense if you play dual-screen games through emulation, or want a handheld you can close and carry like a pocket console. If you mainly play modern Android games such as Genshin Impact or Honkai: Star Rail, a single larger screen gives you more room, because those games only draw on one display.
