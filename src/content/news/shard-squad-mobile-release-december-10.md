Shard Squad is making the jump from PC and consoles to phones on December 10. Android players can now [pre-register on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nuntiusgames.shardsquad), while the publisher plans to open iOS pre-registration separately.

Developed by Brazil's The Root Studios and published by Nuntius Games, the game combines a squad of elemental creatures with the pressure of a horde-survival roguelite. Players assemble their team to fight through waves of enemies in the kingdom of Mellunia, with a pixel-art presentation.

The mobile release comes with an interface redesigned around touchscreens. Previously released free Steam updates are included, and the Wind Character Pack and Ice Character Pack DLCs will be supported on mobile as well. The two Character Packs are optional purchases, sold separately, each adding four Shards.

That makes the phone launch an interesting alternative to starting with the original PC release. The studio is bringing its existing content updates forward on day one. Android pre-registration provides a release notification; the launch itself is planned for both mobile platforms on December 10.
