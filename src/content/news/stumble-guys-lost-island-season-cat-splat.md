Leading a Stumble Guys race got riskier on November 2, 2026. Version 0.104 opened the Lost Island season and added Cat Splat, an ability that locks onto a moving target and launches a missile to knock a rival over from a distance.

Scopely's season also brings creatures, Piñatas, weekly events and what it calls holiday chaos:

- **Matchmaking Area**: instead of a static wait screen, you can move around and emote while the game finds a match.
- **Piñatas**: smash them for cosmetics and currency.
- **Holiday events**: Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Cyber Week each bring events and surprises.

Cat Splat is the one that changes rounds. A homing missile that targets moving players works as a direct counter to whoever is out in front, so expect it to show up most in races where the leader is exposed. Sitting in second place until the final stretch suddenly looks like a plan.

The Matchmaking Area is a smaller touch, but it gives the wait for a match something to do.

Black Friday falls on November 27 this year and Cyber Week follows, so the holiday part of the season runs into early December.
