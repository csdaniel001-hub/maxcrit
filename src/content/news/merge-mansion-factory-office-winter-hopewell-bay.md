Merge Mansion's November 3, 2026 update starts the winter season in Hopewell Bay, the town where the game is set. Metacore's release notes keep it brief and list three things.

- **Factory Office**: the newest area to refurbish, with Maddie.
- **Winter look**: Hopewell Bay turns into a snowy winter wonderland.
- **Events**: a run of events as the holiday season approaches.

The Factory Office is the part that adds something new to do. If you had finished the previous rooms and stepped away, it's the reason to log back in.

The winter makeover is about the look of the town: the release notes describe it as Hopewell Bay turning into a snowy winter wonderland.

The release notes don't name the holiday events or give dates for them. They will show up in the game as the season goes on, so the in-game event list is where to check what's live each week.
