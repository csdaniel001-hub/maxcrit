15 years after its 2011 launch, The Sims FreePlay is throwing a three-week birthday party. Electronic Arts' Firemonkeys studio in Melbourne runs the 15th birthday events from November 24 to December 15, and the November update, the game's 119th, adds holiday content on top.

## The birthday schedule

| Dates | Event | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| November 24 to December 7 | 14 Days of Free Gifts | A daily login gift, including keepsakes, Build Mode items and a puppy |
| November 24 to December 5 | Stay for the Party: Influence Island | Throwback items from moments in the game's history |
| November 25 to December 8 | FreePlay Replay: Craft Challenge | A custom-designed estate with a working Plumbob water fountain on the lawn |
| December 15 | The 15th Birthday Gift | A surprise gift in your inbox, to claim before December 20 |

The estate is the headline prize, and logging in won't earn it: it comes from completing the Craft Challenge's in-game actions. The daily gifts are the opposite case. They only ask you to open the game each day, and every missed day is a missed gift.

## How the four events fit together

The dates overlap, so the first two weeks are the busy stretch. From November 25 to December 5 three events run at once: the daily gifts, the Influence Island throwback event and the Craft Challenge. Influence Island ends first, on December 5, so if you want its throwback items, finish it before turning to the estate. The daily gifts stop on December 7 and the Craft Challenge on December 8, which leaves a quiet week before the final gift on December 15.

That last gift has the shortest window of all. It lands in your inbox on December 15 and must be claimed before December 20, so anyone who only drops in occasionally over the holidays should open the inbox during those five days.

## What else the update adds

Holiday Quests bring festive rewards, and two new Season Passes carry birthday and holiday themed items. The App Store notes also name a Plumbob Mansion among the birthday rewards.

EA marked the anniversary with numbers too: around 5 billion Sims living in nearly half a billion SimTowns, about 140 million engagement rings, roughly 71 million pets including dragons and teacup pigs, and 8 million custom Home Lots submitted. The first version held a single neighbourhood of up to 16 Sims. Towns now hold up to 53 Sims across five regions, with more than 37,000 Build Mode and Create a Sim items to fill them.
