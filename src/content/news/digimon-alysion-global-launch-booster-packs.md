DIGIMON ALYSION is now live on mobile, and new players have four booster sets to explore before spending their first DigiGems. Bandai's launch campaign gives every player 40 booster tickets split evenly between those sets, plus a selection of individual cards.

## Which booster packs are available?

| Pack | Focus | Published card count |
|---|---|---|
| BD-01 NEW EVOLUTION | Green Vegetation and Insectoid cards, plus red, blue and yellow options | 123; 2 SEC and 16 Alt-Art listed |
| BD-02 ULTIMATE POWER | Black and purple cards | 119; 2 SEC and 14 Alt-Art listed |
| BD-03 UNION IMPACT | Security-effect cards | 119; 2 SEC and 14 Alt-Art listed |
| BD-04 GREAT LEGEND | Greymon-related cards | 122; 3 SEC and 17 Alt-Art listed |

The free tickets are allocated as ten per named set. Open them before committing premium currency to a deck: your initial collection may make one strategy substantially cheaper to build than another. The counts above follow Bandai's presentation; they are not a calculated total of distinct card variants.

## Claim the launch rewards first

The 450,000 pre-registration milestone unlocks the following for all players: ten tickets for each of the four sets, Gemmon (BD1-002), MetalGarurumon (BT1-044), WarGreymon (BT1-025), Omnimon (BT1-084), alternate-art Agumon (BT1-010) and one SEC Decryption Ticket. Collect them from the mail icon on the Home screen within the 30-day claim window.

A separate ten-day new player login bonus includes 5,000 Coins across five days, WarGreymon (BT4-048), MetalGarurumon (BT2-081), a Premium Chip (SR), an Upgrade Chip and another SEC Decryption Ticket.

Deck Missions Unlock Keys cost 250 DigiGems each for both the six Starter Decks and nine Entry Decks. Buying a key unlocks missions; completing those missions awards the corresponding deck. The standard Premium Battle Pass costs 750 DigiGems, with a 1,700-DigiGem version offering 20 levels of progress and an Upgrade Chip. Upgrading after earning standard pass levels also grants the corresponding previously unlocked premium rewards.

For a new account, claiming the mail and playing through the available beginner progression before buying a deck key is a reasonable starting approach. The value of each key will depend on the cards you already own and the deck you want to assemble. Starter Decks are designed to teach the core game, while Entry Decks target specific strategies for players who know the rules.
