Pokémon GO's Pokémon Champions Celebration Event runs from December 8 at 10 a.m. until December 14 at 8 p.m. local time. If you have been saving Pokémon for transfer to Pokémon HOME, the event's GO Pass is particularly useful: its first rank unlocks seven times the usual hourly GO Transporter Energy gain.

## Event bonuses and GO Pass

| Rank | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 1 | 7× hourly GO Transporter Energy gain |
| 10 | Double catch Candy |
| 20 | 3,000 extra XP from completed Raid Battles |
| 20, Deluxe only | Up to two free Raid Passes per day from Gym Photo Discs |

The double Candy bonus does not stack with December Community Day bonuses. Kecleon encounters at PokéStops are also more likely during the event. Transferring a Pokémon from GO to HOME and then sending it to visit Pokémon Champions during the collaboration can earn a gift in your Champions mailbox.

The free GO Pass unlocks automatically when the event begins. Deluxe costs US$4.99, with a $6.99 option that starts players at Rank 7. Local pricing and availability may vary. Deluxe includes extra rewards such as an Elite Charged TM and Rare Candy XL, and upgrading later grants previously unlocked Deluxe rewards. The web store’s base Deluxe purchase also includes ten Ultra Balls, five Max Revives, a Premium Battle Pass and five Max Potions. The +6 Ranks web purchase increases the bonus Premium Battle Pass count to two. Unlocked pass rewards expire December 16 at 8 p.m. local time, two days after event progression ends.

## Encounters and raids

Wild encounters include Charmander, Starly and Grookey, with rarer appearances such as Absol and Gible. One-star raids feature Bagon, Litten and Frigibax; three-star raids feature Raichu, Garchomp and Rillaboom. The five-star event pool includes Latias, Latios, Heatran, Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion, Regieleki and Regidrago. These five-star Pokémon follow the seasonal raid schedule from December 9 to 15. The collaboration’s Special Background opportunity ends with the event on December 14 at 8 p.m. local time, before that raid rotation finishes.

Event Field Research awards Rare Candy, and themed stickers are available from PokéStops and Gifts. Players primarily interested in Pokémon HOME transfers can benefit from Rank 1 without buying Deluxe.
