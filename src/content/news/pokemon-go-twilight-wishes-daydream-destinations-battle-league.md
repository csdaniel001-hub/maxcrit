Pokémon GO's week starts with Twilight Wishes, an event running from November 24 to 30, and ends with a new GO Battle League season, Daydream Destinations, on December 1. Niantic has also started a paid gifting trial in five countries.

## Twilight Wishes, November 24 to 30

Twilight Wishes begins at 10:00 local time on November 24 and ends at 20:00 local time on November 30. The event brings themed Pokémon encounters and a dedicated GO Pass, available in a standard and a Deluxe version for the whole event window. The Deluxe track raises the catching Stardust bonus to three times the normal amount, with conditions attached.

Because the event runs on local time, it starts and ends at different moments around the world. Wherever you are, the cut-off is 20:00 on the 30th on your own clock, so a last evening session needs to start early.

## Paid gifts trial in five countries

Gifting is no longer limited to the free gifts from PokéStops. In a trial covering Australia, Germany, South Korea, Mexico and France, you can buy eligible items from the in-game shop for a friend with real money, with a wider rollout planned later. PokéCoins, stickers, avatar items, limited-time bundles and Team Medallions are excluded. The friend has to use the same purchase currency and be at least a Great Friend.

There is a cap of 20 paid gifts a day, and gifts generally cannot be cancelled or refunded. Paid gifts are bought in the in-game shop only, with no web store option, and any Reward Road points stay with the buyer. Recipients can change their gifting preferences if they would rather not receive paid gifts.

## Daydream Destinations GO Battle League, from December 1

The next competitive season begins on December 1 at 13:00 PST (21:00 UTC). That is when you can claim your rewards from the current season and start a fresh ranked climb.

From December 1 to 8, all three main formats run as Mega Edition variants: Great League, Ultra League and Master League. Winning individual battles in that opening week pays four times the usual Stardust, though end-of-set rewards are not boosted. After that, the league line-up changes every week, with each switch at 13:00 PST.

Anyone still chasing this season's ranked goals has until the December 1 reset. Read the Mega Edition restrictions before building a team for that first week, since it is the week with four times the Stardust on the line.
