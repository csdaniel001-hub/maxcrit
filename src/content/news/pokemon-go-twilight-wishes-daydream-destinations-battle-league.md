Niantic has three announcements for Pokémon GO players this week: the Twilight Wishes event, which starts today, a paid gifting trial in five countries, and the next GO Battle League season, Daydream Destinations, which opens on December 1.

## Twilight Wishes, November 24 to 30

Twilight Wishes begins at 10:00 local time on November 24 and ends at 20:00 local time on November 30. The event brings themed Pokémon encounters and a dedicated GO Pass, available in a standard and a Deluxe version for the whole event window. The Deluxe track raises the catching Stardust bonus to three times the normal amount, under the conditions Niantic lists on the event page, which also carries the full raid and encounter rotations.

Because the event runs on local time, it starts and ends at different moments around the world. Check the Today view in the game before you plan a last session on the 30th.

## Paid gifts trial in five countries

Gifting is no longer limited to the free gifts from PokéStops. In a trial covering Australia, Germany, South Korea, Mexico and France, you can buy eligible items from the in-game shop for a friend with real money, with a wider rollout planned later. PokéCoins, stickers, avatar items, limited-time bundles and Team Medallions are excluded. The friend has to use the same purchase currency and be at least a Great Friend.

There is a cap of 20 paid gifts a day, and gifts generally cannot be cancelled or refunded. Purchases only work through the in-game shop, not the web store, and any Reward Road points go to the buyer rather than the friend who receives the gift. Recipients can change their gifting preferences if they would rather not receive paid gifts.

## Daydream Destinations GO Battle League, from December 1

The next competitive season begins on December 1 at 13:00 PST (21:00 UTC). That is when you can claim your rewards from the current season and start a fresh ranked climb.

From December 1 to 8, all three main formats run as Mega Edition variants: Great League, Ultra League and Master League. Winning individual battles in that opening week pays four times the usual Stardust, though end-of-set rewards are not boosted. After that, the league line-up changes every week, with each switch at 13:00 PST, and the full format calendar and battle rules are in the official announcement.

If you are still chasing this season's ranked goals, you have until the December 1 reset to finish them, and the Mega Edition restrictions are worth reading before you build a team for the first week.
