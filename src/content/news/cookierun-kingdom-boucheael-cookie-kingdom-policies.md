Devsisters released a CookieRun: Kingdom update on November 17, 2026. The release notes list:

- **New Cookies**: Bouchéael Cookie (LEGENDARY) and Traffic Candy Cookie (EPIC).
- **New story**: Timeline of Fate episodes 15 and 16.
- **Kingdom Policies**: a new system.
- **Starlight Island and Trials of the Nether Realm**: improvements to both.
- **New costumes**: White Lily Cookie (LEGENDARY), Elder Faerie Cookie (LEGENDARY) and Sugarfly Cookie (EPIC).
- **New Magic Candy**: for Mercurial Knight Cookie.
- **Also**: one new Treasure, plus a Bouchéael Cookie-themed Castle and Fountain design.

Bouchéael Cookie is the headline, with a LEGENDARY rarity and a themed Castle and Fountain to go with it. The Magic Candy for Mercurial Knight Cookie is the change that affects existing teams, since it upgrades a Cookie many players already own.

Kingdom Policies is a new system, but the notes don't explain how it works. The in-game notice should cover it, and it's worth reading before you commit resources, since new kingdom systems tend to compete with Cookie upgrades for the same materials.
