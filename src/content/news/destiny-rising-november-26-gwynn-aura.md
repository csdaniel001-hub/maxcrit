Gwynn players, this one's for you. Destiny: Rising's November 26 update doubles the damage on two of her core abilities, gives her a new Character Aura built around marking enemies, and puts her on the limited Memento Mori draw until December 24.

The numbers are big. Void Dance goes from 2,400 to 4,800 at max level and Void Spirit from 2,000 to 4,000. Soul Harvest climbs from 3,200 to 5,000, a 56.25% jump, and level 5 Penetrating Shot rises from 60% to 80%.

The new Aura, Rending Wound, ties those changes together. Hit an enemy with Void Dance and Gwynn deals 20% more damage to it for 10 seconds. At Amplification Lv2 that bonus becomes 50%, and against marked enemies her Sidearm spends 15% of its magazine to generate a spirit instead of 30%. Lv2 is where the Aura turns into a real upgrade, so that's the first stop for your Light Crystals. You unlock the Aura through its quests, then spend Light Crystals across six amplification levels.

If you don't own her yet, Memento Mori is the draw to watch: every Mythic character it gives you is Gwynn, and pity is shared across all limited-time draws, so progress from earlier banners carries over. Daily Drill runs until December 10 alongside it. Scores use Fair Play stats, faster clears score higher and specific kills earn bonus points, so speed and target picks decide your ranking.

Maintenance starts November 26 at 10:00 UTC and should take about three hours. Accounts created before it that log in within a week get 200 Lumia Leaves by mail. Season of Revival now runs to December 24, so there's a little more time to finish it.
