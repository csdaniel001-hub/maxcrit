Pokémon GO's Tropical Paradise event runs from December 1 at 10:00 a.m. to December 7 at 8:00 p.m. local time, with two headline additions: Pyukumuku's debut and a Super Max Level for Mega Slowbro. Most of the event, from the GO Pass to the Field Research, is built around getting Slowbro Mega Energy.

Pyukumuku arrives in three-star raids and through the event GO Pass, and it can be Shiny. It also gets a new Fast Attack, Soak, with 6 power in both Trainer Battles and raids.

## Mega Slowbro reaches Super Max

Slowbro's Mega Level can go past Max for the first time. It has to reach Max, the third level, before Super Max opens up, and Mega Evolving alone won't get it there: you also have to spend Slowbro Mega Energy.

While Mega Evolved, Slowbro gets an extra Charged Attack, Chilling Water+. It has 45 power in Trainer Battles with a guaranteed one-stage Attack drop on the opponent, and 160 power in raids, and its power rises with Mega Level.

## GO Pass bonuses and deadlines

The free GO Pass gives double Raid XP from Rank 10. Rank 20 adds 5× Mega Energy from exploring with Slowpoke, Slowbro or Slowking as your buddy, provided you've Mega Evolved Slowbro at least once. That bonus only pays out while you walk, so reaching Rank 20 early in the week gives you more days to use it.

GO Pass Deluxe costs US$4.99, or US$6.99 for Deluxe + 6 Ranks, which starts you at Rank 7; prices can vary by region. Pass rewards expire on December 9 at 8:00 p.m. local time, two days after the event ends, so anything unclaimed by then is lost.

## Spawns, raids and research

| When (local time) | Wild encounters |
|---|---|
| From December 1, 10:00 a.m. | Slowpoke, Shellder, Wingull, Slakoth, Snorlax and other event Pokémon |
| December 4, 10:00 a.m. to December 7, 8:00 p.m. | Adds Hoppip, Lotad, Shroomish and Alolan Exeggutor |

One-star raids feature Alolan Diglett, Frillish (male and female) and Sandygast, with Pyukumuku in three-star raids. Event Field Research can reward Psyduck, Tentacool and Shellder encounters, and some tasks give Slowbro Mega Energy.
