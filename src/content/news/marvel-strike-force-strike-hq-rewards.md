Scopely renamed its MARVEL Strike Force website STRIKE HQ on November 24, giving players new reasons to visit outside the game. One reward to collect is 1,000 Power Cores for adding the site's shortcut to your home screen. That reward is restricted to one claim per account, even across several devices.

Separately, the limited-time code `STRIKEHQ` gives 5 million Gold, 700 Strike Points and an exclusive Devil Dinosaur portrait. Scopely hasn't given an end date, so redeem it soon.

The website's free daily claims are now joined by daily and weekly web milestones and a new Free Claim Streak. Open the streak card at the bottom right of the Web Store and check its requirements each day. Strike Points earned through purchases are restricted to the Web Store; the promo code is an additional source of points.

To add the shortcut, visit [STRIKE HQ](https://marvelstrikeforce.com/) in your mobile browser and sign in. Use the share menu, find Add to Home Screen (on Apple devices, expand View More first), and enable Open as Web App where the option is available. Adding a second shortcut will not grant another 1,000 Cores.

The roster tools are the more durable reason to keep the shortcut. You can sync the characters you own, save squads and use Planners to organize upgrades. Alliance tools add member search, activity checks through Alliance History and Alliance Merge.
