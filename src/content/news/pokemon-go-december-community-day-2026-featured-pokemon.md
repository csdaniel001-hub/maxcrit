The Pokémon GO team has announced December Community Day 2026, and as in past years it is a recap event. Pokémon from this year's Community Days return over two days, Saturday, December 12, and Sunday, December 13, 2026, from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. local time each day.

Shiny odds match the original Community Days, and some of these Pokémon can appear with a Special Background. Outside the three-hour windows, any of the featured Pokémon may still appear from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. local time on either day.

## Which Pokémon appear on each day

| Day | Featured Pokémon | May also appear |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday, December 12 | Vulpix, Alolan Vulpix, Grookey, Scorbunny, Lechonk, Tinkatink | Piplup |
| Sunday, December 13 | Zorua, Pawniard, Sobble, Nickit, Frigibax | Gible, Deino |

All of them can be Shiny. If you can only play one afternoon, the split matters: the two Galar starters you need for Rillaboom and Cinderace are on Saturday, while Inteleon's Sobble, the Pawniard line for Kingambit and the Frigibax line for Baxcalibur are on Sunday.

## Featured attacks

Evolving these Pokémon from Saturday 9:00 a.m. to Sunday 9:00 p.m. local time gets you the move from their 2026 Community Day. The window covers both days, so you can catch on Saturday and evolve on Sunday.

|---|---|---|
| Vulpix | Ninetales | Energy Ball (Charged) |
| Alolan Vulpix | Alolan Ninetales | Chilling Water (Charged) |
| Prinplup | Empoleon | Hydro Cannon (Charged) |
| Gabite | Garchomp | Earth Power (Charged) |
| Zorua | Zoroark | Sucker Punch (Fast) |
| Zweilous | Hydreigon | Brutal Swing (Charged) |
| Thwackey | Rillaboom | Frenzy Plant (Charged) |
| Raboot | Cinderace | Blast Burn (Charged) |
| Drizzile | Inteleon | Hydro Cannon (Charged) |
| Nickit | Thievul | Icy Wind (Charged) |
| Lechonk | Oinkologne | Mud Slap (Fast) |
| Tinkatuff | Tinkaton | Gigaton Hammer (Charged) |
| Bisharp | Kingambit | Kowtow Cleave (Charged) |
| Arctibax | Baxcalibur | Glaive Rush (Charged) |

Kingambit also gets a temporary shortcut. With Bisharp as your buddy, you only need to defeat one Dark- or Steel-type Raid Boss to evolve it. The usual requirement returns on December 13 at 9:00 p.m. local time.

## Bonuses

During the 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. windows on both days:

- 2× XP, 2× Stardust and 2× Candy for catching Pokémon
- 2× chance of Candy XL from catches for Trainers level 31 and up
- 1/2 Hatch Distance for Eggs placed in Incubators during the event
- Incense lasts three hours (Daily Adventure Incense excluded)

These run all weekend, from Saturday 9:00 a.m. to Sunday 9:00 p.m. local time:

- Lure Modules last one hour
- Up to two Special Trades per day
- Trades cost 50% less Stardust

## Special Research and Web Store box

The December Community Day Special Research costs US$4.99, or the equivalent tier in your currency. It gives encounters with 2026 Community Day and Community Day Classic Pokémon with Special Backgrounds, one Elite Charged TM, one Elite Fast TM, a Rocket Radar and more. It has no medal. You can gift a ticket to a friend at Great Friends level or higher, as long as they don't already have one.

The Pokémon GO Web Store will also sell an Ultra Community Day Box for US$4.99 during the event, with one Premium Battle Pass and one Special Research ticket.

Free Field Research during the event leads to encounters with the same 2026 Community Day Pokémon. Checking in at a Community Ambassador event adds Timed Research with more encounters and premium items.

The two Elite TMs are the strongest reason to pay. If you missed a 2026 Community Day move, the free featured attacks above already cover it this weekend, so the TMs are better kept for a Pokémon outside this list.
