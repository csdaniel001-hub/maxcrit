Azur Promilia, the creature-companion fantasy RPG from Azur Lane developer Manjuu, went live on November 26 in Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and mainland China on PC, iOS and Android. Players who want it in English have longer to wait: Manjuu's English open beta is planned for summer 2027, on a dedicated US server.

The Japanese service opened at 11:00 JST (02:00 UTC), and one account shares its progress across the Windows, Android and iOS versions. A PlayStation 5 version is in development, but it wasn't part of this launch.

Kibo are the hook. These creatures fight alongside your characters and also work at home, gathering materials, crafting and farming, so collecting them feeds both your team and your base.

## Phone requirements are high

The Japanese launch FAQ asks for a lot more than most mobile RPGs. Check your phone before you download, because the game also wants 55 GB of free space on both platforms.

| | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Android OS | Android 10 or later | Android 10 or later |
| Android RAM | 6 GB | 8 GB |
| Android chipset | Snapdragon 855, Dimensity 1000+, Kirin 990 | Snapdragon 8 Gen 1, Dimensity 9000+ |
| iPhone | iPhone 12 Pro | iPhone 13 Pro or newer |
| iOS | iOS 15 or later | iOS 15 or later |
| Free storage | 55 GB or more | 55 GB or more |

The English beta will run on its own US server. Manjuu hasn't said whether progress from the Asian servers will carry over, so treat an Asian account as a separate start if you plan to switch to English later.
