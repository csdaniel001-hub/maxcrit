Pokémon Sleep's next Super Skill Week gives players a reason to reconsider their research island. Hisuian Growlithe and Hisuian Arcanine arrive on December 7, with increased encounter rates during the event and a new support skill.

Super Skill Week Part 4 begins December 7 at 4 a.m. and ends December 14 at 3:59 a.m. in all research areas. Helpers with the Skills specialty have a 1.5-times main skill activation chance and gain three levels on activation, subject to the normal skill level cap. The two newcomers arrive later on the opening day, at 3 p.m.

## Where to research the new arrivals

Both Hisuian forms are Rock-type Snoozing Pokémon. They can appear at Greengrass Isle, Taupe Hollow, Snowdrop Tundra, Greengrass Isle (Expert) and Cyan Beach (Expert). The last of those has the highest encounter chance for the new arrivals. Regular Cyan Beach is absent from their listed habitats.

Their new main skill immediately triggers a regular help action, and each time it does, the helping Pokémon may get a bonus that grants one extra activation of its main skill. The chance depends on that Pokémon's normal main skill trigger rate. The bonus cannot stack on a helper that already has it, and conducting sleep research or moving to a different research site removes it.

## Mini Candy Boost returns

During the event, Mini Candy Boost doubles the EXP obtained from each candy while costing four times the usual Dream Shards per candy. The limit is 50 candies per day, and the feature is enabled by default. Switch it off on the Level Up screen when you want to conserve shards.
