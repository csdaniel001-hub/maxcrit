Dicevaders has arrived on Android and iOS alongside the game's November 26 content update. The mobile release uses a try-before-you-buy model: downloading and starting the game is free, while the full game requires a single purchase.

Pengonauts' roguelike deckbuilder puts the player in charge of an invading alien army. The battlefield is a turn-based grid, where arranging units and dice can create scoring combinations. Chrono allows players to reroll dice or refresh the shop, while budges can alter dice values to line up combinations. Those tools give players ways to salvage awkward rolls and build around the units they have drafted.

The release coincides with update 1.1 on Steam and Switch. That update adds the Commander Phantom, whose Echo From Beyond perk creates Echo Dice that score again on the following turn. Planning around those extra scoring opportunities is central to Phantom's playstyle. Gauntlet Mode offers an additional challenge for experienced players, while Planet X introduces tougher endgame scaling and a new final boss.

Players can download Dicevaders from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playstack.dicevaders) or find it on the App Store under Dicevaders - Try & Buy.
