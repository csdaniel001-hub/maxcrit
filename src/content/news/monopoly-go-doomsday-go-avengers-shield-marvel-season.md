MONOPOLY GO! has gone full Marvel. Doomsday Go!, the new season running from November 25, 2026 to January 27, 2027, hands every player who passes board 4 a free Avengers Shield, and Scopely has built a 23-set sticker album and a handful of new mechanics around the heroes and villains.

The addition regular Treasures players will notice is Super Hero Smash. Depending on its Charge, it randomly blows up between 25% and 100% of the remaining tiles, which could take a lot of the grind out of the last stretch of a treasure board.

Collectors get a new chase item too. Super Stickers are ultra-rare, visually enhanced versions of select stickers, and each one unlocks an exclusive Super Token cosmetic. The album runs from Thor and Iron Man to Deadpool, Wolverine, Spider-Man and the X-Men.

Doctor Doom shows up in person for a Coin Flip battle on the board, the seasonal boards move to Doomstadt and the X-Mansion, and new Super Hero Coins each come with their own twist.

If you're coming back after a break, the shield is the easy win: it's available from the start of the season, and all it takes is getting past board 4 before January 27.
