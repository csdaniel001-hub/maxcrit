Limbus Company's November 26 update adds Schemacraft Beta, a new mode where you draft a roster of Identities and send it through automated battles, plus two new 3★ Identities from the Haute Couture line. Maintenance runs on November 26 from 01:00 to 03:00 UTC, and every account created before December 3 gets 300 Lunacy for it.

## Haute Couture Gregor and Yi Sang lead the new extraction

The new Identity Target Extraction features two Season 8 Identities:

- Haute Couture::Le Rouge B2F Skein Nest Gregor
- Haute Couture::Le Noir B3F Drying Room Yi Sang

It runs from November 26 at 03:00 UTC until December 10 at 01:00 UTC. Two more Target Extractions share that window: one for Rodion that also boosts three Rodion E.G.O (Indicant's Trial, Pursuance and Into the Sunset), and one for The Middle Identities.

The Overseer Announcer can drop from all of these and from the Standard Extraction during the same period. Its rate comes out of the 2★ and 1★ Identity rates, so your odds of a 3★ Identity or E.G.O stay the same. After December 10 it leaves the pools, though Project Moon says it may return through Yisang Exchange in certain future Target Extractions, and the Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange] can also pick it.

Two limited packs sit alongside the banner until December 10 at 01:00 UTC, each priced at $21.99:

- **Target Extraction Pack**: 4 Decaextraction Tickets, buyable once.
- **Identity Growth Pack**: Takeoff Module - ID Uptie x1, Identity Training Ticket IV x90 and Identity Training Ticket III x40, buyable twice.

## Schemacraft Beta unlocks after Chapter 10

You need every stage of Chapter 9.5 "Mnestic Experience" and Chapter 10 "The Gaze Bearing" cleared. The mode then opens from the Schemacraft button on the Drive tab and costs no Enkephalin or Enkephalin Modules to play.

Schemacraft has its own pass. Runs earn Schemacraft Pass EXP, and nothing you do there raises your Limbus Pass level or counts toward Limbus Pass missions. Project Moon calls this a playable beta that it will keep adjusting after launch, and warns that the preview images may differ from the final version. Our [Schemacraft Beta guide](/hub/guides/limbus-company-schemacraft-guide.html) covers team building, dice rolls, E.G.O resources and the pass in detail.

## 4th Match Flame gets Synergy effects

The Yi Sang, Ryōshū and Rodion versions of 4th Match Flame gain a Synergy effect. Project Moon corrected its notice on this point: the first version said Synergy would only activate at Threadspin Tier 4, while the amended note says activation conditions may vary for each E.G.O. Existing E.G.O at Threadspin Tier 5 will get Synergy effects in future updates.

E.G.O animations also get a fast-forward setting. Tapping or clicking during an animation now plays it at the speed you pick in settings: x2.0 (the default), x2.5 or x3.0. Until now PC ran at x2.0 and mobile at x3.0, so mobile players who liked the faster speed need to set x3.0 themselves after the update.

## Claim dates for the Lunacy

| Gift | Contents | Claim by (UTC) |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance compensation | Lunacy x300 | December 3, 01:00 |
| Project Moon 10th Anniversary gift | Lunacy x1300 and a 3★ Guarantee Decaextraction Ticket | December 17, 01:00 |

The compensation arrives in the in-game Mailbox after the update and can be claimed once per account. The anniversary gift has been available since the November 19 update and only needs a login before its deadline.
