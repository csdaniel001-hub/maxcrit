Mega Clefable makes its Pokémon GO debut on Saturday, November 28, 2026, in a Super Mega Raid Day running from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. local time. Shiny Clefable is possible, at boosted odds from Super Mega Raids.

## Super Max Level and Moonblast+

Mega Clefable is the first Pokémon whose Mega Level you can raise to Super Max. At that level it can learn an extra Charged Attack while Mega Evolved, Moonblast+:

- **Trainer Battles**: 80 power, with a chance to lower the opponent's Attack by one stage.
- **Raid Battles**: 160 power.

Moonblast+ gets stronger as the Mega Level rises. You need to reach Max, the third level, before you can push to Super Max, and Mega Evolving alone won't get you there: you have to spend Clefable Mega Energy, which comes from Mega Raids. Clefable caught in Super Mega Raids during the event already have Mega Level 1 unlocked.

## The Global Challenge

Players worldwide complete Raid Battles from Friday, November 27 at 5:00 p.m. PST to Saturday, November 28 at 8:00 p.m. PST. The more tiers they clear, the bigger the bonus. From November 30 at 12:00 p.m. PST to December 13 at 12:00 p.m. PST, Moonblast+ gets a base power boost on any Mega Clefable at Mega Level 1 or higher, and Super Max Mega Clefable gets a combat power boost. Neither bonus applies in GO Battle League.

## Bonuses, research and the ticket

Free for everyone:

- Remote Raid limit raised to 20, from November 27 at 5:00 p.m. to November 28 at 8:00 p.m. PST.
- Up to six free Raid Passes from spinning Gym Photo Discs.
- Timed Research with a Premium Battle Pass and a Metagross encounter.

The US$4.99 ticket is in the in-game shop until November 28 at 5:00 p.m. local time. It gives up to 14 Raid Passes from Gyms, a higher chance of Rare Candy XL from raids, and an extra 5,000 XP and 5,000 Stardust per Super Mega Raid. It can't be bought with PokéCoins, and you can gift it to Great Friends or higher.

Super Mega Raids only appear at certain Gyms. The in-game Map shows where they may spawn and lists nearby community meetups in advance, which helps, since these raids need a group.

Six free passes across a three-hour window is plenty of raiding for most groups. The ticket earns its US$4.99 only if your group can realistically clear more than six raids in that time.
