King has put eight Xbox franchises into Candy Crush Saga for Game On Season, its celebration of 25 years of XBOX. The season runs until December 6, 2026. King's release, dated November 12, says it launched that day, but its own "Play" line gives November 9 as the start, so if you play daily it has probably been running in your game for longer.

## Pins, Leagues and rewards

The centre of the season is an XBOX 25 pin collection. The pins show characters and moments from all eight franchises, Halo, Fallout, Sea of Thieves, Forza, DOOM, Banjo-Kazooie, Crash Bandicoot and Spyro, with examples like the Master Chief's helmet, a Cacodemon and Dr. Neo Cortex. You earn them by clearing levels and build the set across the four weeks.

There's also an arcade-themed Leagues event and exclusive rewards for the anniversary. King hasn't listed the individual rewards in the release.

Some of the pins also exist as real ones. VIP players in the US can enter a contest at candycrush.com for one of 1,000 limited-edition pin packs.

## Candyfied music

Six of the franchises got their music reworked in Candy Crush style: Halo, Fallout, Sea of Thieves, Forza, DOOM and Banjo-Kazooie. Composer Federico Truzzi arranged them, and they were recorded by the German Film Orchestra Babelsberg, the BoChorBerlin choir and an accordion player. You hear them while you play, and the album is also on Spotify and other streaming services if you want to listen outside the game.

Crash Bandicoot and Spyro appear in the pin collection but not in the music.

Game On Season is available on iOS, Android, Amazon and the Windows App store. With two weeks left, the pins are the part with a deadline. Once the season ends on December 6 there's no word on whether the collection comes back.
