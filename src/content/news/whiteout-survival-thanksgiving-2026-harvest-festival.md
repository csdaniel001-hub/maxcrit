Whiteout Survival's Thanksgiving event has a useful reward for Chiefs outside the highest leaderboard ranks. The Harvest Festival Menu includes a points target that can award a permanent City Skin, and the event remains open until November 30 at 23:59:59 UTC.

The important limitation is alliance membership. At the start of Harvest Festival Menu, Alliance Fealty links you to your current alliance. Submitting dishes earns Alliance Dining Points while you remain in that alliance. Moving to a different alliance during the event prevents your submissions from earning those points for the new group.

## Thanksgiving event deadlines

| Activity | UTC deadline |
|---|---|
| Harvest Festival Menu, Gourmet Rally, Gourmet Quest, King of the Meadows, Fertile Symphony | Nov 30, 23:59:59 |
| Kasia's Wish House | Nov 30, 23:59:59 |
| Top 10 alliance feast hosting window | Dec 1, 23:59:59 |

The official event table gives Harvest Festival itself a broader December 1–2 window, but its hosting instructions specify December 1 for starting the one-hour feast. Eligible alliances should arrange their timing around the stricter start-day instruction. Members need to belong to the qualifying alliance when the menu leaderboard settles to attend its feast. Members who leave during the feast can rejoin their original eligible alliance and resume participation while that feast is still active.

For players seeking the permanent City Skin, hitting the points target matters independently of the ranking tiers: places 11–25 have their temporary skin upgraded, while players outside the top 25 gain an additional permanent skin after reaching the target. King of the Meadows has a separate permanent Name Card target.

Gourmet Quest ingredients can come from beast hunts, gathering, gem spending and Speedups. Use activities that already fit your growth plan before committing resources solely to a leaderboard. Fertile Symphony rewards are available through completed rows and columns, and earned but unclaimed rewards are mailed afterward. Unused Gourmet Bags are converted to 10K Secured Resource Supply Chests after the event. Some rewards depend on server progress. Players who primarily want cosmetics should compare their points against the permanent reward thresholds shown in the event, since the permanent reward depends on reaching its points threshold.
