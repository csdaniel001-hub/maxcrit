Azur Lane's November 24 update opened The Transfer Student of Veritas Academy, an event running until December 9 at 23:59 UTC-7. Molotov and Sinop feature in limited construction, while Imperatritsa Mariya is obtainable through accumulated event PT.

The event has two routes through its content. Operation Mode supplies event points for the milestone rewards; completing Story Mode awards a limited gear skin. The PT reward period extends until December 16 at 23:59 UTC-7, a week after the event stages close. Finish the required farming before December 9 and leave the later date for claiming earned rewards.

The associated Commemorative Album also runs through December 9. Complete its missions to collect stickers for the Veritas Academy Medal. Players can earn three outfit rental vouchers during the event.

Rapid Training is on a different schedule, ending December 15 at 23:59 UTC-7. It includes Skill Books and 20 Wisdom Cubes through login, and allows ten instant Tactical Class completions per day using Skill Books. The December Cruise Missions start December 1, with Kersaint META and Specialized Bulin Custom MKIII among the regular-track rewards.
