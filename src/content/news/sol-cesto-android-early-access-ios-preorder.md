Sol Cesto has opened Android early access, while iPhone and iPad players can pre-order the mobile edition. Mobile publisher Snapbreak Games announced the rollout on November 26 for the game by Géraud Zucchini and Chariospirale, with the full mobile launch planned for the first quarter of 2027. The US App Store currently lists February 24, 2027 as its expected release date.

The dungeon roguelite makes probability part of its combat system. Instead of selecting a precise tile, players choose a row and let the game determine which of its four spaces they land on. A row containing treasure, enemies and traps can offer a worthwhile reward at the risk of taking damage. Choosing a less dangerous row can be more valuable than chasing loot when health is low.

The published feature list includes five distinct biomes and seven playable characters. Stone teeth alter probabilities and rules, while metal teeth create curses and combinations. Items offer additional ways to deal with enemies or avoid dangerous encounters.

## Mobile availability

| Platform | Availability |
|---|---|
| Android | Early access on Google Play |

The mobile release uses a free trial with a single payment to unlock the complete game. The US App Store lists the full-game unlock at $6.99.
