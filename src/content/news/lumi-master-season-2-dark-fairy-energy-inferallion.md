Lumi Master is changing the pace of its monster collection with Season 2, Dark & Fairy, which launches today, November 27. Bilibili promises a 50% increase in free Energy acquisition compared with Season 1, alongside a new Royal Lumi named Inferallion. The changes are meant to ease the stamina management that slowed collecting in Season 1.

## Where the extra Energy comes from

| Reward source | Season 2 change |
|---|---|
| Four new activities and systems | More than 60,000 Energy in total |
| Season Pass | 3.8 times the previous Energy rewards |
| Lumi-dex completion | 38,400 cumulative free Energy |
| Returning and veteran players | Additional 999 Energy |

These rewards come from separate activities and progression milestones. In particular, the Lumi-dex total requires collection progress.

All players who log in during the launch promotion can receive an S2 Shiny Lumi Egg. Inferallion provides another collection target, but Bilibili has not specified its acquisition cost or odds in this announcement.

Energy funds random exploration encounters, including creature encounters and resource gathering. The expanded rewards therefore matter most to players whose collection progress has been limited by their exploration budget. Completing collection milestones and checking the new activity rewards are sensible early-season priorities before spending resources elsewhere.
