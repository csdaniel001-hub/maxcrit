Albion Online's Dragonfire Patch 5 rebuilds the Crystal League for the extended Season 35. Matches in both 5v5 and 20v20 now last at most 15 minutes instead of 25, there are three matches an hour, and a guaranteed reward now drops even when you lose. The patch also adds a new top tier to the Conqueror's Challenge, doubles the loot in Azaryon's chest and fixes three mobile interface bugs.

Season 35 runs three weeks longer than a normal season, to avoid an overly long offseason afterwards. Most of the patch is built around that.

## Crystal League changes

| | 5v5 | 20v20 |
|---|---|---|
| Max match length | 25 → 15 minutes | 25 → 15 minutes |
| Levels | 8 and 9 removed; loot pays out in full at Level 7 | IP soft cap now 900 at Level 1 to 1500 at Level 7, matching 5v5 |
| Map and scoring | Unchanged | New Tristone map; Runestones 5 → 3; tickets lost per death 3 → 4 |

Both formats now run three matches per hour, and the Crystal Gladiator Helm becomes a rare reward for winning a Level 7 match in either one. The season also gets one extra Level 7 match. In 20v20, losing a Runestone costs 30 points, two cost 40 and three cost 50.

A 15-minute cap leaves far less time to recover from a bad opening, and with fewer Runestones on the 20v20 map each one swings more of the score. Teams that relied on long comebacks will want their first engagement planned before the match starts.

## Season 35 rewards

The Conqueror's Challenge gets a new top level at 2,000,000 Might, rewarding the Darkfang Swiftclaw skin. Rewards from Conqueror's Chests go up by about 50% at every level. A new Crystal League Might Challenge is worth 19,800 Season Points.

## Dragon Sanctuary loot doubles

The developers say the Dragon Sanctuary fights are in a good place but the rewards weren't worth the gear and time. Azaryon's chest now holds double the loot value overall, with more generous progression at higher tiers, and the team says it will keep watching loot values.

The Armory adds categories for the Ancient Lands and the Dragon Sanctuary. Arena, Crystal Arena, Crystal League, Hellgates and Corrupted Dungeons now show stats per match with a win rate, and the Mists and Open-World Solo categories get an Escapability metric.

## Balance and quality of life

The combat pass covers Arcane, Fire, Frost, Holy and Nature Staffs, War Gloves and all Cloth Armor. Evensong's Dark Sphere becomes a damage option, with impact damage up from 112 to 179 and a new damage-over-time effect, while Dawnsong's Flaming Phoenix drops from 15 m to 12 m range. Frost Beam's radius grows from 4 m to 5 m, and Glacial Obelisk now shields for 200 on recast instead of dealing damage.

Elsewhere, spells can be swapped by right-clicking each slot on the spell bar, guild invites show the Guild Tax rate and active member count, and training islands get shrines that reset health, energy and cooldowns.

Mobile players get three fixes:

- Holding an icon on the minimap now shows its tooltip.
- The HUD Tracker no longer loses its Destiny Board elements, or its collapse option, after you change regions.
- Double-tapping to equip or loot an item no longer opens Item Details by mistake.
