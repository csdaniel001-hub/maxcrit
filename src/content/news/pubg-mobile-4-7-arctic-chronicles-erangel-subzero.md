Winter has taken over PUBG MOBILE: in Version 4.7, on the App Store since November 12, 2026, the Ancient Beast of the new Arctic Chronicles mode covers the entire map, and a blizzard-hit Erangel follows in December. The update also brings a PvE map, interface changes and the Bentley collaboration that went live on November 20.

## Arctic Chronicles and Erangel Subzero

Arctic Chronicles is this update's Battle Royale themed mode. The Ancient Beast has taken over the whole map, and its body is a set of interactive areas you can explore from head to tail. PowNin, the penguin ally, is back to fight alongside you.

Erangel Subzero is the December arrival. Erangel goes through drastic weather changes during a blizzard, pitched as new tactics on a familiar map. For squads who know Erangel's rotations by heart, that is the part of 4.7 most likely to change how a match plays out.

The App Store screenshots also show a fishing activity marked as a Classic Mode update, and a Godzilla collaboration.

## Seal City and WOW Brainrot

Seal City is a new PvE map, an abandoned city with a day-night cycle. Enemy behavior changes between day and night, and the loop is loot, fight, extract. It's pitched as easier to pick up and faster paced than what came before.

WOW Brainrot gets snowy-themed content and new gameplay. It's listed as coming soon, and the store screenshots label it "available in December."

## Interface and event changes

- **Chicko's Loot**: a new redemption system that collects event rewards so you can redeem them in one place.
- **Activity Calendar**: shows all the new weekly content at a glance.
- **Interfaces**: X-Suit, Inventory, Workshop, Firearm, The Lab and Popularity Battle all got reworked screens.

Chicko's Loot is the one to open first, since it pulls event rewards into a single place to redeem.

A good part of 4.7 hasn't landed yet. Erangel Subzero and the Brainrot content are dated for December and the Godzilla collaboration is only teased, so expect the update to keep growing well after launch.
