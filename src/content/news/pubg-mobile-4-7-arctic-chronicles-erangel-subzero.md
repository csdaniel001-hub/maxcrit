PUBG MOBILE's Version 4.7 has been on the App Store since November 12, 2026. The official release notes describe a winter update with a new themed mode, a PvE map, interface changes and the Bentley collaboration that went live on November 20.

## Arctic Chronicles and Erangel Subzero

The Battle Royale themed mode this time is Arctic Chronicles. The Ancient Beast has taken over the entire map, and its body is a set of interactive areas you can explore from head to tail. PowNin, the penguin ally, is back to fight alongside you.

Erangel Subzero is coming in December. Erangel goes through drastic weather changes during a blizzard, and the developer pitches it as new tactics on a familiar map.

The App Store screenshots also show a fishing activity marked as a Classic Mode update, and a Godzilla collaboration with no date attached. The release notes themselves don't give a date for either.

## Seal City and WOW Brainrot

Seal City is a new PvE map, an abandoned city with a day-night cycle. Enemy behavior changes between day and night, and the loop is loot, fight, extract. The notes describe it as easier to pick up and faster paced than what came before.

WOW Brainrot gets snowy-themed content and new gameplay. The release notes say it is coming soon, and the store screenshots label it "available in December."

## Interface and event changes

- **Chicko's Loot**: a new redemption system that collects event rewards so you can redeem them in one place.
- **Activity Calendar**: shows all the new weekly content at a glance.
- **Interfaces**: X-Suit, Inventory, Workshop, Firearm, The Lab and Popularity Battle all got reworked screens.

A good part of 4.7 is still to come. Erangel Subzero and the Brainrot content are dated for December, and the Godzilla collaboration is only teased, so the update keeps adding content after launch.
