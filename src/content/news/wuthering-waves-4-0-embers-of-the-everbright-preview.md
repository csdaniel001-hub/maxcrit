Wuthering Waves Version 4.0, Embers of the Everbright, arrives on December 22, 2026 (UTC+8). Kuro Games' gameplay reveal introduces Anna and Lucielle as playable Resonators and shows the Strider Mechsuit in combat and exploration across the New Federation, a Western-inspired region where Rover's journey begins at Buffalo Rock.

## Seven Resonators are coming across Version 4.x

The December update starts the game's first Chapter of the Year, a single connected story that continues across updates through 2027. Kuro has revealed seven characters for that wider cycle, each with a two-part tagline. Only Anna and Lucielle are confirmed as playable in 4.0, so the list is not a seven-character launch banner.

| Resonator | Tagline | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anna | Roses & Desires | Playable in 4.0 |
| Lucielle | Future & Falsehoods | Playable in 4.0 |
| April | Truth & Illusion | Announced for 4.x |
| Gordia | Silent & Honed | Announced for 4.x |
| Cairn | Blood Debts & Purifying Rain | Announced for 4.x |
| IYA | UFOs & Sparkles | Announced for 4.x |
| Simulsys | Bones & Bygones | Announced for 4.x |

## How the Strider Mechsuit changes exploration

The Strider Mechsuit is a wearable mech with full 360-degree mobility. You can fly it across the new region, and the footage moves through coastlines, the ruins of a post-apocalyptic city and canyons under dynamic weather. It also teases underwater exploration for the first time, with a seamless transition into the water.

In combat, Anna and Lucielle each bring their own abilities and ultimate attacks, and Kuro points to coordinated attacks between the Resonators and the mech as a new option. The footage comes from a test build, so visuals and performance can still change before launch.

## What to do before December 22

Chapter IV Act V, A Bouquet for Tomorrow, is the main quest added in Version 3.8, and it introduces a man from the New Federation. Finishing it sets up the story, though Kuro hasn't announced what unlocks the new region.

If you're saving Astrite for Anna or Lucielle, their full kits and Convene details aren't out yet, and Kuro hasn't said in which 4.x update the other five arrive. Version 3.8 compensation of 600 Astrite, for accounts set up before the 3.8 update, stays claimable in your mail until the 4.0 maintenance, so collect it before December 22.
