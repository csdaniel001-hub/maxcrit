Reverse: 1999 Version 3.9 lets you claim up to 100 summons, 14 of them just for logging in, and it brought two 6-Star debuts when Bluepoch released it on November 5, 2026. The version also adds a new main story and a collaboration event, and that summon count puts it among the larger giveaways the game has run.

For a free player, 100 summons is a meaningful amount. Collect all of them before you decide whether to spend on either new character.

## Hedone and Narcissus

The two new characters are both 6-Star:

- **Hedone**, with the Mineral Afflatus.
- **Narcissus**, with the Beast Afflatus.

Two existing characters, Moldir and Noire, receive Character Enhancements in this version. If you already own either of them, look at their updated kits before you pull, because an enhanced character you have may cover the role you wanted a new one for.

## New story, events and Garments

The new story is called Rekindled! Games of the Summer-Gold Sea. Alongside it come these additions:

- **Ducks in the Suitcase**: a collaboration event.
- **A Series of Dusks · Chamber of Sounds**
- **Musae's Arcade**
- **Mane's Bulletin · Featured Observations**
- **A Flowing Feast**: a time-limited re-release.

The re-release matters if you joined the game after A Flowing Feast first ran, since it is a second chance at an event you missed.

Five characters get new Garments in Version 3.9: To Fathom the Depths for Enigma, Waltz of the Depths for Ms. Stranger, Full Quack Ahead for Brume, Front Page Exclusive for ONiON, and Repose Amidst the Roses for Moldir.

Moldir has the busiest version of anyone, with a Character Enhancement and a new Garment at once, so hers is the updated kit to read first.
