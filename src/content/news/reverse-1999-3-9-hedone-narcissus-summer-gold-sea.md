Bluepoch released Reverse: 1999 Version 3.9 on November 5, 2026. It brings a new main story, two 6-Star characters, a collaboration event and one of the larger summon giveaways the game has run.

## Up to 100 summons, with 14 for logging in

The release notes open with the offer: you can claim up to 100 summons during this version, and logging in gives you 14 of them. The notes don't break down where the other 86 come from, so expect them to be spread across the version's events and check each event page for its share.

For a free player, 100 summons is a meaningful amount. It is worth collecting all of them before you decide whether to spend on either new character.

## Hedone and Narcissus

The two new characters are both 6-Star:

- **Hedone**, with the Mineral Afflatus.
- **Narcissus**, with the Beast Afflatus.

Two existing characters, Moldir and Noire, receive Character Enhancements in this version. If you already own either of them, look at their updated kits before you pull, because an enhanced character you have may cover the role you wanted a new one for.

## New story and events

The new story is called Rekindled! Games of the Summer-Gold Sea. Alongside it, the notes list these additions:

- **Ducks in the Suitcase**: a collaboration event.
- **A Series of Dusks · Chamber of Sounds**
- **Musae's Arcade**
- **Mane's Bulletin · Featured Observations**
- **A Flowing Feast**: a time-limited re-release.

The re-release matters if you joined the game after A Flowing Feast first ran, since it is a second chance at an event you missed.

## New Garments

Five characters get new Garments in Version 3.9:

| Garment | Character |
|---|---|
| To Fathom the Depths | Enigma |
| Waltz of the Depths | Ms. Stranger |
| Full Quack Ahead | Brume |
| Front Page Exclusive | ONiON |
| Repose Amidst the Roses | Moldir |

The release notes don't give the banner dates for Hedone and Narcissus or the end dates of the events. The in-game event calendar has them.
