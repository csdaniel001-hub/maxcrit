Com2uS released Summoners War: Sky Arena v9.4.0 on November 20. The headline addition is Summoner Mastery, a permanent record of your account's progress. The update also brings a limited SWC Special Battle built around the SWC2026 World Finals and the schedule for Siege Battle Season 23, and it widens what you can play while Repeat Battle runs.

## How Summoner Mastery counts your progress

Mastery sits under Summoner's Tower > Profile > Mastery and grades your account across 10 categories. Scores and grades update instantly, while the overall Mastery rankings refresh periodically. Reaching a higher tier also completes every tier below it: a 24-second best time in Giant's Keep, for example, ticks off the 60, 50, 40, 35 and 25-second requirements at once.

Records are permanent. Rune Mastery progress stays even after you sell the rune that earned it, so there is no reason to hoard old gear for the score. For monster collection and progression categories, a Collab Monster and its original version count as the same monster.

How older progress carries over depends on the category. Com2uS lists five rules:

- **Applied immediately**: current progress counts as it stands.
- **Best records applied**: your highest grade and ranking to date count.
- **Counted after update**: earlier records don't count, so the content has to be replayed.
- **Progress from update onward**: earlier accumulated progress doesn't count.
- **Ongoing season**: progress from a season already running still counts, so 8 wins before the update plus 2 after makes 10. It registers once you play that content after the update.

Trial of Ascension falls under the replay rule for Normal and Hard: old clears don't count, and you need to clear the floors again. Clearing a higher floor completes the lower ones, so a Normal 100F clear also covers 20F, 40F, 60F and 80F. If you can already reach 100F, go straight for it. Hell mode works differently: play it once after the update and your previously earned Tower Tokens apply retroactively.

## SWC Special Battle and Siege Season 23

The SWC Special Battle runs from November 20 at 00:00 PST to December 6 at 06:59 PST (08:00 UTC to 14:59 UTC). It uses Single Pre-ban, with each player banning one monster before the fight. The Support Monsters are the ones fielded at the SWC2026 World Finals, already fitted with Runes, Artifacts and Relics, so you can try finals teams without building them. The SWC Special Battle Pass, whose rewards scale with cumulative Special Battle wins, stays on sale until December 13 at 06:59 PST (14:59 UTC), a week after the battle closes. Buy it early if you want it, since wins only count while the battle is running.

Siege Battle Season 23 runs from November 30 at 11:00 to February 26 at 22:59 server time, followed by the tournament from March 1 at 11:00 to March 20 at 23:59 server time. The reward and Support Monster lists were also updated. Season 12 Siege rewards return to the Guild Seal Shop from November 22, after the Season 22 Tournament: the Dragon Lord of Doom Demon transmogrification and the Magma of Enraged Earth and Dazzling Spotlight appearance effects.

## Teleport, Repeat Reappraisal and smaller changes

The Gateway > Teleport menu now lists everything you can enter while Repeat Battle runs, with remaining attempts and tickets: Arena, World Arena, Dimension Arena, Temple of Chaos, Hall of Heroes, Event Dungeons (Devilmon, Angelmon, Rainbowmon and Mana Stone Dungeon), World Boss, Trial of Ascension, Challenge of Ascension and Dimensional Rift. Temple of Chaos, Hall of Heroes and the Event Dungeons only appear while they are open.

Repeat Reappraisal can now target Rune Score, and Monster Collection shows where certain monsters come from: Scenario, Arena Shop, Guild Shop, Ancient Coin Shop, Secret Dungeons or the Fusion Hexagram. Dimension Arena gets labels for shared monsters and runes in chat, a battle counter on its rewards and owned Scrolls on the Summon button. Among the bug fixes, a lingering Provoke icon on Nobara Kugisaki, Aya and Kung Fu Girl was display-only, so Provoke was never actually applied.
