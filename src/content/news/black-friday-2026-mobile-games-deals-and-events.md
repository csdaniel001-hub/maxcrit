Black Friday falls on November 27 this year, and several mobile games have already announced deals and events around it. This list only includes what the developers themselves have announced, with each end date.

## Deals and events by game

| Game | What | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Pokémon GO | Double bonus PokéCoins on Web Store bundles over $19.99, up to 2,000 bonus coins | Until Nov 29, 8:00 p.m. PST |
| Pokémon GO | Seasonal Delights Box (6,095 PokéCoins), Web Store only, up to three per player | Until Nov 27, 8:00 p.m. PST |
| Asphalt Legends | Black Friday Spotlight with the Czinger 21C and McLaren Saber | Nov 23 – Dec 10 |
| Asphalt Legends | Bugatti Bolide Burst of Speed | Nov 26 – Dec 17 |
| Asphalt Legends | Cyber Monday event with the Trion Nemesis and Icona Vulcano Titanium | Nov 30 – Dec 5 |
| UNO! | Black Friday Bonanza: collect five stamps to spin a wheel for up to 6400% extra | Nov 23 – Nov 29 |
| Stumble Guys | Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Cyber Week events | Not dated in the notes |

## What's worth knowing

Most of these are events, not discounts. Asphalt's Black Friday Spotlight and Cyber Monday event are race events with featured cars, and UNO!'s Bonanza is a wheel you unlock with stamps. Pokémon GO's offer is the only straightforward price deal on the list, and it only applies on the Web Store, not in the in-game shop.

Pokémon GO's two offers end on different days. The Seasonal Delights Box goes on November 27 and the coin bonus on November 29, so if you want both, buy the coins first and the box before Friday evening.

UNO!'s "up to 6400% extra" is the top prize on the wheel, not the typical payout.

We'll add other games here as their developers announce Black Friday plans.
