abxylute has opened retail pre-orders for the M5 and M6 magnetic phone controllers following its crowdfunding campaign. The M5 with grip costs $84.99 in the US, while the M6 with grip is $89.99. The current store estimates dispatch in late January 2027 for M5 orders and late December 2026 for M6 orders.

The $5 difference buys a substantially different control layout. The M5 connects through USB-C and has no separate battery to charge. Its low-profile joysticks, tactile shoulder buttons and separated directional pad are aimed at players who want a compact setup. It also lets a compatible phone charge through the controller while playing.

The M6 connects over Bluetooth 5.3 and carries its own rechargeable 500 mAh battery. It has capacitive analog sticks, Hall-effect analog triggers with a shorter-travel mode, a continuous D-pad and detachable magnetic grips. Its analog trigger design makes it the more interesting of the two for racing games that accept variable throttle and braking input. That advantage depends on the game recognizing analog inputs.

Both controllers use a repositionable magnetic mount, with an included ring for compatible phones or cases without native magnetic attachment. Check your phone's camera placement and case before ordering, as the strength and balance of the mount vary with the hardware. The M5 additionally requires a compatible USB-C connection. A magnetic attachment alone does not make a game compatible with controller input.

The November 25 press release distinguishes the initial M6 backer shipments, expected to arrive in the first half of December, from orders placed through the retail site. Buyers ordering today should use the store's later dispatch estimate when planning their purchase. Neither estimate guarantees a delivery date.

For players deciding between the two, the practical choice is USB-C simplicity and charging on M5 versus wireless connectivity and analog triggers on M6. Both are still pre-orders, so battery life, comfort and input latency have yet to be reviewed.
