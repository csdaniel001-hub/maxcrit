Apple Arcade's December 3 drop has two names almost everyone will recognise: Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown+ and Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted. A roguelike spin on solitaire and a LEGO DUPLO game for younger players round out the four.

Prince of Persia arrives as the complete package, with all DLC included, the Mask of Darkness story expansion among it. If you skipped The Lost Crown the first time, there's nothing extra to buy.

Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted brings new levels and modes to the tower-defense classic, plus local co-op and PvP for two players on the same device. Its Party mode, which gives the backyard a makeover, is exclusive to Apple Arcade.

The wildcard is solitaire(ish), from Playstack, the publisher of Balatro. You stack cards to beat climbing score targets and upgrade your deck from more than 150 special cards. The Balatro comparison writes itself, but the card game underneath is solitaire, so expect a cousin, not a sequel.

LEGO DUPLO Playtime, from StoryToys, is the calm one: an open-ended digital toy chest where children build their own characters and worlds out of familiar bricks.

All four come with the subscription, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and one subscription covers a family of up to six.
