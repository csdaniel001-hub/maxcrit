Agent 47's latest assignment brings a character from 007 First Light into HITMAN World of Assassination. The Mutiny, an Elusive Target mission starring Lenny Kravitz as the Pirate King Bawma, is available from November 26 through December 24.

For mobile players, the notable part of IO Interactive's announcement is the explicit inclusion of iPhone and iPad, alongside Apple silicon Macs. The mission is available free during the promotion, including through the game's Free Demo on supported platforms. Android is absent from the platform list.

The assignment takes Agent 47 to Ambrose Island, where Bawma is directing an operation of his own. Kravitz reprises his performance from the studio's Bond game, supplying both his likeness and voice. Bawma communicates with 47 during the operation, and completing the mission unlocks The Buccaneer Suit with Gloves. Players with an IOI Account who play The Mutiny during its active period can also earn the Rock Royalty Duck. Owners of World of Assassination (Standard Edition or above), or players with Ambrose Island access on iOS and macOS, retain The Retaliator Arcade mission and the Special Assignment after the free Elusive Target expires.

A separate Rockstar Pack launches alongside the mission. It contains themed outfits, weapons and Freelancer Safehouse cosmetics. The pack is DLC; the free mission offer is separate. If the crossover is your reason for installing HITMAN on an Apple device, look for The Mutiny in the game or Free Demo before the December 24 deadline.
