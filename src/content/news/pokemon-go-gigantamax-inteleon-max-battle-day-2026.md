Gigantamax Inteleon appears in six-star Max Battles on December 5, with a chance to encounter its Shiny form for the first time in Pokémon GO. The main event lasts from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. local time, but the Max Particle preparation window starts much earlier.

## Plan your Max Particles before 2 p.m.

From midnight to 5 p.m. local time on December 5, exploring awards twice as many Max Particles at one-quarter of the usual required walking distance. Claim every pending Max Particle reward in the Nearby menu first to activate these bonuses. Adventure Sync can help register distance while exploring.

During the three-hour battle event, the collection limit increases to 1,600 and Power Spots award eight times their usual Max Particles. More Power Spots will be active, all will host Gigantamax Battles, and they refresh more frequently. Players can also make up to three Special Trades that day.

The Remote Raid limit increases from 10 to 20 in a separate global window: December 4 at 4 p.m. PST through December 5 at 8 p.m. PST (December 5 at 00:00 UTC through December 6 at 04:00 UTC). This raises the daily Remote Raid allowance; it does not grant free passes.

## What the $4.99 ticket adds

The optional US$4.99 ticket (or local equivalent) grants Timed Research with one Max Mushroom, 25,000 XP and 6,400 Max Particles, plus double XP from Max Battles. The research window matches the main 2–5 p.m. local-time event. Tickets are sold until 5 p.m. local time on December 5 and cannot be purchased using PokéCoins. The Web Store’s Ultra Ticket Box costs the same US$4.99 and adds 800 Max Particles.

If you want to make the most of the free bonuses, coordinate with other Trainers before the event and collect your pending exploration rewards before starting your reduced-distance walks. Six-star Max Battles are team encounters, so planning your group is as important as planning your particle budget.
