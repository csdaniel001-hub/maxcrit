BrownDust2 is calling its 3.5th anniversary its biggest giveaway yet: more than 1,200 pulls spread over 8 weeks, plus a free special skin for Popularity Poll winner Olivier, Magical Winter Olivier. NEOWIZ reveals the full update in a livestream on Friday, November 27 at 10:00 UTC, on the game's official YouTube and X channels.

The 8-week spread matters for planning. Those pulls are paced across all 8 weeks, so a Santa costume you want in the first weeks has to be budgeted from what you already hold plus whatever the early handouts turn out to be.

## When the livestream starts

The broadcast was announced for 7:00 PM KST, alongside a Director's Cut trailer posted on November 25 that teases a collaboration between director Soo-yeon Kim and game artist Origami.

## Pre-register for 10 Draw Tickets by December 3

Pre-registration on the anniversary site closes on December 3 at 02:00 UTC (11:00 AM KST). Enter an email address and tick the two required consent boxes, one for data processing and one for promotional messages, and the account earns Draw Ticket x10. The reward is limited to one per account and arrives after the anniversary update goes live, so the tickets won't be in your inventory before then.

## Christmas at the Noel Company

The season story, Operation Christmas, is set at the Noel Company toy factory, which delivers toys to the children of Carta City every Christmas. This year its employees start quitting for no apparent reason, and Vice President Rudolph calls in help to save the tradition.

Alongside it comes Honest Clause, a character story for Pure Santa Sacred Justia. Justia visits the factory to negotiate a contract, but a Ghenar executive wants to use the deal to steal Noel Company's patented automated distribution system and pushes her to become a corporate spy.

The anniversary site features four Santa costumes:

- Pure Santa Sacred Justia
- Miracle Santa Mamonir
- Dominant Santa Valze
- Override Santa Luvencia

The site doesn't give their banner order or dates, so if you're choosing between them, hold your pulls until Friday's broadcast.
