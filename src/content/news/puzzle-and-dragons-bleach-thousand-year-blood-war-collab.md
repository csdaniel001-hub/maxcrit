Puzzle & Dragons' BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War collaboration is live until December 6 at 17:59 PST, which is December 7 at 01:59 UTC. It brings nine collab-only dungeons, a BLEACH Egg Machine at 10 Magic Stones a pull, and an event quest whose 10 levels pay 1 Magic Stone each.

So the quest alone covers one pull. Before spending more, farm the free stuff. Beating the boss of Schrift "B", Jugram Haschwalth Descended! guarantees Jugram, and beating the boss of Founder of the Quincies, Yhwach Descended! guarantees Yhwach. Clearing a single floor of each Descended dungeon also unlocks an Egg Machine: the BLEACH [2020] machine from Jugram's, the new BLEACH machine from Yhwach's.

The BLEACH Colosseum has the best farming rule of the lot. Clear it with a BLEACH collab character as leader and the drop rate is 100%. In 3-Player Multiplayer every team has to run a collab leader to get it.

The premium machine features Ichigo Kurosaki & Orihime Inoue, Schrift "A", Uryu Ishida and Yhwach. Pull five at once and you also get five pulls from the BLEACH [2020] Bonus Egg Machine, so if you're going to spend, spend in fives.

On a tight Magic Stone budget, the order is quest, Descended bosses, Colosseum, then the machine. Plenty of the collection is free if you play the dungeons first.
