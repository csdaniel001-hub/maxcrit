Supercell has updated its Boat Game mobile listings, and Mobilegamer.biz reported on November 26 that the title is back in limited testing. The store descriptions now position it as an extraction shooter set across tropical islands, with players collecting loot and trying to reach an exit alive.

Each run begins with a hero selection and a mission to retrieve marked loot. Other players occupy the same world and can fight over items, including near the extraction point. Successful extraction lets players keep what they found. Loot and completed extractions award experience, which helps unlock heroes; existing heroes can also be upgraded for better stats.

The game is listed as free on the US App Store, where it requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or newer. Its Google Play listing shows a November 25 update. Players can look for the official listings, but access is limited: Mobilegamer.biz reports availability in the US, Canada, Mexico, Malaysia and the Philippines, with the exact testing arrangements still being investigated. A store listing alone does not guarantee a player can join the test, and Supercell has not announced a worldwide release date in these listings.
