MARVEL Future Fight's November 18 War of Kings update brought more than its 3 new heroes. Netmarble added 5 Comic Cards, automated Odin's Blessing awakening and fixed an issue with Type Enhancement damage for heroes at level 81 or above.

## New Comic Cards and artifacts

The War of Kings collection grants +3% Recovery Rate after all 5 cards reach Mythic grade. The Comic Card Chest in the Dimension Chest section is one acquisition route, while some cards are available through gameplay. Fantastic Four (1961) #49 has joined the Premium Comic Card Chest that uses Premium Comic Card Exchange Tokens. War of Kings (2009) #1 is excluded from that chest, and its Energy Attack increase activation rate option does not stack with the corresponding option on other cards.

The update also adds exclusive passive-skill Artifacts for Lilandra, Deathbird and Vulcan. Hulk and Vulcan also receive maximum-level expansions, with further hero level expansions planned across subsequent updates.

## Auto Awaken and combat fixes

Odin's Blessing Auto Awaken accepts selected Mythic Enchanted Uru, Odin's Blessing, Engraved Odin's Blessing and Odin's Glory as materials. It follows the chosen material list and attempt count, stopping on Awakening Super Success, exhaustion of those attempts or manual cancellation. Locked materials trigger a confirmation prompt. The option is also available on the Equip Uru screen.

Before using it, review the order of your materials. A locked item is still selectable with confirmation, so automatic awakening is useful for repeated attempts but does not replace checking what you are consuming.

The update changes Master Mold's summoning pattern in Giant Boss Raid and fixes a Phase 2 Onslaught issue affecting certain skills after the special pattern at 35% HP or lower. Dormammu's Ultimate Skill and side-view visual problems also received fixes. The Instinct slot now appears in the hero information pop-up.

A hero above level 80 may behave differently because the missing basic Type Enhancement advantage damage was fixed, independently of any new gear.
