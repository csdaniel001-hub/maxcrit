Riot's next Teamfight Tactics set, Prismatic Tour, goes live on December 2. It's a music festival theme, with traits built around bands and genres. The App Store notes for today's patch say this is the last Enchanted Wilds patch before the switch.

## Unlocks and Prismatic Quests

Unlocks return from Lore & Legends. Prismatic Tour has 94 units, and 32 of them only show up in your shop after you complete an in-game quest. Some are simple: you unlock Tibbers by fielding a two-star Annie holding an item. Others take more work: Mordekaiser needs 10 unique star levels of Pentakill units.

Riot sorts the unlocks into three kinds:

- **Flexible options**: units you can unlock at any point to round out your roster.
- **Capstones**: 5-costs locked behind playing their vertical trait, which are game-winning at full strength.
- **Chases**: unlocks that need a specific situation you won't see every game.

Prismatic Quests are new. They're rare, trait-specific quests you get through Augments, and finishing one grants that trait's prismatic tier. Riot says this lets emblems be stronger and more common without players hitting prismatic all the time. It hasn't revealed what the quests are.

There's also a dynamic music system: as you add units to your board, their music tracks blend into your own soundtrack during combat.

## The traits

Some of the bigger traits:

| Trait | Breakpoints | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| K/DA | 3, 5, 7, 10, 13, 15 | Releases songs based on total star level, gaining power for each at Combat Start and during combat |
| Heartsteel | 1, 3, 5, 7 | Team bonuses, plus a Bias to empower abilities at 3 and a second at 5 |
| Pentakill | 3, 5, 7 | Attack Damage and Ability Power, and the first enemies below a Health threshold are executed and replaced by allied ghouls |
| True Damage | 3, 5 | Earn Bling by winning on the A-Side or losing on the B-Side, then cash it in or keep rising |
| Gu Feng | 2, 3, 4 | Stats that scale with player level, and at 4 a Scroll, one of which opens a path to Level 11 |
| Rock | 3, 4, 5, 7 | Summons the Infernal Idol, which grows with your Rock champions |

The single-unit traits are the strangest part of the set. With Composer Malzahar, Mystical Instruments can appear in your shop, and collecting all five while meeting his damage requirement unlocks Baron Nashor, who takes two team slots as the One Monster Band.

Riot says there are about 100 new augments and item refreshes on top of this. With 32 units locked behind quests, the first days of the set will reward players who learn the unlock conditions, since a unit you never unlock never appears in your shop.
