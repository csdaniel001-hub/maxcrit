Capcom's Monster Hunter Rise is now available on phones and tablets through Netflix Games. The mobile edition brings the main game's hunt-and-craft loop to iPhone, iPad and Android devices, with access included in an active Netflix subscription.

The essential gameplay is unchanged in concept: take on quests, hunt monsters, collect materials and craft weapons and armor. The mobile version supports solo hunts and online co-op with up to three other players. The store listing confirms redesigned weapon-specific touch controls, a Recovery Button that selects a suitable item based on your hunter’s health and status, and access through progression to previously paid or time-limited Challenge and Event Quests. RPG Site reports that the release includes the base game's title updates, including Crimson Glow Valstrax.

There is one important distinction for portable play. Netflix's support page lists cloud saves but explicitly rules out offline play. If you are planning a long flight or commute without a stable internet connection, do not treat this version as an offline alternative to the console release. Netflix lists cloud saving for its own edition. If you have an existing Switch or PC save, treat the Netflix release as a separate version unless Capcom provides a migration option.

To install, use the [Netflix game download page](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/728129439873636) from a supported mobile device, or locate Monster Hunter Rise NETFLIX in the app store. Google Play recommends 8 GB of RAM and a Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1, and the iOS listing an A15 or M1 chip, so check the listing on your own phone before clearing storage.
