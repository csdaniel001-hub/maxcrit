Infantry players have two new Legendary heroes to chase. Call of Dragons 1.0.55, which reached the App Store on November 25, adds Bromgar and Feng I, two infantry artifacts, the Sabershark War Pet and an autumn event series built around seven days of logins.

The two heroes are built for different jobs, going by their tags. Bromgar is Infantry, PvP and Skills, and comes from the Lucky Spin and Wheel of Destiny events. Feng I is Infantry, Rally and Tank, and comes from Wheel of Destiny and Strongest Lord. If your alliance leans on you for field fights, Bromgar's tags point your way; if you lead rallies, Feng I's do.

The artifacts split the same way. Earthshaker (Infantry, PvP, Assault) comes from the Forge of Light and Riches of the Forest events, while Echo of Rancor (Garrison, Assault) is bought with Yggdra Amber in the Season Store, and an Exemplar of the same name sits in Exemplar Wish. Sabershark, an Infantry, Fighter and Skills War Pet, can be caught in the field.

The Autumnal Abundance series is the free part. Log in for 7 days for exclusive Avatar Frames and Nameplates, and work through Caravan Escort, the Alliance Quiz, Mineshaft Madness, the Lucky Ball Machine, Path of Diligence and the Festival of Fortitude. Blessing of Plenty, Black Friday Bonanza and Purchase Rewards are the paid side.

Collectors should look at the new Chest of Destiny rewards: the Sacred Covenant City Theme and its three matching cosmetics, the Prayer of Light Adornment, the Radiant Pilgrimage City Effect and the Blessing of Valor Relocation Effect.
