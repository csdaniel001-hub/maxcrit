Heartopia's second My Little Pony collaboration phase is underway, bringing back the first phase's collections alongside new outfits, vehicles and activities. XD Games says the event began November 21 and runs until December 27, though the game's Google Play event card lists December 18, so plan your purchases and collections for the earlier date.

Players can earn Friendship Sparkles from event tasks and contribute them toward a shared server goal to restore the Tree of Harmony. Cloud Gallop adds pony-themed parkour, including a developer-designed course and routes created by players. Finishing courses and the associated Rainbow Puzzles yields rewards such as Cupcake Food Recipes, Party Hats and a Spike Cushion Pet Bed.

New Princess Celestia and Princess Luna designs include outfits and vehicles obtainable through purchases or draws. Collecting every Celestial Pony-themed Vehicle awards a voucher for the Pony Magic Projector. The Mistmane and Photo Finish jumpsuits are available in the Acorn Store.

Returning players have an advantage if they already started their Phase 1 collection: the publisher confirms that previous collection progress carries forward. Earlier outfits, vehicles and the Spike pet costume return to the Exchange Store. Check existing collection progress before spending on any returning items.
