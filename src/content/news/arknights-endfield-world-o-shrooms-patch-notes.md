GRYPHLINE's full notes for the Meeting You in the World o'Shrooms update are out. The update is mandatory: iOS players update through the App Store, Android players through the in-game prompt. On mobile it needs about 4 GB plus another 4 GB of free space during extraction.

## Compensation: 1,800 Oroberyl in two mails

The pre-download notice promised 900 Oroberyl. The final notes add a second payment, so every character created before the update gets two separate mails once Mail unlocks:

- **Update & Maintenance Compensation**: Oroberyl x900
- **Bug Fix Compensation**: Oroberyl x900

Both expire at the next version update, so claim them early.

## Kay, Mimino and the banners

6★ Kay is the Rate-UP operator on the [Anvil, Backpack, Plus A Pot o'Honey] Chartered Headhunting until December 23, 11:59 server time. Before pulling, you can try her in the [Combat Drills] trial. Clearing it with the preset team gives Oroberyl x100, an Arsenal Defense Crate with a Fully Artificed Level 60 gear set and a Mark of Perseverance.

5★ Mimino costs nothing. From December 16, 12:00, the [Workers' Guards: Roll Call] giveaway hands out Mimino at max potential plus the 5★ Star of Tomorrow (Handcannon) for logging in and playing modes. The weapon banner, [Casual Roamer Issue], features the new 6★ Sweet Deal (Polearm). Gilberta leads a RE-Factor Headhunting until December 16, and Rossi takes over after that.

## Wuling - Old Dam and the new modes

The new area is Wuling - Old Dam. Its signature system is Waffle Piloting: you launch and steer honey waffles that collect Aurylenes, and dipping them in different spores changes what they do. Chilly spores, for example, reveal and dispel Delulu Walls.

Factory players get Exclusion Zone Rebuilding, a permanent mode that unlocks after the side mission Rebuilding a Home II. You produce supplies, deliver them to the Rebuilding Supply Port and raise Wuling's development. Wuling's Regional Development Level cap also goes up, and Sub-PAC can now be deployed in the Old Dam.

## Notable fixes

Operator ultimates could get interrupted abnormally, and that is fixed. 6★ Si had several combo and damage bugs fixed. 6★ Typhoeus's repeated combo casts now raise Nature Burst DMG taken as they should. In the factory, Item Control Ports and Pipe Control Ports can now be placed before they are connected and snap into place automatically.
