Hello Games has brought back four past No Man's Sky expeditions for the holidays, running back to back from November 25 to January 20. The game launched on iOS this year, so for iPhone and iPad players these are the first chance at rewards that were previously out of reach. Aquarius is live now.

| Expedition | Dates | Main rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Aquarius | November 25 to December 9 | Deep-sea diving suit |
| Remnant | December 9 to December 23 | Heirloom customisation set |
| Our Journey Continues | December 23 to January 6 | Diplodocus companion, golden edition of the Rasamama S36 |
| Swarm | January 6 to January 20 | Swarm-themed armour and equipment, plus a trophy base part for the most successful team |

Each expedition gets two weeks, and the next one starts the day the previous one ends, so each reward set is only available in its own window.

## What each expedition is about

Aquarius is a fishing run built around the legend of The Angler, with fish to catch and stories to gather. Remnant is a cross-country drive through a world of industrial waste, using the Gravitino Coil to collect material. Our Journey Continues is the anniversary expedition, moving through versions of the universe with anecdotes from the game's development. Swarm puts you alongside other fragments of a splintered Traveller soul against a threat in the sky, and the best-performing team earns the extra trophy base part.

## Patch 7.15 fixes for iPhone and iPad

The expeditions arrive with patch 7.15, which carries several mobile fixes:

- Mission notification boxes are easier to dismiss on touchscreen.
- Better resizing support on iPad.
- Support for iPhone Duo.
- A rare crash when loading save games on iOS is fixed.
- Photo Mode captures with HDR enabled on iOS now match what was shown in game.
