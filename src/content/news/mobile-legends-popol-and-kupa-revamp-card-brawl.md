The US version of Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, published by Skystone Games, received an update on November 11, 2026. The release notes list two changes: a revamped hero and a mode with a start date.

- **Revamped Hero**: Popol and Kupa return as "Icefield Companions". The notes describe Popol as a Marksman who fights alongside Kupa and uses traps to control the battlefield.
- **Card Brawl**: the mode became available on November 20, server time.

Popol and Kupa have always been an unusual pick, because you control a Marksman and his companion together. The revamp keeps that idea: the description still centres on fighting with Kupa and placing traps, so players who already know the hero should recognise the role.

What the notes don't include are the numbers. There are no skill values, no list of changed abilities and no word on whether the revamp comes with a new look. If you main Popol and Kupa, read the hero's skill descriptions in the game before taking him into ranked, since a revamp can change his damage and timings.

Card Brawl has been open since November 20. The notes give only the date, so the mode's rules and how long it stays are on its page in the game.

This story covers the US app, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.US. Other regions use a different app and may be on a different schedule.
