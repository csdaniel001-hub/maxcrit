Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Patch 2.2.23 went live on the Original Server on November 11, 2026. The store release notes name only two things: the revamped Popol and Kupa, "Icefield Companions", and Card Brawl. The full patch notes are published inside the game, and they are much longer.

## How the Popol and Kupa revamp works

Moonton's designer note says the old version lacked mobility and combo options, and that Popol spent too long fighting alone after Kupa went down. The new kit answers each point:

- **Passive, We Are Friends**: Kupa's HP scales with Popol's level, and Kupa inherits Popol's other attributes and equipment effects. Kupa recovers HP out of combat and deals extra True Damage to creeps. If Kupa falls, Popol can pray to bring Kupa back.
- **Skill 1, Bite 'em, Kupa!**: Popol throws a spear and Kupa lunges at the target, biting and greatly slowing them.
- **Skill 2, Kupa, Help!**: Popol calls Kupa back, gains a shield and can ride Kupa to move quickly. Kupa recovers HP while carrying him.
- **Skill 3, Popol's Surprise**: a trap that briefly immobilizes the enemy who steps on it, then explodes into a frost zone that slows.
- **Ultimate, We Are Angry!**: both enter the Enraged state for 9 seconds. Kupa returns to full HP and takes Alpha Wolf Form, which adds Max HP and strengthens the other skills. In that form Skill 1 knocks nearby enemies airborne.

The role is the same as before: a Marksman who fights beside a companion and controls space with traps. What changes is how forgiving it is. Losing Kupa used to switch off half the hero for a long stretch, and now there is a way to get Kupa back.

## Who got stronger and who got weaker

The notes list six buffs, three nerfs and several adjustments.

- **Xavier**: his Mystic Field damage doubles, from 220-345 (+65% Total Magic Power) to 440-690 (+130%), and his passive movement speed bonus rises from 50% to 60%. In exchange Mystic Barrier loses its direct damage.
- **Lunox**: her passive now grants 10% Spell Vamp when the balance shifts to the Power of Order and 10% Magic Penetration when it shifts to the Power of Chaos. Skill 2 extra damage starts at 3.5% instead of 2.5%, and the Ultimate changes from 250-450 (+100% Total Magic Power) to 150-300 (+135%), which favours built-up magic power.
- **Freya**: her enhanced Basic Attacks during the Ultimate deal 100-200 (+70% Total Physical Attack), up from 30-60 (+100%), and she gains 30 Movement Speed. The Ultimate's cooldown goes up, from 44-28 seconds to 60-44.
- **Aldous**: Skill 2 cooldown is 8 seconds at every level, down from 12-9.5, and its damage reduction scales from 30% to 55%.
- **Melissa**: Muddles's Damage Transfer and attack effect inheritance scale from 50% to 60%, where both were a flat 52%.
- **Kaja**: targets with Control Immunity can no longer escape his Ultimate's area, though the Ultimate's delay is slightly longer. Hitting enemies while Soaring now extends that state.
- **Gloo**: Skill 1 immobilizes for 0.6 seconds, down from 0.8, and Skill 3's damage transfer falls from 80% to 60%.
- **Masha**: passive HP Regen drops from 4-10% to 3-6% of Max HP, and Skill 1's damage bonus from 70% to 55%.
- **Atlas**: Frigid Breath lasts 4 seconds, down from 5, and the freeze cooldown rises from 2.5 to 4 seconds.
- **Adjusted**: Lukas gains base HP (2550 to 2620) and Physical Defense (21 to 23), Karrie's Ultimate gets stronger (50 base damage per Lightwheel, up from 40, and a 30% Movement Speed boost, up from 20%) while Skill 1's Physical Attack bonus is cut, and Uranus's Basic Attack splash now deals Magic Damage.

## Gold lane, turrets and items

The designer note gives the reason for the wider changes: fighters had been taking the Gold Lane and roaming tanks the EXP Lane.

- **Gold Lane Siege Minion**: 60% extra gold in the early game, up from 40%.
- **Turrets**: the Golden Turret's base HP increase doubles from 1,000 to 2,000, while team gold falls from 100 to 90 for an Outer Turret and from 140 to 120 for an Inner Turret.
- **Sprint**: cooldown drops from 100 to 75 seconds.
- **Items**: Antique Cuirass now cuts the attacker's Physical Damage by 8%, up from 6%. Chastise Pauldron is weaker: it reduces the attacker's Attack Speed to 80%, where it was 75%.
- **Concussive Blast**: damage scales with 8% of Total HP, up from 7%, but it deals 50% less damage to minions. Moonton says this is meant to bring fighters back to the EXP Lane.
- **Healing Turtle**: killing an enemy Turtle now gives Gold and EXP roughly equal to a minion wave, shared among allies in range.

## Card Brawl

Card Brawl opened on November 20 and runs until December 15, server time. It is played in Necrokeep: at specific hero levels you choose from randomly offered cards, up to five times, and combine them to power up.

The detailed figures come from the in-game patch notes as copied by Liquipedia and MLBBHub, which agree with each other. Moonton does not post the full notes on a public page.

This is the global update. The US has its own app, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.US from Skystone Games, which received the same version, 2.2.23, with the same release notes on the same day.
