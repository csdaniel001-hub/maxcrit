Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Patch 2.2.23 went live on the Original Server on November 11, 2026. The store release notes name only two things: the revamped Popol and Kupa, "Icefield Companions", and Card Brawl. The full patch notes are published inside the game, and they are much longer.

## How the Popol and Kupa revamp works

Moonton's designer note says the old version lacked mobility and combo options, and that Popol spent too long fighting alone after Kupa went down. The new kit answers each point:

- **Passive, We Are Friends**: Kupa's HP scales with Popol's level, and Kupa inherits Popol's other attributes and equipment effects. Kupa recovers HP out of combat and deals extra True Damage to creeps. If Kupa falls, Popol can pray to bring Kupa back.
- **Riding**: Popol can call Kupa to his side, gain a shield and ride Kupa to move quickly, either to chase or to retreat.
- **Traps**: Popol sets a trap that briefly immobilizes the enemy who steps on it.
- **Ultimate**: both enter the Enraged state for 9 seconds. Kupa returns to full HP and takes Alpha Wolf Form, which raises Max HP and strengthens the other skills.

The role is the same as before: a Marksman who fights beside a companion and controls space with traps. What changes is how forgiving it is. Losing Kupa used to switch off half the hero for a long stretch, and now there is a way to get Kupa back.

## Who got stronger and who got weaker

The notes list six buffs, three nerfs and several adjustments.

- **Buffed**: Xavier, Lunox, Freya, Aldous, Kaja and Melissa. Aldous's Skill 2 cooldown is now 8 seconds at every level, down from 12 to 9.5.
- **Nerfed**: Gloo, Masha and Atlas. Gloo's Skill 1 immobilizes for 0.6 seconds, down from 0.8, and Atlas's freeze cooldown rises from 2.5 to 4 seconds.
- **Adjusted**: Lukas, Karrie, Kagura and Uranus. Uranus's Basic Attack splash now deals Magic Damage.

The designer note gives the reason for the wider changes: fighters had been taking the Gold Lane and roaming tanks the EXP Lane. Two battlefield changes push the other way. The Gold Lane Siege Minion gives 60% extra gold early on, up from 40%, and the Sprint battle spell's cooldown drops from 100 to 75 seconds. Chastise Pauldron and Antique Cuirass were also retuned.

## Card Brawl and what to check yourself

Card Brawl opened on November 20 and runs until December 15, server time.

The balance figures in this story come from published copies of the in-game notes, because Moonton does not post them on a public page. The revamp text and the date match across both copies and the store notes. If you plan around an exact number, confirm it in the game's own patch notes first.

The store notes we cite are for the US app, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.US, published by Skystone Games. Other regions use a different app.
