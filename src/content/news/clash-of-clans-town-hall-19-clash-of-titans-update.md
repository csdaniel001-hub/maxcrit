You don't need to be anywhere near Town Hall 18 to get something out of Clash of Clans' Clash of Titans update. Supercell's update went live on November 16 with Town Hall 19 at the top, but Squads unlock from Town Hall 8, and the Star Bonus rework changes Ore income for almost everyone.

## Squads deploy with one tap and save housing space

Squads are fixed groups of troops that deploy together with a single tap and give you more troops per housing space than deploying them one by one. They unlock from Town Hall 8 through a new building, the Squad Barracks, which has 12 levels and gains one maximum level per Town Hall from 8 to 19. Each level unlocks a new Squad.

Squad members use the levels of troops you have already upgraded. Each Squad deploys in its own formation with built-in delays: in the Golem Witch Squad, for example, the Golems go down first and the Witches follow less than a second later, so defenses target the Golems first. Squad upgrades are exclusive to Town Hall 19, and every Squad gains an ability at Squad Level 2. Squads can't be donated.

There are twelve Squads at launch, from the Barch Squad at Town Hall 8 to the Baby Dragon Squad at Town Hall 19. We've put all of them, with troops, housing space and abilities, on one page: [every Clash of Clans Squad]({{ '/hub/guides/clash-of-clans-squads-every-squad.html' | rel }}).

## What's new on the Town Hall 19 base

- **Town Hall troops**: for the first time, your Town Hall can be occupied by troops that come out to defend it. You start with the Barbarian; maxing the Wizard or Archer (level 15) unlocks those options. The choice is saved per layout.
- **Spiky Tower**: a Dark Elixir defense that never attacks. Every hit it takes sends a spike back for 6% (level 1) or 9% (level 2) of the attacker's max hitpoints. Spells and Hero Equipment abilities don't trigger it, and Freeze has no effect on it. You can build two.
- **Firecracker Tower**: merge a level 21 Archer Tower with a level 7 Scattershot. Its rockets hit ground and air for 483 / 501 damage every 1.8 seconds, with sparks for up to 365 / 385 behind the target. Also two per base.
- **Guardian Shrine**: Guardians move out of the Town Hall into their own free building, and your chosen Guardian becomes the default for every layout. Smasher can now reach level 10.
- **New building counts**: a 5th Air Defense, a 10th Spring Trap and 25 more Walls (350 total). The Clan Castle gains 5 troop capacity, for 60 in total.

## Heroic Spell

The new Heroic Spell creates a 5-tile area where your troops take 34% to 40% less damage, move faster and walk straight through Walls. It unlocks with a new Spell Factory level at Town Hall 17, with two levels there, a third at Town Hall 18 and a fourth at Town Hall 19. Its speed boost doesn't stack with Rage or Haste, and its damage reduction doesn't stack with effects like the Grand Warden's Eternal Tome; the strongest one applies.

## Star Bonus, Magic Snacks and the Prestige Shop

The Star Bonus is no longer tied to the Ranked ladder. Rewards now scale with Town Hall level, so the same Town Hall gets the same rewards, Ore included. You earn a Snack Pack at 2 stars and another at 5 stars, and reaching 5 stars gives the full Loot and Ore rewards. The League Bonus is unchanged.

New Magic Snacks fill those packs: Battle Bakes (2x, 3x and 4x loot from your next battle), the Loot Cart Lolly, the Clan Castle Slice and the Star Bonus Snack. Ranked trophies now cash out into Ranked Medals at the end of each tournament, spent in a new Prestige Shop that combines the Ranked, Clan War League and Fancy shops.

## Economy changes

Supercharges were removed from every building that got a new level, such as Air Defense, X-Bow and the resource collectors. Unfinished Supercharge upgrades were refunded in full, completed ones were not. Upgrade costs and times dropped for most Town Hall 16 to 18 content, and Ore is cheaper in Gems: Glowy Ore went from 5 to 3 Gems and Starry Ore from 35 to 20.

Town Hall 16 to 18 is now cheaper to push through than before the update, so anyone sitting there with free builders has fewer reasons to wait.
