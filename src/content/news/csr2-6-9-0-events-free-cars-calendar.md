CSR2's 6.9.0 calendar opens on December 22 with a 100-race Holiday Pursuit and a free LB Lamborghini Aventador Coupé, then rolls through seven events to February 10, 2027. Three of them hand out a free car, and every one has a prize car at the end.

| Event | Dates | Free car | Prize car |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holiday Pursuit | Dec 22–31 | LB Lamborghini Aventador Coupé | Nilu27 NILU SUMAC |
| Skyborne Sprint | Dec 24–31 | Dodge Challenger SRT Hellcat | Czinger 21C HDF |
| Forged Fury | Dec 30–Jan 6 | None | Weaver Customs Chevrolet Pick-Up 3100 'The One' |
| SIX: Brawn | Jan 16–24 | None | Dodge Charger Super Bee |
| Crew's Code: Challenger's Run V | Jan 23–Feb 5 | Han's Toyota GR Supra | Dom Toretto's Dodge Charger R/T 'Tantrum' |
| Crew's Code: Chapter V: Unstoppable + Victory Lap V | Jan 25–Feb 4 | None | Roman's Pontiac Fiero |
| Flachbau Legacy | Feb 3–10 | None | Porsche 911 Flachbau RS (991.2) |

December is busy. Holiday Pursuit and Skyborne Sprint overlap for a week, so decide early which prize car gets your upgrade parts.

The Fast & Furious events in January are linked. Challenger's Run V pays out Dom Toretto's Dodge Charger R/T 'Tantrum', and that car is one of the lock-ins for Chapter V, the event with Roman's Fiero at the end. Win the Tantrum first and you walk into Chapter V with a qualifying car already in the garage. The other Chapter V lock-ins are Deckard Shaw's Jaguar F-TYPE, Owen Shaw's Flip Car, Brian's C-WEST Nissan Skyline GT-R (BNR34), Magdalene Shaw's Noble M600, Dom Toretto's 1970 Dodge Charger R/T and Reyes' Chevrolet Corvette Grand Sport.

Porsche collectors have February. Flachbau Legacy locks in the 992-generation 911 S/T and the 930-generation 911 Turbo 3.3 Cabriolet "Flachbau", so if either is sitting in your garage, the 911 Flachbau RS is within reach.
