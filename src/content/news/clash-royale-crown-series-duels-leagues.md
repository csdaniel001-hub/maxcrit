Thirteen Leagues, up to four decks and no card used twice. That is the shape of the Crown Series, a new Clash Royale competition announced on November 23 that runs every season and opens from Arena 17, at 6,000 Trophies. You play head-to-head Duels against players of a similar skill level and collect rewards at the end of each run.

## Duel Decks and the Deck Wizard

Every Crown Series match uses the Duel format: a best-of-3 played with decks you build in advance. Your Duel Deck sits in the top right corner of the deck screen and holds four separate 8-card decks. Each card can only be used once across all of them.

Because a Duel is best-of-3, you can get away with building three decks, which is 24 unique cards. Building all four, 32 cards, gives you more room to counter what your opponent brings.

Building three or four decks that don't share cards is harder than it sounds, and Supercell has reworked the Deck Wizard to help. It can generate a deck from scratch based on your Collection, or build one around a win condition you choose, and it takes your other decks into account so its suggestions don't overlap with cards you've already used.

## Leagues and card-level caps

When you join, the game places you in one of 13 Leagues. It estimates your skill from your Trophy Road progress, your best Ranked and Ultimate Champion finishes, and any Crown Series Duel results you already have.

- **Leagues 1 to 8**: Dagger, Pancake, Mustache, Magic, Hammer, Armor, Phantom and Queen's League.
- **Leagues 9 to 13**: together they form the King's League. On Duel Weekends you can be matched against anyone inside the King's League, though not during Grand Duel Weekends.

You can move up a League at the start of each tournament. When a new season starts, your placement is recalculated, so you can begin higher or lower than where you finished.

Matches use your own card levels, but each League caps them, from Level 11 in the lowest Leagues to Level 16 in the highest. So players in lower Leagues aren't facing maxed-out decks.

## How a season is laid out

Each season has 3 or 4 Duel Weekends, depending on its length: a few regular Duels, then a Grand Duel, then a break weekend. In a 4-week season that's Duel, Duel, Grand Duel, break. A 5-week season adds a third Duel. Every tournament runs for 72 hours, from Friday 09:00 UTC to Monday 09:00 UTC.

- **Duel Weekends**: win 8 matches before you hit 3 losses. Rewards are bundled at the end of your run, and more wins pay more. An 8-win run in the King's League pays 8 Champion Wild Cards, 8 Legendary Wild Cards, 100 Epic Wild Cards, 120,000 Gold and a Full Banner.
- **Grand Duel, Leagues 1 to 8**: the target rises to 12 wins before 4 losses.
- **Grand Duel, King's League**: up to 20 battles and a leaderboard. Wins are capped, so ties are broken by fewer losses, then more individual battles won across your Duels, then stronger opponents beaten, then who reached their final score first.

Grand Duel rewards arrive in the days after the event ends.

The 8-win rewards climb steeply. The lowest League pays 4,800 Gold for a perfect run, and the King's League pays 120,000. Over a season, climbing a League is worth more than any single good weekend, so treat the early Duels as a ladder.
