Digital Extremes will broadcast Warframe Devstream 199 on November 27 at 19:00 UTC, with a built Omni Forma available as a Twitch Drop. The hour-long showcase focuses on the December Tau update, including its game modes, highway system and Steel Path content.

The stream runs from 14:00 to 15:00 Eastern time, which is 19:00 to 20:00 UTC or 20:00 to 21:00 CET. Watch on the [official Warframe Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/warframe). Digital Extremes says you need to watch from start to finish to earn the drop, so tune in before 19:00 UTC.

Tau is expected to open a separate Star Chart, with Fornax and Perita as its two playable zones. Fornax's hubs are connected by a highway, while Tau's version of Steel Path will use different enemies in place of Acolytes. The next Prime trailer is also on the broadcast agenda.

Players who want to start Tau's cinematic quest when the update arrives can prepare now by finishing the Prologue to Tau Quest. Digital Extremes has identified that quest as the prerequisite. The November 27 broadcast is a preview, with the update itself planned for December.
