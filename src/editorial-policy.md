## Official sources only

Every news story starts from the developer's or publisher's own announcement: their website, patch notes, press release or official social account. We do not rewrite other news sites. Each story lists its sources at the bottom, with the primary source marked.

## Nothing we cannot verify

If a date, number or name is not in an official source, it is not in the article. We do not fill gaps with guesses. Leaks and rumours are only covered when they are already a major story, and then they are labelled "Reported, not confirmed" in the headline and on the page.

## Names stay as the developer writes them

Characters, modes, items, events and systems keep their official names and spelling, exactly as they appear in the source.

## Real images, credited

Every article uses official artwork: store screenshots, key art or press kit images, credited to the rights holder with a link to where it came from. No article is published without one, and we never use generated images in place of real ones.

## Hub pages show when they were checked

Patch trackers, tier lists, code lists and guides carry a "last checked" date. When a game patches, the page is checked again before that date moves.

## No hands-on claims we did not earn

We only write reviews or impressions of games we have actually played, and say so.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and add a note at the end of the article saying what changed and when. Send corrections to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with a link to the article.

## Independence

MaxCrit is not affiliated with any developer or publisher, and we do not accept payment for news coverage. If that ever changes for a piece of content, it will be clearly labelled as sponsored.
