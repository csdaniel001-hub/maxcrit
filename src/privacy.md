This policy explains what personal data MaxCrit processes, why, and what rights you have. MaxCrit is read worldwide but run from Hungary, so it follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), one of the strictest privacy laws in the world. We give the same protections and rights to every visitor, wherever you live.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is {{ site.legal.operator }}{% if site.legal.commercial %}, {{ site.legal.address }}{% endif %}, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Full details are on the [Contact](contact.html) page.

## The short version

MaxCrit has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter and no advertising. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. Google Analytics only runs if you accept it in the cookie settings. Fonts and images are served from our own domain, and search runs entirely in your browser; what you type is never sent to us.

## What we process

**Server logs.** Like every website, our hosting provider ({{ site.legal.host.name }}) records technical data when a page is requested: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the browser's user agent and the referring page. This keeps the site running and secure, for example to stop attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The logs are kept for up to {{ site.legal.logDays }} days and then deleted, unless they are needed to investigate a specific attack.

**Emails you send us.** If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and your message. We use them only to answer you and handle your request, such as a correction or a rights holder's request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages (Art. 6(1)(f)), or a legal obligation where the request is about your data rights (Art. 6(1)(c)). We delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed, at the latest two years after the conversation ends.

**Visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics).** A small script from Cloudflare counts page views, the pages visited, the referring site, the browser and device type, the country and page loading speed. It stores nothing on your device, uses no cookies and does not track you across visits or sites. Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read and keeping the site fast (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time; blocking the script in your browser also works.

**Google Analytics 4, only with your consent.** If you accept analytics in the cookie settings, Google Analytics sets cookies and records how you use the site: pages viewed, time on page, how you arrived, device, browser and approximate location. Google does not log or store the IP addresses of visitors from the EU; it uses them only to work out an approximate location and then discards them. Google Ireland Limited processes the data for us. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the cookie settings; withdrawing deletes the Analytics cookies. The data is kept for {{ site.analytics.gaMonths }} months. Google signals and advertising features are switched off.

**Your cookie choice.** If the site ever asks for cookie consent, your choice is saved in your browser (see the [Cookie policy](cookies.html)). We do not receive it.

We do not sell or share personal data for advertising, build profiles or make automated decisions about you.

## Who else sees it

Our processors, who handle data only on our instructions under data processing agreements: our hosting provider (server logs), Cloudflare, Inc. (visit statistics) and, if you consent, Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics). Cloudflare and Google may process data in the United States. Both are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and data transfers also rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

## Links to other sites

Articles link to app stores, publishers' sites and official social accounts. Those sites have their own privacy policies; once you follow a link, their rules apply.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, and to correct, delete or restrict it. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest, ask for a copy in a portable format, and withdraw any consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}); we answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work, or where the problem happened. In the EU and EEA, the European Data Protection Board keeps [a list of all national authorities](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en); in the UK it is the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk). Because MaxCrit is operated from Hungary, our lead authority is the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH), [naih.hu](https://naih.hu). You can also take legal action in court.

## Visitors outside Europe

The rights above apply to you too, wherever you live. For visitors in the United States: we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of Google Analytics.

## Children

MaxCrit is a general-audience site. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, for example by email, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete it.

## Changes

If what we process changes, for example if we add advertising, this page is updated first and the date below changes. Anything that needs your consent will ask before it runs.
