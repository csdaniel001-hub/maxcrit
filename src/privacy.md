This policy explains what personal data MaxCrit processes, why, and what rights you have. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Hungarian law (Infotv.).

## Who is responsible

The data controller is {{ site.legal.operator }}, {{ site.legal.address }}, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Full details are on the [Contact and legal notice](contact.html) page.

## The short version

MaxCrit has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter, no analytics and no advertising. Fonts and images are served from our own domain, so reading the site does not send your data to Google, social networks or ad companies. Search runs entirely in your browser; what you type is never sent to us.

## What we process

**Server logs.** Like every website, our hosting provider ({{ site.legal.host.name }}) records technical data when a page is requested: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the browser's user agent and the referring page. This keeps the site running and secure, for example to stop attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The logs are kept for up to {{ site.legal.logDays }} days and then deleted, unless they are needed to investigate a specific attack.

**Emails you send us.** If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and your message. We use them only to answer you and handle your request, such as a correction or a rights holder's request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages (Art. 6(1)(f)), or a legal obligation where the request is about your data rights (Art. 6(1)(c)). We delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed, at the latest two years after the conversation ends.

**Your cookie choice.** If the site ever asks for cookie consent, your choice is saved in your browser (see the [Cookie policy](cookies.html)). We do not receive it.

We do not sell personal data, build profiles or make automated decisions about you.

## Who else sees it

Only our hosting provider, which processes the server logs on our behalf under a data processing agreement. If it processes data outside the European Economic Area, that happens under a European Commission adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

## Links to other sites

Articles link to app stores, publishers' sites and official social accounts. Those sites have their own privacy policies; once you follow a link, their rules apply.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, and to correct, delete or restrict it. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest, ask for a copy in a portable format, and withdraw any consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}); we answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11, postal address 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9, [ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu](mailto:ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu), [naih.hu](https://naih.hu). You can also complain to the authority where you live, or go to court.

## Children

MaxCrit is a general-audience site and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. If you are under 16 and have emailed us, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete the message.

## Changes

If what we process changes, for example if we add analytics or ads, this page is updated first and the date below changes. Anything that needs your consent will ask before it runs.
