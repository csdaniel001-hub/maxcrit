## Findings

### Positioning and teams. Severity: MAJOR (completeness)
Original: "For an account that already owns Zibai, she is a more targeted investment", "less compelling as a general-purpose Geo purchase", and the "Zibai, Linnea, Aino, Illuga | Hydro alternative to investigate" row.
Issue: KQM's team list puts Linnea in non-Zibai teams too: Linnea – Navia – Columbina – Furina; Itto – Gorou – Linnea – Columbina; Linnea – Nefer – Lauma – Columbina; plus a limited-roster team Linnea – Noelle – Aino – Geo Traveler/Gorou. GameRant's reveal coverage also notes her synergy with older Geo characters such as Navia. Presenting her as Zibai-only misleads Navia or Itto owners, the readers most likely to be deciding right now. The "to investigate" row is an untested team presented in a recommendation table (pilot safeguard 2).
Evidence: KQM Linnea quick guide (Luna VI), example teams.
Proposed resolution: add the Navia/Columbina/Furina and Itto lineups, swap the Aino row for KQM's limited-roster team or drop it, and rewrite the investment section so Zibai is one use case among several.
Impact: TL;DR and "Should you invest" section.

### Artifact set order. Severity: MINOR
Specialists disagree. KQM ranks 4pc Husk of Opulent Dreams first and Aubade second. Icy Veins, Mobalytics and Game8 put Aubade first with Husk close behind (Icy Veins notes Husk's DEF also raises her healing). The draft's "Aubade default, Husk viable" is defensible, but say the two are close and that Husk is KQM's pick, so the article doesn't present one site's ranking as settled.

### DEF threshold with Aubade. Severity: SUGGESTION
Reaching 2,000 DEF without Husk's +30% needs both the DEF% Sands and the DEF% Goblet. Base 907 at Lv. 90 x (1 + 0.583 + 0.583) is about 1,965 before weapon and substats (timesaver.gg's breakdown; reproducible). A sentence saying Aubade users need DEF on both pieces makes the threshold advice actionable.

### Talent priority. Severity: MINOR
KQM: Skill > Character Level > Burst. The draft's "Skill for personal damage, Burst for healing" reads as equal priority. State the order.
