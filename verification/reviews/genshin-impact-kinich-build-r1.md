## Findings

### Earth Shaker condition. Severity: MINOR
The draft's conclusion is right, but its explanation ("a teammate outside Dendro and Pyro can help trigger its passive") is vaguer than the mechanism. KQM says Earth Shaker can reach 100% uptime in Burgeon teams but struggles in Burning teams, because Burning only counts as a Pyro reaction when it is first applied. State that, and point Earth Shaker users at the Burgeon lineup.

### Serpent Spine. Severity: SUGGESTION
KQM's real condition is a strong shielder (stacks drop when he's hit). "Maintain stacks" should say "with a shielder".

### Wish name (all Natlan builds). Severity: MINOR, recurring
"Natlan Chronicled Wish" is not an official name. The 7.2 notice calls it "Names Forged Beneath Blazing Skies" (Chronicled Wish). This was also flagged in the original pack drafts. Claude will fix it directly as an unambiguous factual correction: "the Names Forged Beneath Blazing Skies Chronicled Wish".
