# Failed-claims report: honkai-star-rail-robin-build (r2)

Reviewer: fresh Tier 3 agent (Claude), 2026-11-25. Scope: r1 items, claims new in R3 (diff against /home/claude/batch07r2/articles/honkai-star-rail-robin-build.md), Mira's open items F12/F13, depth gate. No gameplay testing. hsr.gachabase.net not opened.

**Verdict: REVISION REQUIRED**

**Depth gate: FAIL** - the DoT and hypercarry archetypes from r1 F3 are now present, but (a) the Yunli rotation gives no concrete action order, and (b) the team section still reflects the 2.x-era roster: the current Icy Veins Robin teams page (created 14 May 2026) lists seven teams built around Feixiao, Phainon, Ashveil, Aglaea, Anaxa and The Herta, and the draft covers only the Feixiao/Aventurine one. For a page set to `game_version: 4.7`, a reader running a Remembrance (Aglaea) or Erudition (Anaxa, The Herta) team gets no guidance. Also, two current specialist pages list Wavestrider Captain as a 4-piece relic option, and the draft leaves it out. Two caveats on (b): it rests on one page, and that page says "This is not a comprehensive list of teams." The rotations in R3 are also editorial interpretations with no visible label (R11). The gate clears with R2, R9 (or Daniel accepting R9 as a `review_by` item) and R11.
